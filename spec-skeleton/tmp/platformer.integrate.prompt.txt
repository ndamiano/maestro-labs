# 2D, side-scrolling camera locked to a horizontal track with vertical follow and a deadzone; level bounds clamp the viewport.

# 1. THE GAME IN ONE PARAGRAPH

You are Kael, a salvage mechanic fighting through two hostile industrial zones to reclaim the city's power grid. Minute to minute you run, jump, crouch under low pipes, and shoot waves of automated enemies and hazards across scrolling stages. You swap between four salvaged guns on the fly, each with a distinct rhythm—tap-fire, spread, charge-release, lobbed explosion—so combat is a constant decision about spacing, timing, and which tool the current fight demands. Stages are short (45–90 seconds of skilled play) and end with a flagpole-style "extraction point" you must reach; the final stage of each world is a multi-phase boss. You get 3 lives per stage attempt and unlimited retries; dying resets you to the stage start with a full heal but no score bonus. The game has a definitive end: clearing World 2 Stage 5 (the final boss) triggers a victory screen and rolls credits. Between stages you pick your next stage from a world map; you can replay cleared stages for higher score ranks (Bronze/Silver/Gold thresholds per stage). What keeps players coming back after the credits is chasing Gold rank on every stage and finding the 4 hidden "upgrade chips" per world that permanently boost a gun's stat.

# 3. SYSTEMS

SYSTEMS: Locomotion, Shooting, DamageAndDeath, EnemyAI, BossAI, StageGenerator, ProgressionAndUnlocks, Scoring, TouchControls

## Locomotion

RULES:
- **Move left/right:** While A/← held, set `Player.vx` toward −280 px/s; while D/→ held, toward +280 px/s. Acceleration 2400 px/s², deceleration (friction) 1800 px/s² when no key held. `Player.facing` matches last horizontal input.
- **Jump:** When W/↑/Space pressed AND `Player.grounded == true`, set `Player.vy = −520 px/s`. Variable-height: releasing the jump key within 0.18 s of press cuts `vy` to −260 px/s (short hop).
- **Crouch:** While S/↓ held AND `Player.grounded == true`, set `Player.crouching = true`; hitbox height shrinks from 48 px to 28 px; max speed halves to 140 px/s; jump is disabled while crouching. Releasing S restores height and speed.
- **Gravity:** `Player.vy` increases by 1600 px/s² each frame while airborne. Terminal velocity 700 px/s.
- **Conveyor tiles:** Standing on a conveyor adds `Tile.conveyorSpeed` (±120 px/s) to `Player.vx` each frame.
- **Platform (one-way):** Player passes through from below and sides; lands only when falling onto the top edge.
- **Breakable tiles:** After 3 projectile hits (`hp` decremented to 0), tile is removed from collision.
- **Slope45:** Moving right on a 45° up-slope converts horizontal speed to a 45° diagonal at the same magnitude; same for down-slopes. Jump off a slope retains the diagonal velocity direction.

## Shooting

RULES:
- **Fire:** While fire key (J / left-click / touch-fire button) held AND `Player.activeGun` magazine > 0 AND not reloading, spawn a Projectile at the muzzle (facing direction, 8 px forward from hitbox edge) with the gun's `projectileSpeed`, `damage`, `spreadDeg` applied as random angular jitter. Decrement `magSize`. Repeat at `1/fireRate` intervals (hold-to-fire).
- **Coil Rifle charge:** If fire key held ≥ 0.25 s, stop auto-firing and begin charging `Player.chargeMeter` from 0→1 over `chargeMax` (1.2 s). Release: fire one projectile with `damage = 15 + 25 × chargeMeter`, `pierceCount = 3`. Tap-fire (release < 0.25 s) fires a normal 15-damage shot.
- **Flare Launcher arc:** Projectile launches at 45° above facing direction at 320 px/s; gravity 800 px/s² applies; on first contact with any solid surface or enemy, detonate: deal 55 damage in `aoeRadius` 90 px circle, apply `knockback` 300 px/s radially.
- **Reload:** Automatic when magazine hits 0, or manual on R key. During `reloadTime` the gun cannot fire. Visual: magazine count resets to `magSize`.
- **Gun swap:** Keys 1/2/3/4 or scroll-wheel cycle `Player.activeGun` among unlocked guns. Swap takes 0.3 s (no fire during swap).
- **Crouch-fire:** While `Player.crouching == true`, muzzle position drops to the crouched hitbox top (28 px above feet) and `spreadDeg` is halved (more accurate low shot). Cannot jump while firing crouched.

## DamageAndDeath

RULES:
- **Player takes damage:** On contact with an enemy hitbox or enemy Projectile, if `Player.hitTimer == 0`: subtract enemy/projectile `damage` from `Player.hp`; set `Player.hitTimer = 1.0`; apply 180 px/s knockback away from source. While `hitTimer > 0` the player is invulnerable.
- **Player dies:** If `Player.hp ≤ 0`: decrement `Player.lives`. If `lives > 0`, respawn at stage start (or nearest checkpoint flag) with `hp = 100`, `hitTimer = 1.5` (longer i-frames), score carries. If `lives == 0`, return to Stage Select.
- **Enemy dies:** Enemy `hp ≤ 0` → remove enemy, add its score value to `Player.score` (Crawler 100, Turret 200, Drone 150, ShieldTrooper 350). Drop chance: 20 % for a small ammo pickup (refills current gun mag by 3), 8 % for a health orb (+15 hp).
- **Hazard damage:** Touching a spike/steam/piston/electricFloor deals its `damage` (spike 20, steam 12, piston 30, electric 18) once per `hitTimer` window.
- **Fall death:** If `Player.y > Stage.height + 200 px`, instant death (same as hp ≤ 0 but no knockback).

## EnemyAI

RULES (per-frame, each enemy independently):
- **Perception:** Enemy checks Euclidean distance to `Player` center. If ≤ `aggroRange`, state = CHASE; else state = PATROL.
- **Crawler PATROL:** Walk `speed` px/s toward nearest edge of `patrolRange`; reverse at boundary. CHASE: run toward player at `speed × 1.4`. Contact deals `damage`.
- **Turret:** Stationary. CHASE: aim at player (no LOS check for simplicity), fire a Projectile (damage 10, speed 300) every 1.8 s. Cannot be knocked back (`knockbackResist 1.0`).
- **Drone:** Hovers at `Player.y − 60 px` (stays above). PATROL: sinusoidal bob (±30 px, period 2 s). CHASE: drift toward player at `speed`; contact deals `damage`. Can be hit only by projectiles fired above 20° or by jump-contact.
- **ShieldTrooper:** PATROL: walk slowly. CHASE: advance toward player at `speed`; front-facing arc (±60°) blocks all projectiles (damage redirected to shield, shield has 60 hp separate pool). Shield breaks → normal hit. Attacks with a 1-s wind-up lunge (30 px/s for 0.5 s) dealing `damage` on contact.
- **Knockback on enemy:** Apply `Gun.knockback × (1 − Enemy.knockbackResist)` as instantaneous vx impulse away from projectile direction.

## BossAI

RULES:
- A boss is a BossPhase sequence. Each phase has its own `hp` pool; when phase hp reaches `transitionAt` fraction, boss flashes (1.5 s invulnerable) and advances to next phase.
- **World 1 Boss "Rust Colossus":** 3 phases (HP 200/200/200).
  - Phase 1 (sweep, interval 2.5 s): walks back and forth across a 400 px arena, swinging arm (hitbox 60 px wide, damage 25).
  - Phase 2 (volley, interval 3.0 s): stops, fires 5 projectiles in a fan (damage 12 each, speed 280).
  - Phase 3 (charge + summon, interval 2.0 s): telegraphs 1 s then dashes at 500 px/s across arena (damage 35); every 6 s summons 2 Crawlers.
- **World 2 Boss "Core Warden":** 4 phases (HP 180/180/180/180).
  - Phase 1: laser sweep (horizontal beam, 2 s active, damage 20/s contact).
  - Phase 2: volley of 8 homing drones (damage 15, speed 180, lifetime 4 s).
  - Phase 3: floor electrification (entire arena floor is electricFloor for 3 s, period 5 s); player must jump onto floating platforms.
  - Phase 4: all three attacks in rotation, interval 1.5 s.
- Boss is immune to knockback. Must be damaged from any angle.

## StageGenerator

SEEDED GENERATOR for each Stage record:
- **Inputs:** `Stage.seed`, `Stage.width` (default 4800 px for normal, 1200 px for boss), `Stage.height` (default 720 px), `worldTheme` (W1 = industrial pipes/catwalks; W2 = cave/underground), `difficultyTier` (1–5, = stage number within world).
- **Placement order:**
  1. **Ground layer:** Lay solid tiles along the bottom (y = height−48). Insert gaps of width 3–6 tiles at intervals of 8–14 tiles (more gaps at higher tier). Place a breakable tile in 15 % of gaps.
  2. **Platform tier:** At every 6–10 tile interval, place a platform (one-way) at y = height−144 or height−240. Width 3–7 tiles.
  3. **Hazard scatter:** Place spikes on 10–20 % of ground tiles (never within 2 tiles of a gap edge). Place 1 steamVent per 12 tiles of ground (period 3 s, activeTime 1.5 s). At tier ≥ 3 add crushingPiston (period 4 s, activeTime 1.0 s).
  4. **Enemy spawn points:** One enemy every 10–16 tiles. Tier 1: Crawlers only. Tier 2: add Turrets. Tier 3: add Drones. Tier 4: add ShieldTroopers. Tier 5: mix all.
  5. **Chest / pickup:** One gun-unlock chest per designated stage (hard-coded stage ids). One health orb every 20 tiles.
  6. **Extraction point:** Place at x = width − 120 px, a tall flagpole tile. Reaching it ends the stage.
  7. **Hidden side-room (UpgradeChip):** In stages 1, 3 of each world, place a breakable wall at a random ground position; behind it a 200×120 px room containing the UpgradeChip.
- **VERIFIER:** Run after generation:
  - Every gap is crossable: max gap width ≤ 6 tiles AND a platform exists within 12 px vertical and 120 px horizontal of any gap edge.
  - No enemy spawns inside a solid tile.
  - No hazard overlaps the extraction point (≥ 200 px clearance).
  - Player can reach the extraction point from the start (BFS on walkable+jumpable graph, max jump height 120 px, max jump distance 180 px).
  - If any check fails → re-roll with `seed + 1`.

## ProgressionAndUnlocks

RULES:
- **WorldMap starts** with W1S1 unlocked. Clearing a stage unlocks the next stage in sequence. Clearing W1S5 unlocks W2S1. Clearing W2S5 triggers victory.
- **Gun unlock chests** are hard-placed: W1S2 → Rivet Shotgun; W1S4 → Coil Rifle; W2S2 → Flare Launcher. Opening the chest sets that Gun's `unlocked = true` permanently (persists across deaths within the run; resets on full game restart).
- **UpgradeChips** (4 per world): permanently modify the target gun's stat for the rest of the run.
- **Checkpoints:** Each stage has 1 mid-stage checkpoint flag. Reaching it saves position for respawn after death (but not after a full game-over/lives-0).

## Scoring

RULES:
- Points: Crawler 100, Turret 200, Drone 150, ShieldTrooper 350, Boss-phase-clear 1000.
- Speed bonus: if stage completed in ≤ 60 % of par time (par = width/200 seconds), add 500.
- No-damage bonus: +1000 if `Player.hp == 100` at extraction.
- Chip bonus: +250 per UpgradeChip collected.
- Rank thresholds per stage are in Stage record (`bronzeThreshold`, `silverThreshold`, `goldThreshold`). Displayed on stage-complete screen.

# 4. PROGRESSION AND DIFFICULTY

**Progression order:**
W1S1 → W1S2 (shotgun) → W1S3 → W1S4 (coil rifle) → W1S5 (Rust Colossus) → W2S1 → W2S2 (flare launcher) → W2S3 → W2S4 → W2S5 (Core Warden) → Victory.

**Early-game ease (W1S1–S2):**
- Only Crawlers (hp 30, one shot with pistol at 12 dmg × 3 hits). No turrets, no drones.
- Ground is mostly continuous; gaps are 3 tiles wide (easy jump).
- Player has 3 lives; first death is a learning moment, not a wall.
- Scrap Pistol: 6 shots/s, 12 dmg, 12-round mag → trivially kills crawlers.

**Mid-game (W1S3–S4, W2S1–S3):**
- Turrets force positioning (can't rush through). Drones punish staying on ground.
- ShieldTroopers demand shotgun (break shield fast) or coil rifle (pierce through).
- Gaps widen to 5–6 tiles; steam vents and pistons add timing pressure.
- Player is expected to die once or twice per stage; checkpoints soften this.

**Late-game / Boss (W1S5, W2S5):**
- Rust Colossus Phase 3 requires using all 3 guns: pistol to clear summoned crawlers, shotgun to stagger the dash, coil rifle for the 3-pierce volley window.
- Core Warden Phase 3 (floor electrification) forces platforming under fire; Phase 4 demands gun-swaps mid-fight.
- Boss HP pools (200/180 per phase) mean ~15–20 hits per phase with mid-tier guns; the fight lasts 60–90 s of skilled play.

**Recovery on failure:**
- Death within a stage: respawn at checkpoint (or start) with full HP, 1.5 s i-frames, score kept. Lose 1 life.
- All lives lost: return to Stage Select. No penalty beyond lost time; stage is immediately retryable.
- No permadeath, no lives between stages (3 lives reset each stage attempt).

**Difficulty scaling knobs:**
- `difficultyTier` (1–5) controls: enemy density (one per 16→10 tiles), gap width (3→6 tiles), hazard count, enemy mix.
- Boss intervals shrink per phase (2.5 s → 1.5 s).
- UpgradeChips provide the player's own scaling: +4 damage or +1 fireRate on a gun makes late content noticeably more comfortable.

# VISUAL DESIGN — "FORGE BREAKER"

**2D side-scroller. Fixed horizontal follow-camera, player offset ~35% from left edge, vertical deadzone of 80 units; camera tracks player X at 8% per frame and Y at 12% per frame. No rotation. No zoom except a 1.15× punch on screen-shake triggers.**

---

# 1. THE LOOK IN ONE PARAGRAPH

A vast abandoned mega-factory rendered in thick, confident vector shapes with a warm-to-cool duotone palette: deep charcoal (#1A1A2E) foundations, hot molten amber (#FF8C00) and electric cyan (#00F5FF) accent lighting, and bruised violet (#4A1A6B) midtones. Everything reads as heavy industrial machinery — riveted plate, dripping coolant, spinning gears, hissing valves — but stylised into clean geometric silhouettes with 3-px dark outlines. The one screenshot that sells the game: the player mid-air, crouched-then-jumping over a conveyor belt of glowing ore, muzzle-flash of a scatter cannon painting the scene amber, two rivet-drones exploding into sparks behind, and the parallax background showing three receding layers of factory architecture fading into violet haze.

---

# 2. THE SPACE

**Stage dimensions:** Each stage is a horizontal strip, 4 800 units wide × 1 200 units tall. Camera viewport is 960 × 540 units. Player starts at X = 120, Y = ground level (Y = 1 080 in World 1 stages, Y = 1 040 in World 2 stages). Goal exit is always at X ≈ 4 600–4 700, a blast-door or teleport pad.

**Regions per stage (5 zones, each ~900 units wide):**

| Zone | World 1 (Smelting Yards) | World 2 (Cooling Towers) | Eye-cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 – Entry | Loading dock, forklifts, crate stacks | Airlock vestibule, frost-rimmed pipes | Warm floor glow vs. cold blue floor glow |
| 2 – Gauntlet | Conveyor belts, piston platforms, slag pits | Cryo-tunnels, slippery ice plates, vent shafts | Moving horizontal elements vs. vertical ice columns |
| 3 – Combat | Smelter floor: molten channels, crane hooks | Coolant reservoir: floating debris, submersible pods | Orange light pools vs. cyan light pools on floor |
| 4 – Puzzle | Gear-maze, pressure-plate bridges | Thermal-exchange puzzle, colour-coded valve doors | Mechanical rotating cogs vs. liquid-flow pipes |
| 5 – Exit | Blast furnace core, heat-shimmer | Central chiller, frozen fountain | Red/amber ambient shift vs. blue/white shift |

**World differentiation:** World 1 palette skews amber/red/charcoal; World 2 skews cyan/white/deep-blue. Floor tile texture differs (riveted steel plate vs. frosted ceramic tile). Background parallax layers change content (smokestacks & gears vs. condensation towers & water pipes).

**Stage 5 of each world** is a boss arena: a single wide room (2 400 × 1 200) with no side-scrolling; camera locks.

**Procedural note:** Stage layouts are hand-authored per stage (10 total). No procedural generation. A "good" stage roll means: at least 2 vertical platforming sequences per zone, at least 1 gun-pickup visible in zones 2 and 4, and a clear silhouette read of the exit door from the start of zone 5. A "bad" roll: player cannot see the next platform's landing spot due to foreground prop overlap, or zone boundaries are ambiguous (no colour/light shift).

**Rendering decision: 2D, HTML5 Canvas 2D context.** A side-scrolling platformer does not benefit from 3D; the tile grid, AABB collision, and sprite compositing all map directly to `CanvasRenderingContext2D`.

---

# 1. CONVENTIONS

## Coordinate system

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Unit** | 1 unit = 1 logical pixel. The canvas backing store is `canvas.width × canvas.height` physical pixels; the logical viewport is always **960 × 540** units. A CSS transform scales the canvas to the browser window; all game math stays in logical pixels. |
| **Axes** | X increases **rightward**. Y increases **downward** (canvas-native). "Up" in gameplay text means **−Y**. Gravity is a positive Y acceleration. |
| **Origin** | Top-left of the logical viewport, `(0, 0)`. The level's world origin is also `(0, 0)`; the camera offset translates world coordinates into viewport coordinates. |
| **Tile grid** | The level is a grid of **32 × 32**-unit tiles. Tile coordinate `(tx, ty)` maps to world position `(tx*32, ty*32)`. |
| **Ground rule** | The lowest solid tile row of a stage is the floor. A player standing on it has their feet at `y = floorTileRow*32`. There is no infinite ground; falling below `y > stageHeight*32 + 64` triggers death. |

## Update loop

```
const DT = 1/60;          // fixed timestep, seconds
```

Each animation frame:

1. Accumulate real elapsed time.
2. While accumulator ≥ DT: run **one tick** (see system order below), decrement accumulator.
3. After all ticks for the frame: **render once**.

If accumulator exceeds 5 DTs (tab was backgrounded), clamp to 5 DTs to avoid spiral-of-death.

### Tick system order (strict)

| Order | System | Why this position |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `input.update()` | Latch new key states, build the frame's input snapshot. |
| 2 | `progression.update()` | Check stage transitions / death → reload level before physics. |
| 3 | `level.update()` | Animate tiles, move platforms, tick hazards. |
| 4 | `player.update()` | Apply input → velocity → position → collision. |
| 5 | `weapons.update()` | Fire held guns, advance bullet pools. |
| 6 | `enemies.update()` | AI, movement, collision with bullets and player. |
| 7 | `camera.update()` | Follow player, clamp to stage bounds. |
| 8 | `audio.update()` | Flush queued sound events. |
| 9 | `renderer.draw()` | **After all ticks**, outside the while-loop. |

## Random source

One seeded PRNG, named **`rng`**, implemented as a 32-bit xorshift. Every module that needs randomness calls `rng.next()` (returns float in [0,1)) or `rng.nextInt(n)` (returns int in [0,n)). **No call to `Math.random()` exists anywhere in the codebase.** The PRNG is reseeded only via `seed(n)` in the debug API or at the start of a stage.

## Controls

| Input | Action | Binding |
|---|---|---|
| A / ← | Move left | `KeyA`, `ArrowLeft` |
| D / → | Move right | `KeyD`, `ArrowRight` |
| W / ↑ / Space | Jump | `KeyW`, `ArrowUp`, `Space` |
| S / ↓ | Crouch (hold) | `KeyS`, `ArrowDown` |
| J / Enter / Z | Shoot (hold) | `KeyJ`, `Enter`, `KeyZ` |
| K / Shift | Switch gun (cycle) | `KeyK`, `ShiftLeft` |
| Touch: left half of screen | Move left / right (relative to touch x vs. player screen x) | Pointer events |
| Touch: bottom-left virtual dpad | Move + crouch | Pointer events |
| Touch: bottom-right virtual buttons | Jump, Shoot, Switch | Pointer events |

Keyboard state is read from a `keydown`/`keyup` map; the debug API can inject synthetic key events.

# 2. MODULES

## Module table

| Module | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `game` | State machine: TITLE → PLAYING → PAUSED → STAGE_CLEAR → GAME_OVER. Owns the main loop and the single `ctx` (global context). |
| `input` | Reads keyboard / pointer events, maintains key-state snapshot, exposes `isDown(action)`. |
| `progression` | World/stage index, lives, score, checkpoint, stage-clear logic, death → respawn or game-over. |
| `level` | Tilemap data, solid/hazard/platform tiles, parallax layers, stage geometry, `stageWidth`, `stageHeight`. |
| `player` | Player entity: position, velocity, state (idle/run/jump/crouch/hurt), health, animation frame. |
| `weapons` | 4 gun types, ammo, fire-rate timers, bullet pool, bullet physics & collision. |
| `enemies` | Enemy pool, per-type AI, damage, death, drop generation. |
| `camera` | Viewport offset, follow logic, screen-shake accumulator. |
| `audio` | Web Audio graph, generated SFX (oscillator + noise buffers), music loop scheduling. |
| `renderer` | Canvas 2D draw calls: clear, parallax bg, tiles, entities, HUD, transitions. |
| `ui` | HUD elements (health bar, ammo, gun icon, score, world/stage label), title screen text layout, pause overlay. |

## Global context

Every module reads and writes **only** through this object. No module imports another.

```js
const ctx = {{
  // --- timing ---
  time: 0,                    // float, seconds elapsed in current stage
  frame: 0,                   // int, total ticks since seed
  dt: 1/60,                   // float, fixed step

  // --- rng ---
  rng: null,                  // {{ next(), nextInt(n), seed(n) }}

  // --- game state ---
  state: 'TITLE',             // 'TITLE'|'PLAYING'|'PAUSED'|'STAGE_CLEAR'|'GAME_OVER'
  paused: false,              // bool

  // --- progression ---
  world: 1,                   // int 1..2
  stage: 1,                   // int 1..5
  lives: <start lives>,       // int
  score: 0,                   // int
  stageClearTimer: 0,         // float, counts down after flag reached

  // --- level ---
  level: {{
    tiles: null,              // Uint8Array, width*height, tile-type enum
    tileW: 32,                // int
    tileH: 32,                // int
    width: 0,                 // int, tiles across
    height: 0,                // int, tiles down
    stageWidth: 0,            // float, world px
    stageHeight: 0,           // float, world px
    spawnX: 0,                // float
    spawnY: 0,                // float
    flagX: 0,                 // float, stage-end trigger
    platforms: [],            // [{{x,y,w,h,vx,vy}}], moving platforms
    hazards: [],              // [{{x,y,w,h}}]
    parallax: [],             // [{{layer,src}}]
  }},

  // --- player ---
  player: {{
    x: 0,                     // float, world px
    y: 0,                     // float, world px
    vx: 0,                    // float, px/s
    vy: 0,                    // float, px/s
    w: <player width>,        // int, px (standing)
    h: <player height>,       // int, px (standing)
    crouchH: <crouch height>, // int, px
    facing: 1,                // int, +1 right / -1 left
    grounded: false,          // bool
    crouching: false,         // bool
    jumping: false,           // bool
    health: <player max hp>,  // int
    maxHealth: <player max hp>,// int
    invulnTimer: 0,           // float, seconds of invulnerability after hit
    anim: 'idle',             // 'idle'|'run'|'jump'|'fall'|'crouch'|'hurt'
    animFrame: 0,             // int
    animTimer: 0,             // float
    gunIndex: 0,              // int 0..3
    alive: true,              // bool
  }},

  // --- weapons ---
  weapons: {{
    guns: [                   // 4 entries, filled at level load
      {{ name:'pistol',  dmg:<pistol dmg>,  rate:<pistol rate>,  ammo:<pistol ammo>, maxAmmo:<pistol max>, bulletSpeed:<pistol speed>, spread:0,    pellets:1 }},
      {{ name:'shotgun', dmg:<shotgun dmg>, rate:<shotgun rate>, ammo:<shotgun ammo>, maxAmmo:<shotgun max>, bulletSpeed:<shotgun speed>, spread:<shotgun spread>, pellets:<shotgun pellets> }},
      {{ name:'laser',   dmg:<laser dmg>,   rate:<laser rate>,   ammo:<laser ammo>,   maxAmmo:<laser max>,   bulletSpeed:<laser speed>,   spread:0,    pellets:1, piercing:true }},
      {{ name:'rocket',  dmg:<rocket dmg>,  rate:<rocket rate>,  ammo:<rocket ammo>,  maxAmmo:<rocket max>,  bulletSpeed:<rocket speed>,  spread:0,    pellets:1, aoeRadius:<rocket aoe> }},
    ],
    fireTimer: 0,             // float, seconds until next shot allowed
    bullets: [],              // pool, see §3
  }},

  // --- enemies ---
  enemies: {{
    pool: [],                 // see §3 caps
    spawnQueue: [],           // [{{x,y,type,timer}}] generated at level load
  }},

  // --- camera ---
  camera: {{
    x: 0,                     // float, world px of viewport left edge
    y: 0,                     // float, world px of viewport top edge
    shakeX: 0, shakeY: 0,    // float, screen-shake offset
    shakeTimer: 0,           // float
  }},

  // --- input snapshot (rebuilt each tick) ---
  input: {{
    left: false, right: false, jump: false, crouch: false,
    shoot: false, switchGun: false,
    switchGunPressed: false,  // edge-triggered
    jumpPressed: false,       // edge-triggered
  }},

  // --- audio ---
  audio: {{
    ctx: null,                // AudioContext or null
    masterGain: null,         // GainNode
    sfxQueue: [],             // [{{type, x, y, time}}]
    musicPlaying: false,      // bool
    musicNodes: [],           // active oscillators/nodes
  }},

  // --- render ---
  canvas: null,               // HTMLCanvasElement
  c2d: null,                  // CanvasRenderingContext2D
}};
```

## Module exports

### `game`
```
init(canvasEl, opts) → void
  // Wires canvas, builds ctx, installs window.__game, starts rAF loop.
start() → void
  // TITLE → PLAYING. Loads world 1 stage 1.
tick() → void
  // Runs one DT tick through the system order.
render() → void
  // Calls renderer.draw().
pause() / resume() → void
```

### `input`
```
attach(canvasEl) → void
  // Binds keydown/keyup/pointerdown/pointerup on window/canvas.
snapshot() → void
  // Writes into ctx.input from the raw key map.
isDown(action: string) → bool
inject(action: string, down: bool) → void
  // Debug: set a synthetic key state.
```

### `progression`
```
loadStage(world: int, stage: int) → void
  // Resets player, clears pools, calls level.generate(), spawns enemies.
checkStageClear() → void
  // If player.x >= level.flagX → state='STAGE_CLEAR'.
checkDeath() → void
  // If player.y > stageHeight+64 or health<=0 → lose life or game over.
nextStage() → void
advanceWorld() → void
```

### `level`
```
generate(world: int, stage: int) → void
  // Fills ctx.level from tile data + rng for decorations.
getTile(tx, ty) → int
isSolid(tx, ty) → bool
resolveAABB(entity) → {{grounded, hitX, hitY}}
  // Moves entity, clamps to solid tiles, returns collision flags.
update() → void
  // Advances moving platforms and hazard timers.
```

### `player`
```
update() → void
  // Reads ctx.input, applies acceleration/gravity, calls level.resolveAABB.
hurt(amount: int) → void
respawn() → void
  // Reset to spawn point, invulnTimer = <invuln duration>.
```

### `weapons`
```
update() → void
  // If input.shoot and fireTimer<=0 → fire(). Advance bullets.
fire() → void
  // Spawn bullet(s) from gun at player pos, decrement ammo, reset fireTimer.
switchGun() → void
  // gunIndex = (gunIndex+1)%4.
```

### `enemies`
```
update() → void
  // Tick AI, move, check bullet collisions, check player contact.
spawn(type: string, x: float, y: float) → int
  // Returns enemy id or -1 if pool full.
damage(id: int, amount: int) → bool
  // Returns true if killed.
```

### `camera`
```
update() → void
  // Lerp toward player, clamp to [0, stageWidth-960]. Decay shake.
addShake(intensity: float, duration: float) → void
```

### `audio`
```
init() → void
  // Create AudioContext (deferred to first user gesture).
playSFX(type: string) → void
stopMusic() / startMusic(track: string) → void
update() → void
  // Flush sfxQueue.
```

### `renderer`
```
draw() → void
  // Full-frame: clear → parallax → tiles → entities → bullets → HUD → overlays.
```

### `ui`
```
drawHUD(c2d) → void
drawTitle(c2d) → void
drawPause(c2d) → void
drawStageClear(c2d) → void
```

# 3. BUDGETS

All numbers below are **hard caps the test suite asserts**. Exceeding any one is a build failure.

| Resource | Cap | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Frame time (tick + render) | **≤ 14 ms** (target 60 fps) | 960×540 canvas, no GPU compositing tricks. |
| Canvas draw calls per frame | **≤ 200** `drawImage`/`fillRect`/`stroke` ops | Tiles visible ≈ 30×17 = 510, but only non-empty tiles drawn; cull to viewport. |
| Tile draws per frame | **≤ 540** (viewport 30×17 tiles) | Cull off-screen columns/rows. |
| Player entity count | **1** | Single protagonist. |
| Enemy pool size | **64** | Ring buffer; `spawn` returns -1 when full. |
| Bullet pool size | **128** | Ring buffer; oldest recycled if full. |
| Particle pool size | **256** | Hit sparks, explosions. |
| Moving platforms per stage | **≤ 32** | |
| Hazard tiles per stage | **≤ 64** | |
| Total entities (enemies + bullets + particles) | **≤ 448** | Sum of three pools. |
| Level tilemap size per stage | **≤ 400 × 40** tiles (12 800 × 1 280 px) | Uint8Array = 16 KB. |
| JS heap (steady state, mid-stage) | **≤ 32 MB** | No per-frame allocations in hot loops; use pools. |
| Audio nodes live | **≤ 16** | Oscillators + gain nodes; auto-disconnect on end. |
| `ctx` object total keys | **≤ 120** | Flat enough for `getState()` to serialise in < 1 ms. |

# 4. DEBUG API

`game.init()` installs `window.__game`. Every method is **synchronous**, returns **only plain data** (numbers, strings, booleans, arrays, nested plain objects). No Promises, no DOM nodes, no functions in return values.

| Call | What it does | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `start()` | Sets `state='PLAYING'`, calls `progression.loadStage(1,1)`. No click needed. | `undefined` |
| `step(dt, n)` | Runs exactly `n` ticks of length `dt` seconds (updates all systems in order), then calls `renderer.draw()` once. Does **not** wait for rAF. | `undefined` |
| `setTime(t)` | Advances simulation to absolute time `t` seconds (runs the required number of DT ticks) **without drawing**. | `undefined` |
| `seed(n)` | Reseeds `rng` with integer `n`, then rebuilds all generated content (tilemap, enemy spawn queue, decorations). Resets `time` and `frame` to 0. | `undefined` |
| `getState()` | Deep-clones every field of `ctx` into a plain object and returns it. | `{{ time, frame, state, world, stage, lives, score, player:{{...}}, weapons:{{...}}, enemies:{{...}}, camera:{{...}}, level:{{...}}, input:{{...}} }}` |
| `setField(path, value)` | Sets `ctx` field at dot-path, e.g. `"player.health"`. | `undefined` |
| `moveDir(dx)` | Sets persistent horizontal input: −1 left, 0 stop, +1 right. Persists until next call. | `undefined` |
| `jump()` | Fires one jump press (sets `jumpPressed=true` for one tick). | `undefined` |
| `crouch(down)` | `true` = hold crouch, `false` = release. | `undefined` |
| `shoot(down)` | `true` = hold fire, `false` = release. | `undefined` |
| `switchGun()` | One cycle press. | `undefined` |
| `spawnEnemy(type, x, y)` | Calls `enemies.spawn(type,x,y)`. | `{{id, x, y, type, health}}` or `{{id:-1}}` if pool full |
| `spawnBullet(gunIndex, x, y, dir)` | Directly inserts a bullet into the pool. | `{{id, x, y, vx, vy}}` |
| `skipTo(world, stage)` | Calls `progression.loadStage(world, stage)`. | `undefined` |
| `setHealth(v)` | Sets `player.health = v`. | `undefined` |
| `setAmmo(gunIndex, v)` | Sets that gun's ammo. | `undefined` |
| `setPos(x, y)` | Teleports player. | `undefined` |
| `getFPS()` | Returns measured FPS over last 60 real frames (0 if stepped). | `number` |
| `getDrawCalls()` | Returns draw-call count from last `renderer.draw()`. | `number` |

**Determinism contract:** Given the same `seed(n)` and the same sequence of `step`/input calls, `getState()` returns a byte-identical JSON string. The PRNG is the sole source of nondeterminism and is fully controlled by `seed()`.

# 5. TESTS

All checks run through `window.__game` only. Each check is a call sequence followed by an assertion on `getState()`.

### Boot & state machine

1. **Title draws.** `init()` called; `getState().state === 'TITLE'`. Canvas exists, `getDrawCalls() > 0` after one manual `step(1/60,1)`.

2. **Start enters play.** `start()`. `getState().state === 'PLAYING'`, `world === 1`, `stage === 1`, `player.alive === true`, `player.health === <player max hp>`.

### Movement & physics

3. **Walk right.** `seed(42)`, `start()`, `moveDir(1)`, `step(1/60, 60)`. Assert `player.x > spawnX + <player speed> * 0.5` (moved right), `player.grounded === true`, `player.anim === 'run'`.

4. **Walk left.** `moveDir(-1)`, `step(1/60, 30)`. Assert `player.x` decreased.

5. **Stop.** `moveDir(0)`, `step(1/60, 60)`. Assert `Math.abs(player.vx) < 1` (friction applied).

6. **Jump.** `moveDir(0)`, `jump()`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `player.jumping === true`, `player.grounded === false`, `player.vy < 0` (upward). After `step(1/60, 40)`, assert `player.grounded === true` (landed).

7. **Crouch.** `crouch(true)`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `player.crouching === true`, `player.h === <crouch height>`. `crouch(false)`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `player.crouching === false`, `player.h === <player height>`.

8. **Gravity / fall.** `setPos(0, 0)` above any platform, `step(1/60, 30)`. Assert `player.y` increased (fell), `player.vy > 0`.

### Weapons

9. **Pistol fire.** `seed(42)`, `start()`, `switchGun()` × 0 (pistol is index 0). `shoot(true)`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `weapons.bullets.length ≥ 1`, `weapons.guns[0].ammo === <pistol ammo> - 1`. `shoot(false)`.

10. **Switch gun.** `switchGun()`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `player.gunIndex === 1`. `switchGun()` × 3 more → `player.gunIndex === 0` (wraps).

11. **Shotgun pellets.** `setField('player.gunIndex', 1)`, `shoot(true)`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert bullets spawned this shot = `<shotgun pellets>` (check `weapons.bullets` length delta). `shoot(false)`.

12. **Laser piercing.** `setField('player.gunIndex', 2)`. `spawnEnemy('grunt', player.x + 100, player.y)`. `shoot(true)`, `step(1/60, 5)`. Assert the laser bullet's `id` is still alive (passed through enemy, enemy health reduced by `<laser dmg>`). `shoot(false)`.

13. **Rocket AoE.** `setField('player.gunIndex', 3)`. Spawn 3 enemies within `<rocket aoe>` radius of target. Fire rocket, `step(1/60, 30)`. Assert all 3 enemies' health ≤ 0 (killed).

### Enemies & combat

14. **Enemy spawn.** `seed(7)`, `start()`. `spawnEnemy('grunt', 500, 300)`. Assert `enemies.pool` contains entry with `type==='grunt'`, `x===500`, `y===300`, `health === <grunt hp>`.

15. **Player takes damage.** `setPos(<enemy contact x>, <enemy contact y>)`. `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `player.health === <player max hp> - <grunt dmg>`, `player.invulnTimer > 0`.

16. **Invulnerability.** Immediately `step(1/60, 1)` again. Assert `player.health` unchanged (still invulnerable).

17. **Death & respawn.** `setHealth(0)`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `player.alive === false`, `lives === <start lives> - 1`. After `step(1/60, <respawn delay>)`, assert `player.alive === true`, `player.health === <player max hp>`, `player.invulnTimer > 0`.

### Level & progression

18. **Tile collision.** Place player at `y` of a known solid tile row. `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `player.grounded === true`, `player.y === <that tile row> * 32 - <player height>`.

19. **Stage clear.** `setPos(level.flagX + 1, player.y)`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `state === 'STAGE_CLEAR'`. After `step(1/60, <stage clear duration>)`, assert `stage === 2`, `state === 'PLAYING'`.

20. **World progression.** `skipTo(1, 5)`, clear stage 5. Assert `world === 2`, `stage === 1`.

21. **Game over.** `setHealth(1)`, take damage → `setHealth(0)`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Set `lives = 0` beforehand. Assert `state === 'GAME_OVER'`.

### Camera

22. **Follow & clamp.** `moveDir(1)`, `step(1/60, 120)`. Assert `camera.x > 0`, `camera.x <= level.stageWidth - 960`.

### Audio

23. **SFX queue.** `shoot(true)`, `step(1/60, 1)`. Assert `audio.sfxQueue.length > 0` (at least one 'pistol' event queued). `shoot(false)`.

### Determinism

24. **Reproducibility.**
```
seed(1234); start(); moveDir(1); jump(); shoot(true);
step(1/60, 300);
const a = JSON.stringify(getState());

seed(1234); start(); moveDir(1); jump(); shoot(true);
step(1/60, 300);
const b = JSON.stringify(getState());
```
Assert `a === b` (byte-identical).

### Budgets

25. **Draw calls.** After `step(1/60, 1)`, assert `getDrawCalls() <= 200`.

26. **Entity cap.** `spawnEnemy('grunt', 100, 100)` × 65. Assert 65th call returns `{{id: -1}}`. Assert `enemies.pool.length === 64`.

27. **Bullet cap.** Fire pistol continuously for 200 ticks without bullets hitting anything. Assert `weapons.bullets.length <= 128`.

# 7. RISKS

### Risk 1 – Y-axis sign inversion between physics and rendering
**What:** Canvas Y grows downward, but "up" in gameplay (jump, gravity direction, tile placement) is intuitive as Y-decreasing. A mismatch between `player.vy < 0` meaning "jumping up" and a tilemap generator that places ground at high Y values will cause the player to fall through the floor or stick to the ceiling.

**Prevention rule:** **Gravity is always a positive Y acceleration** (`vy += gravity * dt`, gravity > 0). The tilemap's solid tiles have **higher** Y values than the air above them. `level.resolveAABB` checks `entity.y + entity.h` against tile top. Every physics test (checks 6, 8, 18) asserts the sign explicitly. No module may negate Y before writing to `player.y`; the renderer is the sole place that may apply a visual flip (and for a 2D side-scroller it doesn't need to).

### Risk 2 – Timing race between `step()` and the rAF loop during tests
**What:** The debug API's `step()` advances the simulation synchronously, but the real `requestAnimationFrame` loop is still running. If a test calls `step(1/60, 300)` while rAF ticks are interleaved, the game advances more than 300 ticks and `setTime(t)` overshoots, breaking determinism.

**Prevention rule:** `window.__game.step()` and `setTime()` **cancel the rAF handle** on entry and **do not restart it** until the test calls a hypothetical `game.resume()` (or the page is reloaded). The main loop sets a flag `__game._stepping = true` during `step()`/`setTime()`; the rAF callback checks this flag and returns immediately without ticking if it is set. Tests that need real-time FPS use `getFPS()` only after calling `game.resume()`.

### Risk 3 – Tile-culling off-by-one causes flicker or missed collision at screen edges
**What:** The renderer culls tiles to the viewport (≈ 30 × 17 tiles). A one-pixel rounding error in `Math.floor(camera.x / 32)` vs. `Math.ceil((camera.x + 960) / 32)` can skip the leftmost or rightmost tile column, so a player standing on a platform whose left edge is one pixel off-screen has no collision check and falls through.

**Prevention rule:** The culling window is **padded by 1 tile in every direction**: `txMin = Math.floor(camera.x / 32) - 1`, `txMax = Math.ceil((camera.x + 960) / 32) + 1`, same for Y. Collision (`resolveAABB`) queries the tilemap in **world coordinates**, not viewport coordinates, so it is immune to camera position. Test 18 places the player at the exact left and right edges of the viewport and asserts `grounded === true`.
