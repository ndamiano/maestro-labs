As a human playtest, the opening is visually charming and immediately readable — you know your size, your goal (100), and the eat-or-be-eaten rule at a glance. The early game feels a touch slow and fiddly, though: at size 8 you're so small that the guppies are barely bigger than you and they flee the moment you get close, so the first few points are a chase you almost never win, which can read as 'nothing is happening' for a while. Once you land a meal, the size bar and ring cues promise a satisfying ramp, but the very first minute asks for patience and a lot of nudging. The camera-follow and obstacle slides make navigation feel physical and pleasant, and the reef is busy enough to keep your eye moving — it just needs the tiniest fish to feel a little more catchable so the growth loop clicks sooner.
