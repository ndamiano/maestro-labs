Build this 2D top-down haunted forest escape where you find three keys and reach the gate before dawn. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

RECORDS: Player, Wraith, Key, Battery, Gate, ForestMap, DawnClock.

PLAYER: x (starting 960), y (starting 960), facing (radians, starting 0), hp (100, max 100), stamina (100, max 100), fear (0, max 100), flashlightOn (boolean, starting true), flashlightBattery (100, max 100), keysCollected (integer, starting 0), sprinting (boolean, starting false), invulnerableTimer (seconds, starting 0).

WRAITH: x, y (set by generator), facing (radians), state (one of patrol, hunt, lost, retreat), patrolAnchorX, patrolAnchorY (the wraith's home point it returns to), patrolRadius (80 px), patrolAngle (radians, current sweep angle), speed (px/s, patrol 50, hunt 130, retreat 160), detectionCone (120 degrees), detectionRange (180 px), closeRange (50 px), huntTimer (seconds, 0), lostTimer (seconds, 0), retreatTimer (seconds, 0). There are 5 wraiths in the forest.

KEY: x, y (set by generator), collected (boolean, starting false), label (one of "shrine key", "clearing key", "oak key").

BATTERY: x, y (set by generator), collected (boolean, starting false). There are 8 batteries in the forest.

GATE: x (960), y (8), radius (40 px), unlocked (boolean, starting false). The gate sits at the north edge of the map.

FORESTMAP: a 60-by-60 tile grid, each tile 32 pixels, world is 1920 by 1920 pixels. Each tile is one of: dense (blocks movement and line-of-sight), sparse (blocks line-of-sight only, walkable), path (open, walkable, transparent), clearing (open, walkable, transparent), water (blocks movement, transparent). The map is generated per seed.

DAWNCLK: secondsRemaining (600, counts down to 0), dawn (boolean, starting false).

SYSTEMS: SEEDED GENERATOR, FLASHLIGHT, WRAITH AI, PLAYER CONTROLS, DAWN TIMER, FEAR, COLLECTIBLES, ESCAPE.

SEEDED GENERATOR: the forest map is built from a seed in this order. First, lay a base of 70% dense, 15% sparse, 10% path, 5% clearing across all 3600 tiles. Then carve 6 paths, each a random-walk of 20 to 40 tiles connecting two points, converting tiles to path and giving adjacent tiles a 50% chance of becoming clearing. Then dig 4 circular clearings of radius 3 to 5 tiles at random locations. Then place 2 small ponds, each an ellipse of 4 by 3 tiles, converting those tiles to water. Then verify: the tile at (30,30) and its 5-tile-radius neighborhood must be walkable (path or clearing or sparse); the tile at (30,0) must be path or clearing for the gate; the 3 key tiles and all 8 battery tiles must sit on walkable tiles (path, clearing, or sparse). Place the gate at world coordinate (960, 8). Place 3 keys at three distinct walkable tiles that are each at least 400 px from every other key and at least 300 px from the player start (960,960) and at least 200 px from the gate. Place 8 batteries on distinct walkable tiles at least 150 px from any key and at least 100 px from the player start. Place 5 wraiths on walkable tiles, each at least 350 px from the player start and at least 200 px from every other wraith; each wraith's patrolAnchor is its spawn tile and patrolRadius is 80. Re-roll with the next seed if any check fails.

FLASHLIGHT: each tick, if the player's flashlightOn is true and flashlightBattery is greater than 0, subtract 1.5 from flashlightBattery and clamp at 0; if flashlightBattery reaches 0, set flashlightOn to false. The light cone extends 220 px forward from the player in a 90-degree arc centred on the player's facing. The small ambient glow around the player is a circle of radius 40 px that is always on. When flashlightOn is false, only the 40 px ambient glow illuminates. Toggling the flashlight (F key or R touch button) sets flashlightOn to its opposite if battery is greater than 0.

WRAITH AI: each wraith runs a state machine each tick. In patrol: the wraith's patrolAngle advances by 0.4 radians per second; its position is patrolAnchorX plus patrolRadius times cosine of patrolAngle, patrolAnchorY plus patrolRadius times sine of patrolAngle; its speed is 50; its facing is the direction of travel. In patrol, if the player is within the wraith's detectionCone (120 degrees from wraith facing) and within detectionRange (180 px) and the player is illuminated (the player's position falls inside the wraith's cone and the tile between wraith and player has no dense tile blocking line-of-sight, checked by sampling 5 points along the line), OR if the player is within closeRange (50 px) regardless of light, then set state to hunt and reset huntTimer to 0. In hunt: the wraith moves directly toward the player at speed 130; its facing is the angle to the player; increment huntTimer by dt; if the player is no longer in the wraith's cone and not within closeRange, set state to lost, reset lostTimer to 0. If huntTimer exceeds 12 seconds, also set state to lost. In lost: the wraith moves toward the last-known player position at speed 50; increment lostTimer; if lostTimer exceeds 5 seconds, set state to patrol and reset patrolAngle to the angle from patrolAnchor to the wraith's current position. In retreat (entered when a wraith makes contact, described below): the wraith moves directly away from the player at speed 160; its facing is the angle away; increment retreatTimer; if retreatTimer exceeds 3 seconds, set state to patrol. Wraiths are not blocked by dense tiles; they phase through them. Wraiths cannot enter water tiles; they path around by sliding along the water edge.

PLAYER CONTROLS: WASD and arrow keys both move the player. The player's facing is the angle of movement (last movement direction is held). Pressing Shift or the sprint touch button sets sprinting to true; releasing sets it false. If sprinting and stamina is greater than 0, move at 180 px/s and subtract 25 per second from stamina; if sprinting but stamina is 0, move at 100 px/s. If not sprinting, move at 100 px/s and add 15 per second to stamina, clamped at 100. Movement is blocked by dense and water tiles: the player's 20 px radius circle cannot overlap those tiles. F key or the light touch button toggles the flashlight. Touch: a virtual joystick in the lower-left quarter of the screen controls movement, a sprint button in the lower-right, a light button beside it. Escape pauses.

DAWN TIMER: each tick, subtract dt from DawnClock.secondsRemaining; clamp at 0. When secondsRemaining reaches 0, set DawnClock.dawn to true and trigger game over (defeat). The HUD shows the remaining time as minutes and seconds.

FEAR: each tick, for every wraith in any state except retreat, if the distance from wraith to player is less than 200 px, add 10 times dt to player fear. If no wraith is within 200 px, subtract 5 times dt from player fear. Clamp fear between 0 and 100. If fear reaches 100, set flashlightOn to false, set flashlightBattery to 0, and after 5 seconds (tracked by a local timer) reset flashlightBattery to 30 and allow the flashlight to be toggled on again. Fear above 70 causes a visual vignette pulse; fear above 90 causes the screen to shake 2 px.

COLLECTIBLES: each tick, if the player's position is within 24 px of a key's position and the key's collected is false, set collected to true and increment player keysCollected. If the player's position is within 24 px of a battery's position and the battery's collected is false, set collected to true, add 40 to flashlightBattery (clamped at 100). Each tick, check if keysCollected equals 3; if so, set Gate.unlocked to true.

ESCAPE: each tick, if the player's position is within Gate.radius (40 px) of Gate's position and Gate.unlocked is true, trigger victory. If the player touches the gate while unlocked is false, nothing happens (the gate is solid). A wraith contact: if the distance from a wraith in hunt state to the player is less than 30 px, subtract 10 from player hp, add 30 to player fear, set the wraith's state to retreat and reset retreatTimer to 0, and set player invulnerableTimer to 1.0 second (during which no further wraith contact is processed for that wraith). If player hp reaches 0, trigger game over (death).

CORE LOOP: the player jogs through the dark forest, sweeping the flashlight cone to reveal paths and avoid dense thickets, hunting for the three keys hidden in the shrine, the burnt clearing, and the hollow oak. Each key found is a small victory and a risk: the flashlight beam that revealed the key also attracted a wraith. The player must manage the battery (finding pickups, conserving light), manage fear (running from wraiths, letting fear decay in safe ground), and manage stamina (sprinting to outrun a hunting wraith, then walking to recover). The 10-minute dawn clock ticks down, visible in the HUD. The tension escalates: early keys are safer, later keys are in wraith-heavy zones, and by the end the player may be near the gate but low on battery with a wraith circling. The win is reaching the gate with all three keys before the timer hits zero; the loss is dawn arriving or hp hitting zero.

DEBUG API: start() enters play from the title without a click and returns the initial player position. step(dt, n) advances the simulation n ticks of dt seconds without waiting for real time, then draws once, returning nothing. setTime(t) advances the simulation to t seconds without drawing, returning nothing. seed(n) reseeds the SEEDED GENERATOR with integer n and rebuilds the map, keys, batteries, and wraiths, returning the new player x and y. getState() returns an object with fields playerX, playerY, playerHP, playerStamina, playerFear, flashlightBattery, keysCollected, wraithStates (array of 5 strings), secondsRemaining, dawn, gateUnlocked. spawnWraith(x, y, state) places a 6th wraith at the given coordinates in the given state, returning its index. setFear(value) sets player fear to value (0 to 100), returning the new fear. setBattery(value) sets flashlightBattery to value (0 to 100), returning the new battery. teleportPlayer(x, y) moves the player to the given coordinates, returning true if the tile is walkable. setKeyCollected(index, bool) sets the collected flag on key at index 0, 1, or 2, returning the new keysCollected count.

SCREENS: title (shows the game name "Before Dawn", the text "Find the three keys. Reach the gate. Do not linger.", a list of controls: WASD/arrows to move, Shift to sprint, F for flashlight, and a Start button) → game (the top-down forest view with HUD) → paused (overlay with "Paused" and Resume, Restart, and Quit buttons) → game over (if dawn or death: shows "Dawn has come" or "The forest keeps you", the number of keys collected, and a Try Again button) → victory (shows "You slipped out before the sun", the time remaining, and a Play Again button). Escape during game toggles paused; Escape during paused resumes. The HUD shows, from top-left to top-right: a small flashlight icon with the battery percentage from flashlightBattery, the keys collected as 3 small key icons (lit when collected) from keysCollected, and the dawn timer as M:SS from secondsRemaining. Bottom-left shows a heart icon with hp from player hp. Bottom-right shows a thin bar for stamina from player stamina and a thin bar for fear from player fear.

AUDIO: a low ambient drone (a 55 Hz sine with slow 0.1 Hz amplitude LFO) plays continuously during the game screen. A wraith proximity whisper (a band-passed noise burst at 800 Hz, 200 ms) plays each second when any wraith is within 150 px of the player, pitch dropping as distance decreases. A key pickup chime (a 1200 Hz triangle, 150 ms, with a 50 ms decay) plays when a key is collected. A battery pickup blip (a 600 Hz square, 80 ms) plays when a battery is collected. A wraith hit thump (a 80 Hz sine, 200 ms, sharp attack) plays on wraith contact. A gate unlock creak (a 200 Hz sawtooth with a 0.5 s downward pitch slide) plays when the third key is collected and the gate unlocks. A dawn horn (a 440 Hz sustained note, 2 s, with a 1 s fade) plays when DawnClock.dawn becomes true. A victory jingle (three ascending notes: 523, 659, 784 Hz, each 100 ms triangle) plays on victory. All are generated with the Web Audio API oscillator and noise nodes; no samples are loaded.

ART: ask for a top-down dense tree tile as a sprite, a top-down sparse tree tile as a sprite, a top-down forest path tile as a sprite, a top-down moonlit clearing tile as a sprite, a top-down dark water tile as a sprite, a small glowing ancient key as a sprite, a small green battery cell as a sprite, a wooden gate with three keyholes as a sprite, a translucent pale wraith figure as an anim with a drift animation (a slow vertical bob and trailing particles, one image showing the wraith mid-drift), and a small player character seen from above as an anim with walk, idle, and sprint frames showing one facing (the character rotates in code to face any direction; the single upward-facing anim is rotated). Everything else — the flashlight cone, the ambient glow circle, the darkness overlay, the fear vignette, the HUD icons and bars, the gate locked/unlocked state, the screen shake, the path between tiles — is drawn in code.
