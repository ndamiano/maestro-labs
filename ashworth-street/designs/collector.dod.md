Build this 2D top-down open world collector game where the player walks a single continuous tile map, gathers berries, ore, and fish, sells them to a shopkeeper, and decorates a home and a museum that earns them money from visitors. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

RECORDS: Player, ResourceNode, Fish, FurnitureItem, Shopkeeper, Building, Visitor, GameMeta.

PLAYER: position x and y in tile coordinates (starting at the home door tile, 60, 64), facing direction (north, south, east, west, or the four diagonals, starting south), inventory as a list of resource entries each with type (blueberry, strawberry, golden_berry, copper_ore, silver_ore, gold_ore, minnow, trout, pike), quantity (integer, starting 0 for every type), and for fish additionally weight in kg (floating, 0.5 to 5.0), money in coins (integer, starting at 10), a flag home_interior (boolean, starting false) and a flag museum_interior (boolean, starting false), and a flag shop_ui_open (boolean, starting false).

RESOURCE_NODE: id (integer, 1 to 70), type (berry_bush or ore_rock), sub_type (for berry_bush: blueberry, strawberry, golden_berry; for ore_rock: copper, silver, gold), position x and y in tile coordinates, current_amount (integer, 1 to 3 for berry_bush, 1 to 5 for ore_rock), max_amount (same range as current_amount at placement), regrow_timer in seconds (floating, counts down from a set value when current_amount reaches 0, then resets current_amount to max_amount and regrow_timer to 0), and a depleted boolean (true when current_amount is 0).

FISH: id (integer), sub_type (minnow, trout, pike), weight in kg (floating, minnow 0.5 to 1.5, trout 1.0 to 3.0, pike 2.0 to 5.0), a fish_spot_id it is tied to (integer, referencing a designated water tile), and a caught boolean (starting false, set true when the player catches it).

FURNITURE_ITEM: id (integer, 1 to 12 for the fixed roster), name, price in coins, category (furniture or exhibit), and a museum_value in points (furniture has 0, exhibits have a set value). The roster: Wooden Chair, 20 coins, furniture, 0; Oak Table, 50 coins, furniture, 0; Bookshelf, 40 coins, furniture, 0; Flower Pot, 15 coins, furniture, 0; Fireplace, 80 coins, furniture, 0; Crystal Lamp, 60 coins, furniture, 0; Portrait, 35 coins, furniture, 0; Grand Piano, 150 coins, furniture, 0; Aquarium, 100 coins, furniture, 0; Telescope, 90 coins, furniture, 0; Ancient Vase, 200 coins, exhibit, 50; Golden Throne, 300 coins, exhibit, 100. Additionally each FurnitureItem instance in the player's possession has an owned boolean (starting false), a placed_building (null, "home", or "museum"), and a placed_cell (row, column integer pair or null).

SHOPKEEPER: position x and y in tile coordinates (fixed at 62, 62, the tile inside the shop building), a sell_price_multiplier (floating, 1.0, meaning the player receives 100% of the item's base sell price), a buy_stock as a list of FurnitureItem ids currently available (starting with all 12), and a restock_timer in seconds (floating, starting at 0, counts up; at 120 seconds any sold-out item reappears and the timer resets to 0).

BUILDING: id (integer, 1 for home, 2 for museum, 3 for shop), name, position x and y (the top-left tile of the building footprint), width and height in tiles (home 6 by 5, museum 7 by 5, shop 4 by 3), a grid of cells (home 5 by 5, museum 6 by 4, shop 0 by 0) where each cell is either null or a FurnitureItem id, and a total_museum_value (integer, sum of museum_value of all exhibits placed, only meaningful for museum, starting 0).

VISITOR: id (integer), a timer in seconds (floating, counts down while the visitor is in the museum, starting at 20), a payout in coins (integer, calculated when the visitor spawns), and a position x and y in tile coordinates (a random empty cell inside the museum grid).

GAME_META: elapsed time in seconds (floating, starting 0), museum_total_value (integer, mirroring Building 2's total_museum_value, starting 0), milestone reached (integer, 0 to 4), a flag won (boolean, starting false), and a random seed (integer, starting 1).

SYSTEMS: WorldGenerator, Movement, Harvesting, Mining, Fishing, Trading, Decoration, Museum, ResourceRegeneration.

WORLDGENERATOR: A SEEDED GENERATOR that builds the 120 by 120 tile map. Tile types are grass, water, rock, path, and building_floor. Placement order: first fill all 14400 tiles with grass. Then place the lake: a roughly circular body of water centred at tile (85, 90) with radius 18 tiles, giving 15 designated fish_spot tiles scattered within the water (the generator picks 15 distinct water tiles at least 3 tiles from the shore). Then place the mining zone: a band of rock tiles in the rectangle from (5, 30) to (35, 70), with 30 ore_rock ResourceNodes placed on rock tiles, no two closer than 4 tiles apart, sub_type distribution 15 copper, 10 silver, 5 gold. Then place the berry grove: in the rectangle from (40, 5) to (100, 30), scatter 40 berry_bush ResourceNodes on grass tiles, no two closer than 3 tiles apart, sub_type distribution 20 blueberry, 12 strawberry, 8 golden_berry. Then place buildings: the shop (4 by 3) at tile (60, 60), the home (6 by 5) at tile (60, 66), the museum (7 by 5) at tile (52, 66); their tiles become building_floor and are non-walkable except the door tile (home door at (63, 66), museum door at (55, 66), shop door at (62, 63)). Then carve a path: a 2-tile-wide strip of path tiles connecting home door to shop door to museum door, and a longer path winding from the shop south to a tile on the lake shore and west to a tile at the edge of the mining zone. Place the Shopkeeper at (62, 62). Assign each ResourceNode its current_amount equal to max_amount (random in the type's range) and regrow_timer at 0. VERIFIER: all 40 berry_bushes and 30 ore_rocks are on non-water, non-building tiles; all 15 fish_spots are on water tiles; no two ResourceNodes of the same type overlap; the three buildings do not overlap and each has exactly one door tile reachable by path; the path from home door to each zone is walkable. Re-roll with the next seed if any fails.

MOVEMENT: The player moves at 5 tiles per second. WASD and the arrow keys are both bound: W and Up move north, S and Down move south, A and Left move west, D and Right move east, and any two-key combination moves diagonally at the same speed. On touch devices, a virtual joystick in the lower-left corner drives the same four directions. The player cannot walk onto water tiles, rock tiles, or building_floor tiles (except when entering through a door tile). The camera is centred on the player and clamps so it never shows beyond the map edges. If the player presses E while on the home door tile and home_interior is false, home_interior becomes true and the view switches to the home interior grid. If the player presses E while in the home interior (home_interior true), home_interior becomes false and the player returns to the door tile. The same rule applies to the museum door and museum_interior. If the player presses E while on the shop door tile, shop_ui_open becomes true and the trading UI appears; pressing E again or Escape closes it.

HARVESTING: The player stands on a tile adjacent (8-way) to a berry_bush ResourceNode that is not depleted and presses E. The effect: current_amount of that node decreases by 1, and one berry of the node's sub_type is added to the player's inventory (quantity of that type increases by 1). If current_amount reaches 0, the node becomes depleted and regrow_timer starts at its set value (blueberry 60 seconds, strawberry 120 seconds, golden_berry 240 seconds). Harvesting a berry takes no time; it is instant on the keypress.

MINING: The player stands on a tile adjacent (8-way) to an ore_rock ResourceNode that is not depleted and holds E for 2 continuous seconds. A progress bar fills over those 2 seconds. If the player releases E before 2 seconds, the progress resets to 0. Upon completing 2 seconds: current_amount decreases by 1, one ore of the node's sub_type is added to the player's inventory, and a small rock-chip particle anim plays. If current_amount reaches 0, the node becomes depleted and regrow_timer starts at its set value (copper 120 seconds, silver 240 seconds, gold 480 seconds).

FISHING: The player stands on a fish_spot tile (a designated water tile) that has an uncaught Fish assigned to it and presses E. A fishing UI appears: a horizontal bar 200 pixels wide with a marker that oscillates left to right at 1.5 cycles per second. The target zone is a green band whose width depends on sub_type: minnow 60 pixels, trout 40 pixels, pike 25 pixels, and its position on the bar is randomised each cast. The player presses E (or Space) to stop the marker. If the marker is inside the green band, the Fish's caught flag becomes true, the fish is added to the player's inventory with its sub_type and weight, and the fish spot is empty. If the marker is outside the band, the Fish remains uncaught, the spot stays available, and the player can try again immediately. A new Fish spawns at an empty fish spot every 45 seconds (the ResourceRegeneration system handles this), with sub_type distribution 60% minnow, 30% trout, 10% pike, and weight random within the sub_type range.

TRADING: The shop UI has two panels. The sell panel lists every resource type in the player's inventory with quantity greater than 0, each showing its base sell price (blueberry 2 coins, strawberry 5 coins, golden_berry 15 coins, copper_ore 3 coins, silver_ore 8 coins, gold_ore 20 coins, minnow 3 coins, trout 7 coins, pike 15 coins). The player selects a type and presses Sell; the player's money increases by quantity times base sell price times Shopkeeper.sell_price_multiplier, and the inventory quantity for that type becomes 0. The buy panel lists every FurnitureItem in Shopkeeper.buy_stock. The player selects an item; if the player's money is at least the item's price, money decreases by the price, the FurnitureItem's owned flag becomes true, and its id is removed from buy_stock. If money is insufficient, nothing happens and a "not enough coins" text flashes. The Shopkeeper.restock_timer counts up each second; at 120 seconds, any FurnitureItem not in buy_stock is re-added and the timer resets to 0.

DECORATION: While in the home interior (home_interior true), the player sees a 5 by 5 grid. They press E on a cell to open a placement menu listing every FurnitureItem with owned true and placed_building null or "home". Selecting an item places it: the cell in the home grid becomes that FurnitureItem id, the item's placed_building becomes "home", and placed_cell becomes the row and column. The player can select an already-placed item in the home grid and press X to remove it (placed_building becomes null, placed_cell becomes null, the cell clears). The same mechanic applies in the museum interior with a 6 by 4 grid, using only FurnitureItems whose category is exhibit. The museum interior also shows the Building's total_museum_value updating in real time as exhibits are placed or removed.

MUSEUM: Every 60 seconds of game time, the Museum system checks Building 2's total_museum_value. If total_museum_value is at least 50, it spawns a Visitor: the Visitor's payout is calculated as total_museum_value times 0.1 (floored to integer coins), the Visitor's timer is set to 20 seconds, and its position is a random empty cell in the museum grid. At most 5 Visitors can be present at once; if 5 are already in the museum, no new one spawns. Each second, every Visitor's timer decreases by 1. When a Visitor's timer reaches 0, the player's money increases by the Visitor's payout, the Visitor is removed, and a small "chime" sound plays. If total_museum_value drops below 50 (because the player removed exhibits), no new Visitors spawn but existing ones finish their timer normally.

RESOURCEREGENERATION: Every second, for each depleted ResourceNode, regrow_timer decreases by 1. When regrow_timer reaches 0, current_amount resets to max_amount and the node is no longer depleted. For fish spots: every 45 seconds, each fish_spot that has no uncaught Fish gets a new Fish assigned (sub_type by the 60/30/10 roll, weight random in range, caught false). The Shopkeeper.restock_timer also advances in this system.

CORE LOOP: The player walks from the home to a zone, collects resources using Harvesting, Mining, or Fishing, walks back to the shop and uses Trading to sell their haul for coins, then uses Trading to buy furniture or exhibits. They enter the home interior or museum interior via Decoration to place what they bought. Placing exhibits in the museum raises total_museum_value, which makes the Museum system spawn Visitors that pay out coins every 60 seconds. More coins buy better exhibits, which spawn more and richer Visitors. The progression is: the player starts with 10 coins and an empty museum, earns from selling gathered resources, buys their first cheap exhibit, watches Visitors trickle in, and climbs toward milestones. At total_museum_value 50 the first Visitors appear. At 200, Shopkeeper.sell_price_multiplier rises to 1.25. At 500, Visitor payout doubles (the multiplier on the 0.1 factor becomes 0.2). At 1000, the GameMeta.won flag becomes true and a "World Wonder" banner appears, offering the player to keep playing freely.

DEBUG API: start() enters the game from the title screen and returns the initial state. step(dt, n) advances the simulation n ticks of dt seconds each, updating all timers, movement, and systems, then renders one frame, returning the elapsed time. setTime(t) fast-forwards the simulation to exactly t seconds of game time without rendering, returning the new elapsed time. seed(n) sets the random seed to n and regenerates the entire world, returning the new seed. getState() returns a plain object with fields: player_x, player_y, player_money, player_inventory (an object mapping each resource type to its quantity), player_home_interior, player_museum_interior, nodes (an array of each ResourceNode's id, type, sub_type, current_amount, depleted, regrow_timer), fish (an array of each Fish's id, sub_type, weight, caught), building_grids (an object with "home" and "museum" keys, each a 2D array of FurnitureItem ids or null), museum_total_value, visitor_count, visitor_payouts (array of current visitor payouts), shop_stock (array of FurnitureItem ids still for sale), elapsed_time, milestone, won. spawnNode(type, sub_type, x, y) places a ResourceNode at tile (x, y) with the given type and sub_type and returns its id. spawnFish(sub_type, fish_spot_id) assigns a new Fish of the given sub_type to the given fish spot and returns its id. setMoney(n) sets the player's money to n and returns n. setMuseumValue(n) directly sets the museum's total_museum_value to n (adjusting a hidden accumulator) and returns n. placeItem(item_id, building, row, col) places the given owned FurnitureItem at the given cell in the given building and returns true on success. skipToMilestone(n) sets the GameMeta.milestone to n and applies the corresponding sell_price_multiplier and visitor payout changes, returning the milestone.

SCREENS: title (shows the game name "Gather & Adorn", a "Press Enter to Start" prompt, and a control list: WASD/arrows to move, E to interact, X to remove, Esc to close menus) → game (the main open-world view with HUD) → home_interior (5 by 5 grid view with placement menu, accessed by E at the home door) → museum_interior (6 by 4 grid view with exhibit placement menu, accessed by E at the museum door) → shop_ui (sell and buy panels overlaying the world view, accessed by E at the shop door) → game over / world_wonder (a banner reads "World Wonder Achieved" with a "Keep Playing" button that dismisses it and returns to game). Escape in any interior or UI view returns the player to the game view at the relevant door tile. The HUD in the game view shows: player money (from Player.money) in the top-left, the current resource count summary in the top-right (e.g. "Berries: 12, Ore: 5, Fish: 3"), a small minimap in the bottom-left showing the 120 by 120 map with the player dot and building outlines, and the museum_total_value in the bottom-right (from GameMeta.museum_total_value). In interior views the HUD shows the placement menu and the building's current grid.

AUDIO: All sounds are generated with the Web Audio API. A soft "pop" (short sine burst at 600 Hz, 50 ms) plays on each berry harvest. A low "crack" (noise burst with a 200 Hz lowpass, 100 ms) plays on each successful ore mine. A "plop" (sine at 300 Hz dropping to 100 Hz over 150 ms) plays when a fish is caught. A "coin" sound (two quick sine blips at 800 Hz and 1200 Hz, 80 ms total) plays on each sell transaction. A "ding" (sine at 1000 Hz, 200 ms with decay) plays when a Visitor leaves and pays out. A "click" (short 500 Hz square, 30 ms) plays on every UI selection. A "place" sound (sine at 400 Hz, 100 ms) plays when furniture is placed in a grid. Ambient: a low looping pad (two detuned sawtooth oscillators at 110 Hz and 112 Hz through a lowpass at 400 Hz, very quiet) plays continuously during the game view only.

ART: Ask for a top-down grass tile as a tile, a top-down water tile with gentle ripple detail as a tile, a top-down rocky ground tile as a tile, a top-down dirt path tile as a tile, and a top-down building_floor tile as a tile. Ask for a berry bush with blueberries as a sprite, a berry bush with strawberries as a sprite, a berry bush with golden glowing berries as a sprite, a small copper ore rock as a sprite, a small silver ore rock as a sprite, and a small gold ore rock as a sprite. Ask for a top-down player character as an anim with walk (four-frame cycle) and idle (two-frame breathing cycle), shown facing four ways (north, south, east, west). Ask for a shopkeeper character standing behind a counter as a sprite. Ask for a fishing rod with a line and a small bobber as a sprite. Ask for each of the twelve FurnitureItems as a sprite: wooden chair, oak table, bookshelf, flower pot, fireplace, crystal lamp, portrait, grand piano, aquarium, telescope, ancient vase, golden throne. Ask for a small museum visitor character as a sprite. Ask for a title screen background scene as a scene showing a cross-section of the open world with the home, museum, lake, and berry grove visible in a painterly style. Everything else — the grid lines in interior views, the fishing mini-game bar, the HUD panels, the minimap, the progress bar for mining, the "not enough coins" flash text, the World Wonder banner, particles for rock chips, and all UI buttons — is drawn in code.

DONE: Open world the player walks around in — after start() and step(0.016, 60), getState() shows player_x and player_y changed from the starting position, and the 120 by 120 tile map is rendered with grass, water, rock, path, and building tiles visible. Harvest berries — the player stands adjacent to a berry_bush and presses E; after the press, the node's current_amount is 1 less and the player's inventory count for that berry type is 1 more. Mine ores — the player holds E for 2 seconds next to an ore_rock; on completion the ore count in inventory increases by 1 and the rock's current_amount decreases. Fish fish — the player stands on a fish_spot tile and completes the oscillating-bar mini-game with the marker in the green zone; the fish appears in inventory with a sub_type and weight. Sell to a shopkeeper for each type — the shop UI lists all nine resource types with distinct prices; selling blueberries adds 2 coins per berry, selling gold ore adds 20 coins per ore, and the inventory quantities reset to 0 after selling. Buy furniture to decorate their home — the player buys a Wooden Chair from the shop, enters the home interior, selects the chair, and places it on a grid cell; the cell shows the chair sprite and the chair's placed_building is "home". A small museum-type building — the museum is a separate building on the map with a 6 by 4 grid interior, accessible through its own door; placing an Ancient Vase inside raises the museum_total_value to at least 50, and after 60 seconds a Visitor appears and, after 20 more seconds, pays out coins to the player.
