Build this arcade F1 racing game where the player drives a Formula 1 car around a seeded circuit against six AI rivals over five laps, managing tires, fuel, pit stops, and rain to finish first. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.
SYSTEMS: GAME STATE, TRACK GENERATOR, CAR RECORD, PLAYER CAR, AI RIVALS, PHYSICS, CAMERA, LAPS AND WIN, TIRE AND FUEL, PIT STOP, WEATHER, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART
GAME STATE: The GAME STATE record has fields screen, raceId, seed, lap, totalLaps, raceTime, raceState, position, playerCarId, rivalIds, weatherState, pitTimer, result, unlockedRaces. It is source of truth for screen and race progress. Initial: screen title, raceId 1, seed 11, lap 1, totalLaps 5, raceTime 0, raceState waiting, position 1, playerCarId P1, rivalIds R1 through R6, weatherState dry, pitTimer 0, result none, unlockedRaces [1]. A race is won when position is 1 and lap exceeds totalLaps.
TRACK GENERATOR: The TRACK GENERATOR makes a TRACK record with fields raceId, seed, centerline, width, startLine, checkpoints, pitEntry, pitExit, pitLength, rainPatches, bounds. The RACE record has fields raceId, seed, targetLength, turnCount, width, rainChance. RACE values are: raceId 1 seed 11, targetLength 3200, turnCount 14, width 18, rainChance 0; raceId 2 seed 27, targetLength 4100, turnCount 18, width 16, rainChance 1; raceId 3 seed 43, targetLength 4900, turnCount 22, width 15, rainChance 1. TRACK is source of truth for geometry, read by PLAYER CAR, AI RIVALS, PHYSICS, CAMERA, LAPS AND WIN, PIT STOP, and WEATHER. It places start at x 0 y 0, 14 to 22 centerline points, segments no shorter than 120, corner radius no smaller than 90, 6 checkpoints, pit entry 300 after start, pit exit 700 after start, pitLength 160, and 0 to 8 rain patches of size 90 when rainChance is 1. The verifier checks closed loop, no overlap, no self-intersection, checkpoints reachable, turn count at least 10, and pit on loop; re-roll with next seed if any fails.
CAR RECORD: The CAR RECORD is the source of truth for car stats and is read by PLAYER CAR, AI RIVALS, PHYSICS, LAPS AND WIN, TIRE AND FUEL, and PIT STOP. It has fields carId, topSpeed, acceleration, cornering, tireWearRate, fuelUseRate, tireCompound, tireWear, fuel, damage, health, x, y, heading, speed, lap, checkpointIndex, pitTimer, finished, finishTime, rank. All cars start with x 0, y 0, heading 0, speed 0, lap 1, checkpointIndex 0, pitTimer 0, finished false, finishTime 0, rank 0, tireCompound soft, tireWear 0, fuel 100, damage 0, health 100, tireWearRate 1, fuelUseRate 1. P1 topSpeed 240 acceleration 42 cornering 78. R1 topSpeed 236 acceleration 40 cornering 82 y -10. R2 topSpeed 242 acceleration 44 cornering 72 tireWearRate 1.1 y -20. R3 topSpeed 234 acceleration 38 cornering 86 tireWearRate 0.9 y -30. R4 topSpeed 238 acceleration 41 cornering 76 tireWearRate 1.05 fuelUseRate 1.05 y -40. R5 topSpeed 244 acceleration 45 cornering 70 tireWearRate 1.15 y -50. R6 topSpeed 232 acceleration 37 cornering 88 tireWearRate 0.85 y -60.
PLAYER CAR: The PLAYER CAR system reads CAR RECORD for P1 and GAME STATE. It moves P1 with W, A, S, D and arrow Up, Down, Left, Right both bound; W/Up set throttle 1, S/Down set brake 1, A/Left set steering -1, D/Right set steering 1. Touch shows left steering joystick and right throttle brake pedal. P1 pits with P or pit button when inside pit box. P1 has size 44 by 18 and collision radius 10.
AI RIVALS: The AI RIVALS system reads CAR RECORD and TRACK. Each rival has an AI record with fields carId, aggression, lineOffset. R1 aggression 0.7 lineOffset 0. R2 aggression 0.9 lineOffset 6. R3 aggression 0.5 lineOffset -6. R4 aggression 0.8 lineOffset 3. R5 aggression 1.0 lineOffset -4. R6 aggression 0.4 lineOffset 0. Each rival follows centerline plus lineOffset, brakes when a corner is within 160, overtakes when aggression is at least 0.8 and car ahead is within 30, and pits when tireWear is at least 0.7 or fuel is at most 20. Contact applies 8 damage to player and 4 to rival and reduces speeds by 6.
PHYSICS: The PHYSICS system reads CAR RECORD, TRACK, and WEATHER and writes position, speed, heading, damage, and health. It applies acceleration 42 times throttle, brake 60 times brake, drag 0.02 times speed squared, and steering 2.2 degrees times steering times speed divided by 100. It caps speed by topSpeed and multiplies grip by 0.75 in rain. A hit is a collision when two collision radii overlap; it applies 10 damage, reduces speeds by 20, and pushes cars apart by 4. If health is 0, car becomes finished, speed becomes 0, and cannot steer.
CAMERA: The CAMERA record has fields x, y, zoom, shake, targetCarId. It reads CAR RECORD and GAME STATE. In race, targetCarId is P1, x is car x, y is car y minus 120, zoom is 0.9. Damage sets shake 8 for 0.2 seconds. In title, x 0, y 0, zoom 1. In results, x is winner x, y is winner y, zoom is 0.7. Camera moves 0.2 toward target each frame.
LAPS AND WIN: The LAPS AND WIN system reads CAR RECORD, TRACK, and GAME STATE. A checkpoint counts when a car is within 25 of it and heading is within 45 degrees. Passing checkpoint 5 completes the lap, increases lap by 1, and if lap exceeds totalLaps sets finished true, finishTime to raceTime, and rank to finished cars plus 1. Position is recalculated every 0.5 seconds by sorting unfinished cars by lap, checkpointIndex, and centerline distance, then finished cars by finishTime. Win is player rank 1 at finish; podium is rank 1, 2, or 3. If player finishes rank 1 or 2, next race unlocks.
TIRE AND FUEL: The TIRE AND FUEL system reads CAR RECORD and TIRE records and writes tireWear and fuel. The TIRE record has fields compound, grip, wearRate. Soft grip 1.15 wearRate 1.4. Medium grip 1.0 wearRate 1.0. Hard grip 0.85 wearRate 0.7. tireWear increases by 0.0008 times speed divided by 100 times tireWearRate and wearRate, and fuel decreases by 0.015 times speed divided by 100 times fuelUseRate. When tireWear is at least 1, grip becomes 0.55 and topSpeed becomes 200. When fuel is 0, acceleration becomes 0 and topSpeed becomes 120.
PIT STOP: The PIT STOP record has fields carId, state, timer, chosenCompound, cost. It reads TRACK pitEntry, pitExit, pitLength, and CAR RECORD. Entering the pit box sets state pitting, timer 3.5, speed 30, cost 0. When timer reaches 0, state becomes done, tireWear becomes 0, fuel becomes 100, chosenCompound becomes selected compound, and car leaves through pitExit. The player chooses soft with 1, medium with 2, and hard with 3 in the pit box. Leaving early takes 10 damage and sets speed to 40.
WEATHER: The WEATHER record has fields state, rainAmount, lightningTimer. It reads TRACK rainPatches. Dry has rainAmount 0 and grip 1. Rain has rainAmount 1, grip 0.75, and active rainPatches. Rain starts at raceTime 45 in races with rainChance 1, lasts 90 seconds, then state becomes dry. Rain patches add grip 0.6 and speed loss 5 per second.
SCREENS: The screen state machine is title (shows game name, race select, controls) → countdown (shows 3, 2, 1, Go) → race (shows track, cars, lap, position, speed, tire, fuel, weather) → results (shows rank, time, podium, buttons) → title. Enter starts race, Left and Right choose races, P pits, 1, 2, and 3 choose tires, R restarts, Escape pauses and resumes in race, and returns to title.
AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. Engine is a sawtooth oscillator at 60 to 300 hertz during race. Hit is a square wave at 120 hertz 0.15 seconds on damage. Pit is a sine wave at 440 hertz 0.3 seconds on pit start. Countdown is sine at 660 hertz 0.1 seconds on 3, 2, 1 and 880 hertz 0.4 seconds on Go. Victory is sine notes at 523, 659, and 784 hertz 0.25 seconds each on win.
ART: Ask for an F1 car sprite as a top-down sprite, 1 image; ask for a rain overlay tile as a repeating tile, 1 image. Everything else — track, centerline, curbs, checkpoints, start line, pit box, rain patches, HUD, minimap, shadows, smoke, tire icons, fuel bar, weather icon, and podium — is drawn in code.
