SEEDED WORLD GENERATOR: The generator uses WORLD.seed 12345 to create all structured content in this order: tiles, roads, NPCs, chests, enemies, quests, and shop stock. It fills TILE x 0-159 and y 0-119 with type 0 grass, then for each tile rolls a seeded value and assigns type 1 forest with probability 0.15, type 2 water with probability 0.10, type 3 mountain with probability 0.05, type 4 road with probability 0.05, type 5 snow with probability 0.05, otherwise type 0. It sets the border one tile wide to type 3. It places PLAYER at tile 80,60, sets PLAYER.x to 80*32+16 px and PLAYER.y to 60*32+16 px, and forces a 10 by 10 area around that tile to type 0. It chooses three merchant tiles, one final marker tile, and five shard marker tiles with seeded random coordinates at least 60 tiles from the player start, at least 30 tiles from each other, and on passable tiles. It carves a type 4 road path from the start tile to each merchant tile, final marker tile, and shard marker tile by taking seeded random steps of one tile north, south, east, or west, preferring steps that reduce distance to the target, and marking every stepped tile type 4. It places six NPC records: NPC 1-3 type 0 on the three merchant tiles, NPC 4-6 type 1 on road tiles 20 tiles from the start. It creates twelve CHEST records on passable tiles at least 15 tiles from start and not on road tiles, each with opened 0, quantity 1, and itemId chosen seeded as 6 with probability 0.4, 7 with probability 0.2, 8 with probability 0.2, 1 with probability 0.1, 2 with probability 0.1, and gold chosen seeded from 0 to 100. It creates 44 ENEMY records: 10 type 1, 15 type 2, 10 type 3, 5 type 4, 3 type 5, and 1 type 6, each copied from its type roster, placed on passable tiles not on the same tile as PLAYER, NPC, CHEST, another ENEMY, or a quest marker, with the type 6 enemy within 20 tiles of the final marker tile. It creates twelve QUEST records: QUEST 1-5 type 0 shard state 1 targetType 0 targetCount 1 rewardGold 50 rewardXp 100 at the shard marker tiles; QUEST 6-9 type 3 side state 1 targetType 1,2,3,4 respectively targetCount 3,3,3,1 rewardGold 75,75,75,100 rewardXp 150,150,150,200 marker at start tile; QUEST 10-11 type 1 boss state 1 targetType 4,5 targetCount 1 rewardGold 150,250 rewardXp 300,500 marker at start tile; QUEST 12 type 2 final state 0 targetType 6 targetCount 1 rewardGold 1000 rewardXp 1000 at the final marker tile. For each merchant NPC 1-3, it creates six SHOP_STOCK rows: itemId 6 quantity 3 buyPrice 20 sellPrice 10; itemId 7 quantity 3 buyPrice 20 sellPrice 10; itemId 8 quantity 3 buyPrice 20 sellPrice 10; itemId 1 quantity 1 buyPrice 40 sellPrice 25; itemId 4 quantity 1 buyPrice 60 sellPrice 40; and one seeded choice among itemId 2 buyPrice 90 sellPrice 60, itemId 3 buyPrice 120 sellPrice 70, or itemId 5 buyPrice 30 sellPrice 20. The verifier checks that the PLAYER start tile is type 0 and passable; that every NPC, CHEST, and QUEST marker tile is reachable from the start through passable tiles; that carved road paths do not cross themselves; that no two of PLAYER, NPC, CHEST, ENEMY, or QUEST marker share the same tile; that there are at least 200 type 0 tiles, 40 type 1 tiles, 30 type 2 tiles, and 30 type 4 tiles; that there are 44 ENEMY records, 12 CHEST records, 6 NPC records, 12 QUEST records, and 18 SHOP_STOCK records; and if any check fails, re-roll with the next seed.

INPUT: While WORLD.screen is 1, keyboard W or Up sets PLAYER.moveY to -1, S or Down sets PLAYER.moveY to 1, A or Left sets PLAYER.moveX to -1, D or Right sets PLAYER.moveX to 1, and releasing all movement keys sets both to 0; if two axes are both nonzero, the movement system normalizes them. Mouse position sets PLAYER.facing to the angle from PLAYER.x,PLAYER.y to the cursor in radians. Left mouse button or touch tap on the right half of the screen sets PLAYER.attackPressed to 1 for one frame. Right mouse button, double touch tap on the right half, or key J sets PLAYER.spellPressed to 1 for one frame. Holding Shift or key K sets PLAYER.sprintHeld to 1. Key E or touch tap on a visible NPC or CHEST sets PLAYER.interactPressed to 1. Key I opens inventory by setting WORLD.screen to 3 if it is 1, and sets it back to 1 if it is 3. Key F sets PLAYER.usePressed to 1. Key Q sets PLAYER.dropPressed to 1. Key R on game over or win sets WORLD.seed to WORLD.seed + 1 and WORLD.screen to 0. On touch, dragging in the left half of the screen sets PLAYER.moveX and PLAYER.moveY to the joystick vector divided by 50 px, clamped to -1 to 1, and a held second finger on the right half sets PLAYER.sprintHeld to 1. After any input system rule sets a pressed field to 1, that field is reset to 0 at the end of the frame.

MOVEMENT: Each frame with WORLD.screen 1, decrement PLAYER.attackCooldown, PLAYER.spellCooldown, and PLAYER.inCombat by the frame time, never below 0. If PLAYER.moveX or PLAYER.moveY is nonzero, normalize the vector. If PLAYER.sprintHeld is 1 and PLAYER.stamina is greater than 0, use PLAYER.sprintSpeed and subtract 20 from PLAYER.stamina per second; otherwise use PLAYER.speed. Multiply the chosen speed by the current TILE moveSpeedMultiplier and by 0.9 if WORLD.weather is 1 rain, 0.85 if WORLD.weather is 2 snow, or 1.0 if WORLD.weather is 0 or 3. Move PLAYER.x and PLAYER.y by the vector times speed times frame time, but if the destination tile is type 3 mountain, do not move. If PLAYER.stamina is not being drained, add 30 to PLAYER.stamina per second up to PLAYER.maxStamina. Add 10 to PLAYER.magicka per second up to PLAYER.maxMagicka. If PLAYER.inCombat is 0, add 2 to PLAYER.health per second up to PLAYER.maxHealth. If PLAYER.stamina is 0, set PLAYER.sprintHeld to 0.

COMBAT: If PLAYER.attackPressed is 1 and PLAYER.attackCooldown is 0 and PLAYER.equippedWeaponId is not 0 and PLAYER.stamina is at least 8, set PLAYER.attackCooldown to the equipped ITEM cooldown, subtract 8 from PLAYER.stamina, set PLAYER.inCombat to 5, and add 5 to the matching skill xp field: oneHandedXp for ITEM type 0, twoHandedXp for type 1, archeryXp for type 2. If the equipped ITEM type is 0 or 1, damage every ENEMY whose distance from PLAYER is less than or equal to the ITEM range and whose angle from PLAYER.facing is within 1.0 rad by the ITEM damage plus the matching skill level minus 1, set each damaged ENEMY.state to 1, and if an ENEMY.health becomes 0, apply the enemy death rule. If the equipped ITEM type is 2, create a PROJECTILE at PLAYER.x,PLAYER.y with vx and vy equal to PLAYER.facing direction times ITEM projectileSpeed, speed ITEM projectileSpeed, damage ITEM damage plus archery minus 1, radius 12, range ITEM projectileRange, traveled 0, owner 0, type 2. Each frame, move every PROJECTILE by vx,vy times frame time, add speed times frame time to traveled, and delete the PROJECTILE when traveled is at least range or its tile is type 3. If a PROJECTILE with owner 0 overlaps an ENEMY within the PROJECTILE radius, subtract the PROJECTILE damage from ENEMY.health, set ENEMY.state to 1, set ENEMY.slowTimer to the PROJECTILE slowSeconds and ENEMY.slowFactor to the PROJECTILE slowFactor when slowSeconds is greater than 0, and delete the PROJECTILE. If a PROJECTILE with owner 1 overlaps PLAYER within the PROJECTILE radius, subtract the PROJECTILE damage from PLAYER.health, set PLAYER.inCombat to 5, and delete the PROJECTILE. The enemy death rule is: when an ENEMY.health is 0 and ENEMY.deathTimer is 0, set ENEMY.deathTimer to 1, add ENEMY.xpValue to PLAYER.experience, add a seeded random integer between ENEMY.goldMin and ENEMY.goldMax to PLAYER.gold, and if a seeded roll is less than ENEMY.dropChance, create a DROP at the ENEMY position with ENEMY.dropItemId and quantity 1.

ENEMY AI: Each frame for every ENEMY with health greater than 0 and deathTimer 0, decrement ENEMY.attackTimer and ENEMY.slowTimer by frame time, never below 0. If ENEMY.slowTimer is greater than 0, set ENEMY.speed to ENEMY.baseSpeed times ENEMY.slowFactor; otherwise set ENEMY.speed to ENEMY.baseSpeed. If PLAYER.health is 0, set ENEMY.state to 0. If ENEMY.fleeThreshold is greater than 0 and ENEMY.health is less than ENEMY.fleeThreshold, set ENEMY.state to 3. If ENEMY.state is 0 and the distance from ENEMY to PLAYER is less than or equal to ENEMY.aggroRadius, set ENEMY.state to 1; if WORLD.weather is 3 fog, use 0.75 times ENEMY.aggroRadius in that check; if night is active, use 1.2 times ENEMY.aggroRadius. When one ENEMY changes from state 0 to state 1, set every ENEMY of the same type within 160 px to state 1. If ENEMY.state is 1, set ENEMY.facing to the angle toward PLAYER; if the distance is greater than ENEMY.attackRange, move ENEMY toward PLAYER by ENEMY.speed times frame time, but do not enter a type 3 tile; if the straight-line destination tile is type 3, choose a seeded random adjacent passable tile within 64 px and move toward that tile instead; if the distance is less than or equal to ENEMY.attackRange, set ENEMY.state to 2. If ENEMY.state is 2 and the distance is greater than ENEMY.attackRange, set ENEMY.state to 1. If ENEMY.state is 2 and ENEMY.attackTimer is 0, set ENEMY.attackTimer to ENEMY.attackCooldown and subtract from PLAYER.health the value of ENEMY.attackDamage minus the equipped armor ITEM armor, with a minimum of 1, set PLAYER.inCombat to 5, and if PLAYER.health becomes 0, set PLAYER.health to 0. If ENEMY.state is 3, set ENEMY.facing to the angle away from PLAYER and move ENEMY away from PLAYER by ENEMY.speed times frame time, but do not enter a type 3 tile; if the distance from ENEMY to PLAYER is greater than 440 px, set ENEMY.state to 0. For the type 6 frost dragon only, if the distance from ENEMY to PLAYER is greater than 200 px and ENEMY.attackTimer is 0, set ENEMY.attackTimer to 2.0 and create a PROJECTILE at ENEMY.x,ENEMY.y toward PLAYER with speed 300 px/s, damage 18, radius 24, range 360 px, traveled 0, owner 1, type 0, slowSeconds 0, slowFactor 0. For every pair of ENEMYs within 32 px, push each apart by 4 px per frame along the line between them, but do not push into type 3 tiles.

MAGIC: If PLAYER.spellPressed is 1 and PLAYER.spellCooldown is 0 and PLAYER.equippedSpellId is not 0 and PLAYER.magicka is at least the equipped SPELL magickaCost, set PLAYER.spellCooldown to the SPELL cooldown, subtract magickaCost from PLAYER.magicka, set PLAYER.inCombat to 5, and add 5 to destructionXp for SPELL type 0 or restorationXp for type 1. If the SPELL type is 1, add SPELL heal plus restoration minus 1 to PLAYER.health, capped at PLAYER.maxHealth. If the SPELL type is 0, create a PROJECTILE at PLAYER.x,PLAYER.y toward PLAYER.facing with speed SPELL projectileSpeed, damage SPELL damage plus destruction minus 1, radius SPELL radius, range SPELL projectileRange, traveled 0, owner 0, type 0 for spell id 1 and type 1 for spell id 2, slowSeconds SPELL slowSeconds, slowFactor SPELL slowFactor. If PLAYER.magicka is less than the cost, set PLAYER.spellCooldown to 0.5.

INVENTORY: When PLAYER.interactPressed is 1 and a DROP is within 40 px of PLAYER, pick up the DROP if an INVENTORY_SLOT with the same itemId and quantity less than 10 exists, increasing that quantity by DROP.quantity, or if an empty slot exists, setting that slot's itemId to DROP.itemId and quantity to DROP.quantity; delete the DROP. If no slot is available, do nothing. When PLAYER.interactPressed is 1 and a CHEST within 40 px has opened 0, set CHEST.opened to 1, add CHEST.gold to PLAYER.gold, and if CHEST.itemId is not 0, add it to inventory using the same stacking and empty-slot rules, leaving the CHEST open if no space is available. When PLAYER.usePressed is 1, use INVENTORY_SLOT selectedSlot: if itemId is 6, add ITEM healthRestore to PLAYER.health capped at PLAYER.maxHealth; if itemId is 7, add ITEM staminaRestore to PLAYER.stamina capped at PLAYER.maxStamina; if itemId is 8, add ITEM magickaRestore to PLAYER.magicka capped at PLAYER.maxMagicka; for itemId 6,7,8, subtract 1 from quantity and set itemId to 0 when quantity is 0; if itemId is 1,2,3, set PLAYER.equippedWeaponId to itemId; if itemId is 4 or 5, set PLAYER.equippedArmorId to itemId. When PLAYER.dropPressed is 1 and INVENTORY_SLOT selectedSlot quantity is greater than 0, create a DROP at PLAYER.x,PLAYER.y with that itemId and quantity 1, subtract 1 from the slot quantity, and set itemId to 0 when quantity is 0. When WORLD.screen is 3, key A or Left decreases selectedSlot by 1 and key D or Right increases it by 1, wrapping from 0 to 19.

SHOP: When PLAYER.interactPressed is 1 and an NPC type 0 is within 60 px of PLAYER, set WORLD.screen to 4 and WORLD.shopNpcId to that NPC.id. When WORLD.screen is 4, pressing A or Up cycles WORLD.selectedStock upward among the six SHOP_STOCK rows whose npcId equals WORLD.shopNpcId, and pressing D or Down cycles it downward. Pressing F on a selected SHOP_STOCK row with quantity greater than 0 buys one item if PLAYER.gold is at least buyPrice and an inventory slot is available, subtracts buyPrice from PLAYER.gold, subtracts 1 from SHOP_STOCK.quantity, and adds the itemId to inventory. Pressing Q on a selected SHOP_STOCK row sells one item if INVENTORY_SLOT selectedSlot quantity is greater than 0, the itemId matches the selected SHOP_STOCK.itemId, and the SHOP_STOCK quantity is at most 9; it adds sellPrice to PLAYER.gold, adds 1 to SHOP_STOCK.quantity, and subtracts 1 from INVENTORY_SLOT.selectedSlot.quantity. When WORLD.timeOfDay wraps to 0, set SHOP_STOCK.quantity to 3 for itemId 6,7,8 and to 1 for itemId 1,2,3,4,5. Pressing Escape or I closes the shop by setting WORLD.screen to 1.

QUEST: When PLAYER is within 40 px of a QUEST with type 0, state 1, and targetCount 1, set QUEST.state to 2, add QUEST.rewardGold to PLAYER.gold, add QUEST.rewardXp to PLAYER.experience, and add 1 to WORLD.shardsCollected. When WORLD.shardsCollected is 5 and WORLD.finalUnlocked is 0, set WORLD.finalUnlocked to 1 and set the type 2 QUEST.state to 1. When PLAYER is within 40 px of the type 2 QUEST marker and WORLD.finalUnlocked is 1, set that QUEST.state to 1 if it is still 0. When an ENEMY dies, for every QUEST with state 1 and targetType equal to ENEMY.type, add 1 to QUEST.progress; if QUEST.targetType is 6 and QUEST.type is 2, this rule only applies when WORLD.finalUnlocked is 1; if QUEST.progress is at least QUEST.targetCount, set QUEST.state to 2, add QUEST.rewardGold to PLAYER.gold, add QUEST.rewardXp to PLAYER.experience, and if QUEST.type is 2, set WORLD.gameOver to 1.

LEVELING: While PLAYER.level is less than 10 and PLAYER.experience is at least the threshold for the next level, where thresholds are 100 from level 1 to 2, 250 from 2 to 3, 450 from 3 to 4, 700 from 4 to 5, 1000 from 5 to 6, 1400 from 6 to 7, 1900 from 7 to 8, 2500 from 8 to 9, and 3200 from 9 to 10, add 1 to PLAYER.level, subtract the threshold from PLAYER.experience, add 10 to PLAYER.maxHealth, add 5 to PLAYER.maxStamina, add 5 to PLAYER.maxMagicka, and set PLAYER.health, PLAYER.stamina, and PLAYER.magicka to their new maximums. For each skill field oneHanded, twoHanded, archery, destruction, and restoration, while its level is less than 10 and its xp field is at least the threshold for the next skill level, where thresholds are 50,100,150,200,250,300,350,400, and 450 for levels 1 through 9, add 1 to the skill level and subtract the threshold from the xp field. Skill levels grant damage bonuses in COMBAT and MAGIC: oneHanded adds oneHanded minus 1 to type 0 ITEM damage, twoHanded adds twoHanded minus 1 to type 1 ITEM damage, archery adds archery minus 1 to type 2 ITEM damage, destruction adds destruction minus 1 to SPELL damage, and restoration adds restoration minus 1 to SPELL heal.

WEATHER: Each frame subtract frame time from WORLD.weatherTimer. When WORLD.weatherTimer is 0 or less, choose WORLD.weather seeded as 0 clear with probability 0.4, 1 rain with probability 0.25, 2 snow with probability 0.2, or 3 fog with probability 0.15, and set WORLD.weatherTimer to 90 s. Add WORLD.timeOfDay by frame time and wrap it to 0 when it reaches WORLD.dayLength. If WORLD.timeOfDay is between 270 s and 360 s or between 0 s and 90 s, night is active and ENEMY aggroRadius is used at 1.2 times its value in ENEMY AI perception.

DEATH: When PLAYER.health is 0 and WORLD.gameOver is 0, set PLAYER.deathTimer to 3 and, after 3 s, set WORLD.screen to 5. When WORLD.gameOver is 1, set WORLD.screen to 6. On screen 5 or 6, pressing R sets WORLD.seed to WORLD.seed + 1, resets all records, and sets WORLD.screen to 0.

CORE LOOP: The player repeatedly explores with MOVEMENT, triggers SEEDED WORLD GENERATOR content by finding NPCs, CHEST, DROP, and QUEST markers, fights with COMBAT, MAGIC, and ENEMY AI, collects loot through INVENTORY and SHOP, completes QUEST objectives, and increases PLAYER.level and skill levels through LEVELING. Each death or win uses DEATH and a new seed, while WEATHER changes the feel of travel. Gold and XP grow from enemies, chests, and quests; better weapons, armor, and potions unlock from loot and SHOP; shards unlock the final dragon; the win is completing the type 2 QUEST by killing the frost dragon.

In title, R or Enter sets WORLD.screen to 1 and runs SEEDED WORLD GENERATOR. In game, Escape sets WORLD.screen to 2; in pause, Escape resumes and R restarts with the next seed. In inventory and shop, Escape or I returns to game. The HUD shows PLAYER.health over maxHealth, PLAYER.stamina over maxStamina, PLAYER.magicka over maxMagicka, PLAYER.level, PLAYER.experience, PLAYER.gold, equipped ITEM name, equipped SPELL name, WORLD.weather, WORLD.timeOfDay, WORLD.shardsCollected over 5, and the active QUEST type and progress.
