Build this timed village mystery where you question villagers, match alibis to evidence, and accuse the thief. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

SYSTEMS: TALKING AND CROSS-EXAMINING VILLAGERS, MOVING THROUGH THE VILLAGE, SEARCHING FOR EVIDENCE, THE VILLAGE CLOCK, THE THIEF'S COVER-UP, ACCUSING THE THIEF, CASE PROGRESSION, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART.

TALKING AND CROSS-EXAMINING VILLAGERS: The player opens a conversation by standing within 56 px of a villager's center and pressing E, or by tapping the villager on touch; the conversation is an overlay that pauses movement and the real-time clock. Each villager has a record with name, role, alibiPlace, alibiTime, secret, reliability, isThief, and suspicion, where suspicion starts at 10 for the thief and 0 for every innocent. The dialogue offers six options, chosen with keys 1 through 6 or by tapping: "Where were you?" costs 5 s, "What do you know about the missing thing?" costs 5 s, "Ask about another villager" costs 8 s, "Show a clue" costs 3 s, "Press" costs 10 s, and "Apologize" costs 2 s. "Show a clue" is disabled until at least one clue is found. When the player asks "Where were you?", the villager states alibiPlace and alibiTime; if the player already holds a found clue whose suspect field matches that villager and whose contradictedAlibiPlace or contradictedAlibiTime matches the stated alibi, that clue is marked confirmed, the villager's suspicion increases by 30, and the clue's text is highlighted. When the player asks "What do you know about the missing thing?", an innocent villager with reliability 3 gives the thief's alibiPlace, an innocent villager with reliability 2 gives the thief's alibiTime, an innocent villager with reliability 1 gives a vague line that names no villager, and the thief gives a vague line when thief suspicion is below 30 or one seeded cover-up detail when thief suspicion is at least 30. When the player chooses "Ask about another villager", the player selects a second villager from the other villagers using keys 1 through 9 or by tapping; if the current villager is innocent and has reliability 2 or 3, the line is true and states whether the second villager was at the second villager's alibiPlace during the second villager's alibiTime, a true line that says the second villager was not there confirms any held clue whose suspect is the second villager and whose contradictedAlibiPlace or contradictedAlibiTime matches the second villager's alibi and adds 20 suspicion to the second villager, and a true line that says the second villager was there reduces the second villager's suspicion by 15; if the current villager is the thief or has reliability 1, the line is false and states the opposite, but if the player holds a clue that contradicts the second villager's alibi, that clue becomes confirmed and the second villager's suspicion increases by 20. When the player chooses "Show a clue", the player selects one found clue using keys 1 through 9 or by tapping; if the clue's suspect is the villager being shown to and the clue contradicts that villager's alibi, the clue becomes confirmed and the villager's suspicion increases by 30; if the clue's suspect is the villager being shown to but does not contradict the alibi, the villager's suspicion increases by 15; if the clue's suspect is someone else, the villager's suspicion decreases by 5. "Press" may be used once per villager and only after the player has at least one confirmed clue; on an innocent villager it reveals the villager's secret as a true line and reduces that villager's suspicion by 5, and on the thief it reveals the thief's secret as a false line and increases the thief's suspicion by 20. "Apologize" reduces the current villager's suspicion by 2 and can be used at most three times per villager. A caught lie is exactly the moment a clue becomes confirmed against the villager currently being spoken to; it adds 30 suspicion, plays a sting, and changes that villager's expression with a code-drawn worried outline. Suspicion is clamped between 0 and 100. The player may accuse only when the chosen villager's suspicion is at least the case's accusation threshold and the player has at least the case's required confirmed clues; if those conditions are not met, the accusation collapses, the clock loses 20 s, and the chosen villager's suspicion drops by 20. The core reason to keep talking is that every question either confirms a clue, lowers a false suspicion, exposes a cover-up, or spends time that the clock cannot return.

MOVING THROUGH THE VILLAGE: The village is a 12 by 9 tile grid, each tile 48 px, generated by a SEEDED GENERATOR that places the player start at tile 6,4, the accusation square at tile 6,5, 14 obstacle tiles as buildings, trees, water, and fences, the selected villagers on unique walkable tiles at least 48 px apart, and the active search spots on unique walkable tiles, none on the player start, accusation square, or each other. The player moves with WASD and the arrow keys at 120 px/s, and on touch a virtual d-pad appears in the left half of the screen while tapping a villager or search spot interacts. Buildings, trees, water, and fences block movement; the player cannot leave the 12 by 9 grid. The player's facing changes to the last non-zero direction. When the player is within 56 px of a villager or within 40 px of a search spot, that object pulses and the interact prompt appears. Villagers do not move.

SEARCHING FOR EVIDENCE: Each case has search spots generated from nine templates: blacksmith, well, tavern, garden, bridge, church, market, stables, and dock. Each spot has a record with id, name, tileX, tileY, clueId, and searched. The player stands within 40 px of a spot and presses Q or taps it; the search shows a progress bar for 8 s, and when it completes the clock loses 8 s and the spot yields its clue. A clue has a record with id, text, place, suspect, contradictedAlibiPlace, and contradictedAlibiTime. The clue roster is: flour bag, found at blacksmith, suspect Marta, contradictedAlibiPlace oven; wet coat, found at bridge, suspect Bram, contradictedAlibiPlace dock; ledger page, found at tavern, suspect Sela, contradictedAlibiPlace market; muddy boot, found at well, suspect Fletcher, contradictedAlibiPlace forge; silver coin, found at garden, suspect Old Tom, contradictedAlibiTime dusk; torn ribbon, found at church, suspect Wren, contradictedAlibiTime dawn; red thread, found at stables, suspect Petra, contradictedAlibiPlace well; broken key, found at market, suspect Cato, contradictedAlibiPlace bridge; iron shard, found at dock, suspect Nyra, contradictedAlibiPlace stables. A found clue can be shown in dialogue; a confirmed clue counts toward the case's required confirmed clues; a lost clue from a wrong accusation cannot be shown or counted.

THE VILLAGE CLOCK: The clock starts at the case's clockSeconds. While the village screen is active and no overlay is open, the clock counts down 1 s per real second. Opening dialogue or starting a search pauses the real-time countdown; choosing a dialogue option subtracts its cost, and completing a search subtracts 8 s. At 60 s remaining, every question cost increases by 2 s. At 30 s remaining, "Press" is disabled. If the clock reaches 0, the case ends in failure and the player returns to the case result screen.

THE THIEF'S COVER-UP: The thief is a villager chosen by the SEEDED GENERATOR. The thief perceives the player's current suspicion on the thief, whether the player has confirmed at least one clue against the thief, and whether the player shows a clue to the thief. When thief suspicion is below 30, the thief gives the base alibi and no extra detail. When thief suspicion is at least 30 and below 60, the thief adds one seeded cover-up detail from a list of four: "the missing thing was not in my house", "I was tired", "the road was dark", "I saw no one". When thief suspicion is at least 60 and below 80, the thief blames a rival once: the innocent villager with the highest suspicion gains 15 suspicion, the thief loses 10 suspicion, and if there is a tie the earliest villager in the roster is chosen. When thief suspicion is at least 80, the thief becomes evasive: every question about the thief costs an extra 5 s, and if the player presses the thief, the thief reveals a false secret and gains 20 suspicion. If two clues whose suspect is the thief are confirmed and at least one unfound search spot exists, one unfound search spot becomes locked for 20 s of real time while the village screen is active. The thief never moves, never leaves the village, and never changes alibi except by adding the seeded detail.

ACCUSING THE THIEF: The player opens the accusation screen by standing on the accusation square and pressing R, or by tapping the square on touch. The screen lists every villager with name, role, suspicion bar, and confirmed clue count, where confirmed clue count means confirmed clues whose suspect is that villager. The player selects one villager using keys 1 through 9 or by tapping, then presses Enter or taps Confirm. If the selected villager is the thief, has at least the case's accusation threshold suspicion, and the player has at least the case's required confirmed clues, the case is won. If the selected villager is not the thief but meets the suspicion and clue conditions, the accusation is wrong: the clock loses the case's wrongAccusationPenalty, two random found clues, or all found clues if fewer than two, become lost, every villager's suspicion drops by 10 except the accused, whose suspicion rises by 20, and the thief's cover-up triggers the blame step immediately if it has not already triggered. If the selected villager does not meet the conditions, the accusation collapses: the clock loses 20 s, the selected villager's suspicion drops by 20, and no other values change. The player may accuse again after any failed accusation.

CASE PROGRESSION: The game has three cases. The villager roster is Marta the baker with alibiPlace oven, alibiTime dawn, secret I owe the miller a debt, reliability 2; Bram the boatman with alibiPlace dock, alibiTime dawn, secret My boat was open at dawn, reliability 1; Sela the herbalist with alibiPlace market, alibiTime noon, secret I saw a hooded figure near the well, reliability 3; Fletcher the blacksmith with alibiPlace forge, alibiTime noon, secret I argued with the elder, reliability 2; Old Tom the gravedigger with alibiPlace church, alibiTime dusk, secret I found a coin and kept it, reliability 1; Wren the weaver with alibiPlace garden, alibiTime dawn, secret My ribbon was torn this morning, reliability 2; Petra the priestess with alibiPlace well, alibiTime noon, secret I heard a bell in the stables, reliability 3; Cato the merchant with alibiPlace bridge, alibiTime dusk, secret I sold a key to a stranger, reliability 2; Nyra the stablehand with alibiPlace stables, alibiTime dusk, secret The stable door was open at dusk, reliability 1. The stolen object roster is silver bell, iron key, and red ribbon. Case 1 has 7 villagers, 4 active clues, clockSeconds 240, accusationThreshold 70, requiredConfirmedClues 2, and wrongAccusationPenalty 30 s. Case 2 has 8 villagers, 5 active clues, clockSeconds 210, accusationThreshold 75, requiredConfirmedClues 3, and wrongAccusationPenalty 40 s. Case 3 has 9 villagers, 6 active clues, clockSeconds 180, accusationThreshold 80, requiredConfirmedClues 4, and wrongAccusationPenalty 50 s. Winning case 3 ends the game. Failing a case offers a retry with the next seed for the same case. A SEEDED GENERATOR builds each case from a seed list: case 1 uses 101,102,103,104; case 2 uses 202,203,204,205; case 3 uses 303,304,305,306, then repeats the first seed of that case. It selects the case's villagers from the roster in roster order, chooses one thief, marks the thief's secret as false, chooses one stolen object, selects the case's active clues so that at least one active clue has suspect equal to the thief and every active clue has a unique place, places the active search spots on unique walkable tiles, and sets the player start at tile 6,4. The VERIFIER checks that every active search spot is reachable from the player start, no two search spots share a tile, the thief has at least one active clue, every selected villager has a unique alibiPlace and alibiTime, the accusation square is reachable, at least 60 walkable tiles remain, and the stolen object is one of the three roster objects; if any check fails, re-roll with the next seed in the case's seed list.

SCREENS: The game is a state machine: title (shows the village square scene, the game name, and a Begin button) → how to play (shows the controls and a Back button) → case result (shows the previous outcome, a Retry button, and a Next Case button after a win) → village (the active play area) → dialogue (an overlay over village) → accusation (an overlay over village) → pause (an overlay over village) → case result. From title, Begin goes to case result for the first uncompleted case; from how to play, Back returns to title; Escape in village opens pause; Escape in dialogue returns to village; Escape in accusation returns to village; Escape in pause returns to village; Escape in how to play returns to title; Escape in case result returns to title; Escape in title does nothing. From pause, Resume returns to village, How to Play goes to how to play, and Title goes to title. From case result, Retry starts the same case with the next seed and Next Case starts the following case if the previous case was won. After winning case 3, case result shows a Final Win button that returns to title. Arrow keys move the highlight, Enter activates the highlighted button, and all buttons can also be chosen by tapping.

AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. A soft chime plays when a dialogue option is chosen. A low thud plays every 0.25 s while the player moves. A paper rustle plays when a search starts. A bright sting plays when a clue becomes confirmed. A dull knock plays when an accusation collapses. A bell strike plays when a wrong accusation is made. A rising arpeggio plays when the case is won. A deep gong plays when the clock reaches 0. A tick plays once every 10 s while the clock is below 60 s.
