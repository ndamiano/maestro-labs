Build this village mystery where you talk to villagers to find the thief. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

SYSTEMS: CASE FILE, VILLAGE, VILLAGER, SCHEDULE, DIALOGUE, CLUE, SUSPICION, THIEF, ACCUSATION, PROGRESSION, PLAYER, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART.

CASE FILE: The CASE FILE record is the source of truth for the active case and has fields caseNumber, seed, stolenItemId, stolenItemName, itemValue, victimId, thiefId, deadlineHours, clueCount, suspectIds, solved. The ten stolen items are given as case 1 silver spoon 10, case 2 wedding ring 40, case 3 seed pouch 15, case 4 ledger book 25, case 5 fish lantern 20, case 6 church chalice 60, case 7 wool bolt 30, case 8 honey jar 12, case 9 hunting knife 35, and case 10 music box 50. Every other system reads case fields through this record, so VILLAGER, SCHEDULE, DIALOGUE, CLUE, SUSPICION, THIEF, ACCUSATION, PROGRESSION, and PLAYER never store a second copy of the thief, victim, stolen item, or deadline. A case is won when ACCUSATION records a correct thiefId, and it is lost when deadlineHours runs out or ACCUSATION records a wrong thiefId.

VILLAGE: The VILLAGE record is the source of truth for map geometry and has fields seed, width, height, tiles, buildings, roads, landmarks. Buildings are ids 1 through 15, where 1 through 8 are houses, 9 is the tavern, 10 is the church, 11 through 13 are workshops, 14 is the well, 15 is the mill, and the central square has id 16. A seeded generator with width 16, height 12, and the case seed places, in this order, a central square 2 by 2, eight houses 2 by 2 around the square, three workshops 2 by 3 on the north edge, a tavern 3 by 3 in the southeast, a church 3 by 3 in the northwest, a well 1 by 1 at the square, a mill 2 by 3 on the east edge, four fences 1 by 4 along the outer border, and six trees 1 by 1 in remaining open tiles. The generator fills all other tiles as walkable grass or road. The verifier requires that every building is reachable from the central square, that no two building footprints overlap, that at least 2 walkable tiles separate any two buildings, that no tile has two building types, and that the map contains at least 40 walkable tiles; re-roll with the next seed if any fails. CLUE, SCHEDULE, and PLAYER read locationIds and walkability through this record.

VILLAGER: The VILLAGER record is the source of truth for each villager and has fields id, name, archetype, homeId, workId, baseTrust, memory, lieLimit, suspicionBias. The ten villagers are given as 1 Blacksmith home 1 work 11 baseTrust 40 memory 3 lieLimit 1 suspicionBias 5, 2 Baker home 2 work 12 baseTrust 55 memory 4 lieLimit 1 suspicionBias 2, 3 Herbalist home 3 work 13 baseTrust 60 memory 5 lieLimit 2 suspicionBias 1, 4 Fisherman home 4 work 14 baseTrust 35 memory 2 lieLimit 1 suspicionBias 8, 5 Innkeeper home 9 work 9 baseTrust 50 memory 4 lieLimit 2 suspicionBias 4, 6 Priest home 10 work 10 baseTrust 65 memory 5 lieLimit 1 suspicionBias 0, 7 Farmer home 5 work 15 baseTrust 45 memory 3 lieLimit 1 suspicionBias 6, 8 Tanner home 6 work 11 baseTrust 30 memory 2 lieLimit 2 suspicionBias 7, 9 Weaver home 7 work 12 baseTrust 55 memory 4 lieLimit 1 suspicionBias 3, and 10 Hunter home 8 work 13 baseTrust 40 memory 3 lieLimit 1 suspicionBias 4. DIALOGUE, SCHEDULE, THIEF, and ACCUSATION read these fields through this record, so no other system stores a second copy of a villager's trust, memory, or limits.

SCHEDULE: The SCHEDULE record is the source of truth for time and villager location and has fields caseId, activeHour, hour, villagerId, locationId, activity, alibiText. activeHour starts at 0 and advances by 1 when DIALOGUE or PLAYER inspection spends 1 hour; if activeHour reaches the CASE FILE deadlineHours, the case is lost. A seeded generator using the case seed, suspectIds, and thiefId places 12 hour entries for each suspect, with hours 0 through 11 representing day 1 hours 8, 12, 16, 20, day 2 hours 8, 12, 16, 20, and day 3 hours 8, 12, 16, 20. It places, in order, the thief's false alibis at hours 4, 6, and 8 in the tavern, square, and church; each other suspect's truthful alibis at the same three hours in their home or work; and all remaining suspect hours in their home or work. The verifier requires that every suspect has 12 entries, that the thief has at least 2 contradictions, that at least 3 suspects share the same location at hour 5, that no two suspects have identical 12-hour location sequences, and that every locationId exists in VILLAGE; re-roll with the next seed if any fails. DIALOGUE, ACCUSATION, and PLAYER read the active hour and location through this record.

DIALOGUE: The DIALOGUE record is the source of truth for conversation state and has fields caseId, villagerId, nodeId, topic, line, choices, currentTrust, trustChange, clueId, hourCost. The five topics are given as Greeting, Work, Victim, Stolen Item, and Suspect, and each topic has hourCost 1. Each topic has three choices, polite, direct, and hostile, with trustChange values 2, 0, and -6 added to the topic base value. The topic base values are Greeting +5, Work +3, Victim +2, Stolen Item +0, and Suspect -5. currentTrust starts at the villager's baseTrust. Greeting reveals no clue. Work reveals the first unrevealed clue if a CLUE is located at that villager's workId. Victim reveals the first unrevealed clue if the villager's memory is at least 3. Stolen Item reveals the first unrevealed clue if the villager's currentTrust is at least 50. Suspect reveals one alibi from another suspect if the villager's currentTrust is at least 60 and memory is at least 4. DIALOGUE reads villager stats from VILLAGER, locations from VILLAGE, and time from SCHEDULE, so it stores only conversation nodes, current trust, and revealed clue references.

CLUE: The CLUE record is the source of truth for evidence and has fields clueId, caseId, type, locationId, found, weight, suspectIds, text. The eight clue types are given as fingerprint 30, witness 15, motive 20, alibi break 25, item fragment 20, footprint 15, ledger 10, and confession 50. A seeded generator using the case seed, thiefId, suspectIds, and clueCount places, in order, a confession clue at the thief's home with suspectIds containing the thief, two high clues of type fingerprint and alibi break at two of the thief's false alibi locations with suspectIds containing the thief, the remaining clues at the home or work location of a random suspect with suspectIds containing one suspect, and two red herring clues at the home or work location of non-thief suspects with suspectIds containing that non-thief suspect. The verifier requires that at least 3 clues point to the thief, that no clue is placed at an unreachable location, that no clue type repeats, that at least 1 clue is placed at each of 3 distinct locations, that the total clue count equals the case file clueCount, and that every locationId exists in VILLAGE; re-roll with the next seed if any fails. SUSPICION, DIALOGUE, and ACCUSATION read clue fields through this record.

SUSPICION: The SUSPICION record is the source of truth for player suspicion and has fields caseId, villagerId, score, evidenceIds, cleared, accused. Each suspect starts with score 0. When a CLUE is found, the clue's weight is added to the score of every villager in that clue's suspectIds. When DIALOGUE's Suspect topic reveals an alibi that matches SCHEDULE, the alibi's owner's score decreases by 10 and cannot fall below 0. When DIALOGUE's Suspect topic names a suspect and the speaker's currentTrust is at least 60, the named suspect's score increases by 5. The maximum score is 100, and any value above 100 is set to 100. ACCUSATION reads these scores through this record, so no other system stores a second copy of suspicion.

THIEF: The THIEF record is the source of truth for the thief's behavior and has fields caseId, thiefId, method, lieCount, confessionNode. The ten methods are given as case 1 smash, case 2 pickpocket, case 3 trade, case 4 forge, case 5 borrow, case 6 hide, case 7 sell, case 8 swap, case 9 burn, and case 10 bury. It reads thiefId from CASE FILE and reads lieLimit, memory, and suspicionBias from VILLAGER. The thief can lie at most the villager's lieLimit times. If the player asks Stolen Item before the confession clue is found, the thief lies on the first ask and tells the truth on the second ask if the thief's currentTrust is at least 60. If the thief's suspicionBias is at least 5, the first lie also prevents the Stolen Item topic from revealing a clue. If the confession clue is found, the thief cannot lie. If the thief's SUSPICION score is at least 40 and the player asks Suspect, the thief's line includes a stutter and reveals one red herring clue. DIALOGUE and ACCUSATION read this behavior through the record.

ACCUSATION: The ACCUSATION record is the source of truth for the accusation and has fields caseId, accusedId, evidenceIds, correct, timeUsed. The player may accuse only when a SUSPICION score is at least 30. When the player confirms an accusedId, ACCUSATION copies the current evidenceIds from SUSPICION and compares accusedId with CASE FILE thiefId. If they match, correct is true, the case is won, PROGRESSION increases reputation by 20 and score by 100, and the result screen shows the stolen item's value as a separate number. If they do not match, correct is false, the case is lost, PROGRESSION decreases reputation by 10 and increases failures by 1. If no SUSPICION score is at least 30, the accuse button is disabled. PROGRESSION and SCREENS read this record.

PROGRESSION: The PROGRESSION record is the source of truth for campaign state and has fields caseNumber, unlocked, caseSeed, suspectCount, clueCount, deadlineHours, reputation, score, failures. The ten cases are given as case 1 seed 101 suspects 3 clues 5 hours 12, case 2 seed 102 suspects 4 clues 5 hours 12, case 3 seed 103 suspects 5 clues 6 hours 12, case 4 seed 104 suspects 6 clues 6 hours 11, case 5 seed 105 suspects 6 clues 7 hours 11, case 6 seed 106 suspects 7 clues 7 hours 10, case 7 seed 107 suspects 8 clues 8 hours 10, case 8 seed 108 suspects 8 clues 8 hours 9, case 9 seed 109 suspects 9 clues 8 hours 9, and case 10 seed 110 suspects 10 clues 8 hours 8. Reputation starts at 0, score starts at 0, and failures starts at 0. Case 1 is unlocked, and each later case unlocks when the previous case is solved. If failures reaches 3, the campaign is lost. If case 10 is solved, the campaign is won. CASE FILE, SCREENS, and PLAYER read campaign state through this record.

PLAYER: The PLAYER record is the source of truth for the detective and has fields positionX, positionY, clueInventory, dialogueLog, suspicionLog. The detective starts at tile 7,5 in the central square and moves 1 tile per 0.2 seconds while a movement key is held. The controls are W or up for up, S or down for down, A or left for left, D or right for right, E or Enter to talk, Q to inspect, C to open the case file, Space to advance dialogue, and Escape to back out. Q spends 1 hour to inspect the current location. Touch controls use tap to move to a walkable tile, tap a villager to talk, and tap the case icon to open the case file. PLAYER reads walkability from VILLAGE, case fields from CASE FILE, time from SCHEDULE, clues from CLUE, and suspicion from SUSPICION, so it stores only position, logs, and found clue references.

SCREENS: The screens form a state machine: title shows the village scene and offers Play, Continue, and Help; Play starts case 1, Continue resumes the active case, Continue is disabled when no case is active, and Help lists the controls. Title moves to briefing, briefing shows the case number, stolen item, victim, deadline, and starts the village screen. Village moves to dialogue when the player talks to a villager, moves to case file when the player presses C or taps the case icon, and moves to accusation from the case file. Dialogue moves back to village when the player presses Escape or finishes a topic. Case file moves to accusation when the player selects a suspect with score at least 30. Accusation moves to result after confirm. Result moves to the next briefing when the case is won, moves to game over when failures reach 3, and moves to victory when case 10 is won. Escape in village returns to title, Escape in dialogue returns to village, Escape in case file returns to village, Escape in accusation returns to case file, and Escape in result returns to title. The game over screen offers Retry Case 1 and Title, Retry Case 1 resets failures to 0, reputation to 0, score to 0, and unlocks only case 1, and the victory screen offers Play Again and Title, Play Again resets the campaign to case 1, reputation 0, score 0, and failures 0.

AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. The title sting plays when the title screen appears. A step sound plays on every completed tile move. A talk blip plays when a dialogue choice is selected. A clue chime plays when a CLUE is found. A clock tick plays when the case hour advances. An accuse horn plays when the player confirms an accusation. A victory fanfare plays when ACCUSATION is correct. A failure drone plays when ACCUSATION is wrong or when deadlineHours runs out. A menu click plays when a button is pressed.

ART: Ask for a village scene as a scene, ask for a detective as a sprite, ask for a blacksmith as a sprite, ask for a baker as a sprite, ask for an herbalist as a sprite, ask for a fisherman as a sprite, ask for an innkeeper as a sprite, ask for a priest as a sprite, ask for a farmer as a sprite, ask for a tanner as a sprite, ask for a weaver as a sprite, ask for a hunter as a sprite, ask for a fingerprint as a sprite, ask for a witness as a sprite, ask for a motive as a sprite, ask for an alibi break as a sprite, ask for an item fragment as a sprite, ask for a footprint as a sprite, ask for a ledger as a sprite, and ask for a confession as a sprite. Each image holds one subject. Everything else — tiles, roads, fences, trees, buildings, UI, case file, suspicion bars, clock, dialogue boxes, buttons, and stolen item icons — is drawn in code.
