Build this incremental origami game where you fold seeded paper sheets, unfold them layer by layer, and buy generators to complete the Thousand Cranes. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

SYSTEMS: CORE LOOP, FOLD ENERGY, MANUAL FOLD, UNFOLD CYCLE, CREASE MAP, SHEET GENERATOR, WRAITHS, RUIN, GENERATORS, UPGRADES, PATTERN GOALS, PRESTIGE, SAVE STATE, INPUT, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART.

CORE LOOP: The core loop is fold, unfold, buy, complete. The Player record is the source of truth for foldEnergy, creasePoints, souls, totalFolds, currentSheetIndex, manualYieldMultiplier, generatorRateMultiplier, autoFoldMultiplier, unfoldCostReduction, wraithDamage, baseYieldBonus, sheetRewardBonus, unfoldCreaseBonus, and ruin, and every other system reads those fields instead of keeping its own copy. Each second the game ticks once: GENERATORS adds energy, WRAITHS drains energy, RUIN updates, and SAVE STATE autosaves every 10 ticks. The player folds visible crease tiles with MANUAL FOLD to gain Fold Energy, spends Fold Energy on GENERATORS and UNFOLD CYCLE, uses Crease Points on UPGRADES, completes sheets through PATTERN GOALS, and wins through PRESTIGE. The challenge is that WRAITHS drain energy and can fill RUIN to 100, which causes game over.

FOLD ENERGY: Fold Energy is stored in Player.foldEnergy, starts at 0, has no maximum, and never goes below 0. It is gained by MANUAL FOLD, by GENERATORS each second, and by offline progress in SAVE STATE. It is spent by buying GENERATORS, by UNFOLD CYCLE, and is drained by WRAITHS. When a purchase or unfold would make it negative, the action is blocked. When a drain would make it negative, Player.foldEnergy becomes 0. The game over condition is not caused directly by zero energy; zero energy only matters when WRAITHS are active and RUIN increases.

MANUAL FOLD: A tile is visible when tile.unfoldStage is less than or equal to Sheet.unfoldStage and tile.blocked is false. A manual fold is the player pressing Space or Enter while the cursor is on a visible tile. If the tile is a crease and has no wraith, the tile state becomes folded, Player.totalFolds increases by 1, and Player.foldEnergy increases by (tile.baseYield + Player.baseYieldBonus) * Player.manualYieldMultiplier. If the tile is a crease with a wraith, the fold is a strike: it deals Player.wraithDamage to the wraith, does not change the tile state, and grants no Fold Energy. If the tile is blank, blocked, hidden, or already folded, nothing happens. A fold counts as a fold only when it changes a wraith-free crease tile to folded.

UNFOLD CYCLE: Unfolding is how the sheet opens to reveal deeper layers. The Sheet record has fields seed, index, patternName, width, height, unfoldStage, completed, and a tiles array, and it is the source of truth for which tiles exist and which are revealed. The PatternGoal record supplies unfoldCost, and the effective cost is PatternGoal.unfoldCost reduced by Player.unfoldCostReduction, with the lowest possible unfold cost being 50. Pressing U or touching the Unfold button succeeds only if Sheet.unfoldStage is less than 3, Sheet.completed is false, and Player.foldEnergy is at least the effective cost. On success, Player.foldEnergy decreases by the effective cost, Sheet.unfoldStage increases by 1, every tile whose unfoldStage equals the new Sheet.unfoldStage becomes visible, and Player.creasePoints increases by Player.unfoldCreaseBonus. Unfolding does not remove folded tiles or wraiths. A sheet completes automatically when every crease tile in all four stages has state folded.

CREASE MAP: The Crease Map is a view of the Sheet record, not a second copy. The Tile record has fields x, y, state, unfoldStage, baseYield, wraithId, and blocked, where state is one of blank, crease, folded, or blocked. The MapView record has fields sheet, cursorX, cursorY, lastFoldedTile, and hoverTile, and it reads the Sheet record for every tile it draws. The map is 12 by 12 tiles. Hidden tiles are drawn as dark paper; visible blank tiles are drawn as blank paper; crease tiles are drawn as crease lines; folded tiles are drawn as solid folded paper; blocked tiles are drawn as ink stains; and active wraiths are drawn on their tile. The cursor is a ring one tile wide. The map shows the current unfoldStage, the Fold Energy amount, the Crease Points amount, the Ruin bar, the selected generator, and the selected upgrade.

SHEET GENERATOR: Every sheet is laid out by a seeded generator from the PatternGoal record; no sheet is hand-written. The generator takes PatternGoal.index, PatternGoal.seed, PatternGoal.width, PatternGoal.height, PatternGoal.layers, PatternGoal.creaseCount, PatternGoal.layerCounts, PatternGoal.blockedCount, PatternGoal.wraithCount, PatternGoal.unfoldCost, PatternGoal.baseYield, PatternGoal.rewardCreasePoints, and PatternGoal.wraithHp. It fills all 144 tiles as blank, places blockedCount blocked tiles on random distinct tiles except (0,0), places creaseCount crease tiles on random distinct non-blocked tiles, assigns unfoldStage 0 to the first layerCounts[0] creases, stage 1 to the next layerCounts[1], stage 2 to the next layerCounts[2], and stage 3 to the remaining creases, assigns baseYield to every crease, and places wraithCount wraiths on random distinct crease tiles with unfoldStage 1, 2, or 3. It sets each wraith hp and maxHp to PatternGoal.wraithHp. It ends with a verifier: (0,0) is not blocked; every crease tile is reachable from (0,0) by 4-way moves that do not enter blocked tiles; no tile is both blocked and crease; no tile has two wraiths; the crease count equals PatternGoal.creaseCount; the wraith count equals PatternGoal.wraithCount; and if PatternGoal.index is at least 2 then at least one wraith exists; if any check fails, re-roll with seed + 1.

WRAITHS: The Wraith record has fields id, tileX, tileY, hp, maxHp, drain, active, and revealed, and it is the source of truth for wraith health. Sheet tile.wraithId points to the wraith id, and a wraith is revealed when its tile is visible. Every second, for every wraith with revealed true, Player.foldEnergy decreases by Wraith.drain, which is 1 for every sheet, and if it would go below 0 it becomes 0. A manual fold or auto-fold on a wraith tile deals Player.wraithDamage to Wraith.hp. When Wraith.hp is at most 0, Wraith.active becomes false, tile.wraithId becomes 0, Player.creasePoints increases by 10, and the wraith is removed from the map. Wraiths do not move, do not multiply, and do not drain while hidden.

RUIN: Ruin is stored in Player.ruin, starts at 0, and has a maximum of 100. Every second, if Player.foldEnergy is 0 and at least one wraith is revealed, Player.ruin increases by 1. If Player.foldEnergy is greater than 0, Player.ruin decreases by 2. If Player.ruin reaches 100, the game enters game over. On game over, Player.creasePoints is halved and rounded down, Player.foldEnergy becomes 0, Player.ruin becomes 0, the current sheet is regenerated with the next seed after the current Sheet.seed at unfoldStage 0, wraiths are rebuilt, and the player returns to the game screen with the same currentSheetIndex. Generators, upgrades, souls, and totalFolds are kept.

GENERATORS: The Generator record has fields id, name, baseCost, costGrowth, baseRate, autoFoldRate, count, and nextCost, and it is the source of truth for generator ownership. The roster is Fold Pencil with baseCost 10, costGrowth 1.15, baseRate 1, autoFoldRate 0.1; Paper Crane with baseCost 100, costGrowth 1.15, baseRate 8, autoFoldRate 0.3; Origami Bot with baseCost 1100, costGrowth 1.15, baseRate 45, autoFoldRate 1.0; Crease Printer with baseCost 12000, costGrowth 1.15, baseRate 260, autoFoldRate 2.5; Fold Singularity with baseCost 140000, costGrowth 1.15, baseRate 1400, autoFoldRate 6.0; Unfold Engine with baseCost 1600000, costGrowth 1.15, baseRate 7800, autoFoldRate 14.0; Paper Galaxy with baseCost 20000000, costGrowth 1.15, baseRate 44000, autoFoldRate 32.0; and Thousand Cranes with baseCost 250000000, costGrowth 1.15, baseRate 260000, autoFoldRate 80.0. Pressing 1 through 8 buys that generator if Player.foldEnergy is at least nextCost. On purchase, Player.foldEnergy decreases by nextCost, count increases by 1, and nextCost is multiplied by costGrowth and rounded down. Every second, Player.foldEnergy increases by the total of baseRate times count for every generator, multiplied by Player.generatorRateMultiplier. The AutoFold record has fields progress, and every second progress increases by the total of autoFoldRate times count for every generator, multiplied by Player.autoFoldMultiplier. When progress is at least 1, the system consumes 1, chooses the first visible crease tile in reading order from top-left to bottom-right with y from 0 to 11 and x from 0 to 11, and if that tile has no wraith folds it, increases Player.totalFolds by 1, and increases Player.foldEnergy by (tile.baseYield + Player.baseYieldBonus) * Player.manualYieldMultiplier; if it has a wraith it deals Player.wraithDamage to the wraith; if no visible crease tile exists, progress is capped at 1.

UPGRADES: The Upgrade record has fields id, name, cost, effectType, value, and purchased, and it is the source of truth for which permanent effects are owned. The roster is Sharper Pencil cost 50 effect manualYieldMultiplier +1; Crease Memory cost 250 effect generatorRateMultiplier +0.25; Quick Hands cost 1000 effect autoFoldMultiplier +0.10; Unfold Catalyst cost 5000 effect unfoldCostReduction +100; Wraith Salt cost 20000 effect wraithDamage +1; Paper Blessing cost 100000 effect baseYieldBonus +5; Fold Echo cost 500000 effect sheetRewardBonus +100; Unfold Echo cost 2000000 effect unfoldCreaseBonus +10; Crane Choir cost 10000000 effect generatorRateMultiplier +1.0; and Thousand Fold cost 50000000 effect sheetRewardBonus +1000. Player starts with manualYieldMultiplier 1, generatorRateMultiplier 1, autoFoldMultiplier 1, unfoldCostReduction 0, wraithDamage 1, baseYieldBonus 0, sheetRewardBonus 0, and unfoldCreaseBonus 0. Pressing E or touching an upgrade row buys the selected upgrade if it is not purchased and Player.creasePoints is at least cost. On purchase, Player.creasePoints decreases by cost, purchased becomes true, and the matching Player field increases by value. Upgrades are permanent and are not lost on game over.

PATTERN GOALS: The PatternGoal record has fields index, seed, patternName, width, height, layers, creaseCount, layerCounts, blockedCount, wraithCount, unfoldCost, baseYield, rewardCreasePoints, and wraithHp, and it is the source of truth for sheet parameters. All sheets have width 12, height 12, and layers 4. The roster is: sheet 1 Single Crane seed 1000 creaseCount 12 layerCounts 3,3,3,3 blockedCount 1 wraithCount 0 unfoldCost 100 baseYield 5 rewardCreasePoints 50 wraithHp 0; sheet 2 Double Crane seed 1097 creaseCount 16 layerCounts 4,4,4,4 blockedCount 2 wraithCount 1 unfoldCost 200 baseYield 10 rewardCreasePoints 100 wraithHp 2; sheet 3 Fan seed 1194 creaseCount 20 layerCounts 5,5,5,5 blockedCount 3 wraithCount 2 unfoldCost 300 baseYield 15 rewardCreasePoints 200 wraithHp 3; sheet 4 Lotus seed 1291 creaseCount 24 layerCounts 6,6,6,6 blockedCount 4 wraithCount 3 unfoldCost 400 baseYield 20 rewardCreasePoints 400 wraithHp 4; sheet 5 Dragon seed 1388 creaseCount 28 layerCounts 7,7,7,7 blockedCount 5 wraithCount 4 unfoldCost 500 baseYield 25 rewardCreasePoints 800 wraithHp 5; sheet 6 Phoenix seed 1485 creaseCount 32 layerCounts 8,8,8,8 blockedCount 6 wraithCount 5 unfoldCost 600 baseYield 30 rewardCreasePoints 1600 wraithHp 6; sheet 7 Koi seed 1582 creaseCount 36 layerCounts 9,9,9,9 blockedCount 7 wraithCount 6 unfoldCost 700 baseYield 35 rewardCreasePoints 3200 wraithHp 7; sheet 8 Chrysanthemum seed 1679 creaseCount 40 layerCounts 10,10,10,10 blockedCount 8 wraithCount 7 unfoldCost 800 baseYield 40 rewardCreasePoints 6400 wraithHp 8; sheet 9 Crane Swarm seed 1776 creaseCount 44 layerCounts 11,11,11,11 blockedCount 9 wraithCount 8 unfoldCost 900 baseYield 45 rewardCreasePoints 12800 wraithHp 9; sheet 10 Paper Moon seed 1873 creaseCount 48 layerCounts 12,12,12,12 blockedCount 10 wraithCount 9 unfoldCost 1000 baseYield 50 rewardCreasePoints 25600 wraithHp 10; sheet 11 Galaxy Fold seed 1970 creaseCount 52 layerCounts 13,13,13,13 blockedCount 11 wraithCount 10 unfoldCost 1100 baseYield 55 rewardCreasePoints 51200 wraithHp 11; and sheet 12 Thousand Cranes seed 2067 creaseCount 56 layerCounts 14,14,14,14 blockedCount 12 wraithCount 11 unfoldCost 1200 baseYield 60 rewardCreasePoints 100000 wraithHp 12. When a sheet completes, Player.creasePoints increases by PatternGoal.rewardCreasePoints + Player.sheetRewardBonus. If index is less than 12, Player.currentSheetIndex increases by 1 and the next sheet is generated from its PatternGoal. If index is 12 or greater, the sheet is endless: the next sheet uses the sheet 12 parameters, adds currentSheetIndex - 12 to wraithCount, blockedCount, and wraithHp, adds 5 * (currentSheetIndex - 12) to baseYield, adds 100 * (currentSheetIndex - 12) to unfoldCost, adds 4 * (currentSheetIndex - 12) to creaseCount with the added creases assigned to stage 3, and rewardCreasePoints becomes 100000 + 10000 * (currentSheetIndex - 12).

PRESTIGE: Prestige is the win and the endless reward. Player.souls starts at 0 and never decreases. Completing sheet 12 for the first time triggers the win screen, grants 1 soul, and adds 0.10 to Player.generatorRateMultiplier. Every completed sheet with index 12 or greater grants 1 soul and adds 0.10 to Player.generatorRateMultiplier. Souls do not buy anything directly; they permanently raise generator production through Player.generatorRateMultiplier. The win condition is the first completion of sheet 12, and the game can continue endlessly after the win.

SAVE STATE: The SaveState record has fields version, lastTick, player, sheet, generatorCounts, upgradeIds, autoFoldProgress, and screen, and it is the persistence snapshot built from the live records. Autosave writes it every 10 seconds, on sheet complete, on game over, on win, on purchase, and when the tab becomes hidden. On load, SaveState overwrites the live records and the game resumes from the saved sheet, generators, upgrades, and resources. If lastTick is older than 60 seconds, offline progress grants Player.foldEnergy equal to the elapsed seconds of generator production, capped at 86400 seconds of production, with no auto-fold, no wraith drain, and no ruin. If no save exists, a new game starts at sheet 1 with Player.foldEnergy 0, Player.creasePoints 0, Player.souls 0, Player.totalFolds 0, and Player.currentSheetIndex 1.

INPUT: The Input record has fields cursorX, cursorY, keys, touchMode, selectedGenerator, and selectedUpgrade. W and ArrowUp move the cursor one tile up, S and ArrowDown move one tile down, A and ArrowLeft move one tile left, and D and ArrowRight move one tile right; the cursor cannot move outside the 12 by 12 grid or into a blocked tile. Space or Enter performs MANUAL FOLD on the cursor tile. U performs UNFOLD CYCLE. Keys 1 through 8 buy the corresponding generator. Q cycles selectedUpgrade to the next unpurchased upgrade, wrapping around, and if all upgrades are purchased Q does nothing. E buys selectedUpgrade. On touch, tapping a grid tile moves the cursor to that tile if it is not blocked and performs a manual fold if the tile is a visible crease; tapping the Unfold button performs UNFOLD CYCLE; tapping a generator row buys that generator; tapping an upgrade row buys that upgrade; and tapping the pause button opens pause. The initial cursor is at (0,0).

SCREENS: The screens are a state machine: title shows the paper crane logo, the game name, a Continue button if a save exists, and a New Fold button; Enter or tapping New Fold starts a new game, tapping Continue loads the save, and Escape does nothing. Title leads to game. Game shows the crease map, resource counters, generator rows, upgrade rows, Ruin bar, and Unfold button; Escape leads to pause. Pause shows totalFolds, souls, sheet index, Resume, Restart, and Quit; Escape or tapping Resume returns to game, tapping Restart starts a new game at sheet 1, and tapping Quit returns to title. Game over shows the torn paper message, the reason that ruin reached 100, and Fold Again; Enter or tapping Fold Again regenerates the current sheet with the next seed at unfoldStage 0, keeps generators, upgrades, and souls, halves Crease Points, and returns to game, while Quit returns to title and Escape does nothing. Win shows the Thousand Cranes scene, souls, and Continue Endless; Enter or tapping Continue Endless returns to game with endless sheets unlocked, Quit returns to title, and Escape does nothing.

AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. The fold blip is a short 880 Hz sine tone that plays on every successful manual fold and auto-fold. The wraith hit is a 220 Hz square pulse that plays when a fold damages a wraith. The wraith kill is a 660 Hz triangle arpeggio of three notes that plays when a wraith hp reaches 0. The unfold swoosh is a 120 ms noise sweep that plays when unfoldStage increases. The buy tone is a 440 Hz sine tone that plays when a generator is bought. The upgrade tone is a 520 Hz sine tone that plays when an upgrade is bought. The sheet complete chime is a 1046 Hz sine chord that plays when a sheet completes. The ruin tick is a 60 Hz triangle pulse that plays once per second while Player.ruin is increasing. The game over tear is a 400 ms noise burst that plays when ruin reaches 100. The win chime is a 1318 Hz sine arpeggio of four notes that plays when sheet 12 completes for the first time.
