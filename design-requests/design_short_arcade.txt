Build this top-down space shooter with waves of enemies. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

SYSTEMS: PLAYER SHIP, WEAPONS, ENEMIES, ASTEROID FIELD, WAVE GENERATOR, SPAWN ROUTES, MOVEMENT AND COLLISION, DAMAGE AND HULL, POWER-UPS, SCORE AND PROGRESSION, BOSS WAVE, UI AND HUD, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART.

PLAYER SHIP: The player ship is one Player record with fields id, x, y, vx, vy, angle, hull, maxHull, shield, maxShield, weapon, fireCooldown, invulnerable, lives, rapidUntil, spreadUntil, missileUntil. The Player record is the source of truth for the ship, and PLAYER SHIP, MOVEMENT AND COLLISION, DAMAGE AND HULL, SCORE AND PROGRESSION, UI AND HUD, and SCREENS read it. The arena is 960 by 540 pixels, the ship starts at x 480 and y 270 with angle 0, maxHull 100, maxShield 50, lives 3, radius 14, max speed 220 pixels per second, turn rate 6.5 radians per second, friction 3.5, fire cooldown 0.18 seconds, and invulnerable 2.0 seconds after a hull hit. W, A, S, D and the arrow keys are both bound; W and up thrust forward, S and down thrust backward, A and left turn left, D and right turn right, and Space fires. Touch uses the left half of the screen as a virtual joystick for thrust and turn and the right half to fire. The ship is clamped inside the arena with a 16 pixel margin.

WEAPONS: WEAPONS is the source of truth for all Projectile records, each with fields id, owner, x, y, vx, vy, radius, damage, ttl, weapon, turn, targetId. The player weapon roster is Blaster with speed 720, damage 12, radius 3, ttl 1.2, cooldown 0.18; Rapid with speed 760, damage 9, radius 2, ttl 1.0, cooldown 0.07, duration 8.0; Spread with three shots at -0.25, 0, and +0.25 radians, speed 680, damage 10, radius 3, ttl 1.1, duration 10.0; and Missile with speed 320, damage 35, radius 5, ttl 2.5, turn 4.0 radians per second, cooldown 0.8, duration 12.0. Enemy bullets use BruteBullet with speed 260, damage 12, radius 4, ttl 2.0 and BossBullet with speed 300, damage 14, radius 4, ttl 2.5. A projectile is alive until ttl reaches 0, it leaves the arena, or it collides with a valid target; player projectiles hit enemies and asteroids, enemy projectiles hit the player, and all collisions remove the projectile.

ENEMIES: ENEMIES is the source of truth for all Enemy records, each with fields id, type, x, y, vx, vy, hp, maxHp, radius, speed, angle, fireCooldown, firePattern, score, contactDamage, dropChance, age, state. The enemy roster is Drone with hp 20, speed 180, radius 12, contactDamage 10, score 50, dropChance 0.05, no fire, and a chase behavior; Stinger with hp 14, speed 260, radius 10, contactDamage 8, score 70, dropChance 0.05, no fire, and a sine weave with amplitude 60 and period 1.2; Splitter with hp 30, speed 150, radius 16, contactDamage 12, score 90, dropChance 0.08, no fire, and on death spawns 2 Mini enemies at angles -1.2 and +1.2; Mini with hp 8, speed 240, radius 7, contactDamage 5, score 20, dropChance 0.02, no fire, and a chase behavior; and Brute with hp 60, speed 110, radius 20, contactDamage 25, score 150, dropChance 0.12, fireCooldown 2.2, firePattern 3 bullets at -0.3, 0, and +0.3 radians, and a hold-distance behavior that keeps 220 pixels from the player. Enemies are removed when hp is 0, award score, roll dropChance for a powerup, and play an explosion.

ASTEROID FIELD: The asteroid field is laid out by a seeded generator that creates Asteroid records with fields id, x, y, radius, type, angle, spin, vx, vy, hp. From a seed and the 960 by 540 arena it places 14 asteroids in order from the center outward, with radius 18 to 34, hp 40, spin 0.2 to 0.9 radians per second, velocity 10 to 30 pixels per second, and type small or large by radius. Asteroids drift, bounce off arena walls, and rotate. The verifier requires at least 10 asteroids, no two asteroids overlap, none within 120 pixels of the player start, none within 50 pixels of an arena edge, and all asteroids fully inside the arena, re-roll with the next seed if any fails.

WAVE GENERATOR: The wave list is laid out by a seeded generator that creates Wave records with fields waveNumber, seed, enemyBudget, typeWeights, boss, escortCount, powerupCount, cleared. From a seed and the 20 wave count it places waves 1 through 20 in order, with nonboss enemyBudget 12, boss enemyBudget 5, boss waves 5, 10, 15, and 20, boss escortCount 4, powerupCount 2 for nonboss waves and 1 for boss waves, and type weights chosen by wave band: waves 1 to 3 Drone 100; waves 4 to 7 Drone 60 and Stinger 40; waves 8 to 11 Drone 40, Stinger 30, Splitter 30; waves 12 to 14 Drone 30, Stinger 30, Splitter 20, Brute 20; waves 15 to 19 Drone 25, Stinger 30, Splitter 25, Brute 20; wave 20 Boss 100. The generator selects the enemy type for each spawn slot from the seeded weights and records the order. The verifier requires exactly 20 waves, boss only at waves 5, 10, 15, and 20, every nonboss wave has 12 enemy slots, every boss wave has 1 boss and 4 escorts, no wave has more than 20 enemies, and every type weight in a wave sums to 100, re-roll with the next seed if any fails.

SPAWN ROUTES: Spawn routes are laid out by a seeded generator that creates SpawnPoint records with fields id, waveNumber, x, y, side, delay, enemyType. For each wave it places spawn points on the left, right, top, or bottom edge with a 40 pixel margin, assigns nonboss enemies delays from 0.0 to 4.5 seconds, places the boss at top center after 1.5 seconds, and places boss escorts at the four corners after 3.0 seconds. Enemies enter from their SpawnPoint and move toward the arena center. The verifier requires every enemy to have a SpawnPoint, no spawn point within 80 pixels of an asteroid, no two spawn points within 30 pixels, all spawn points inside the arena margin, every spawn point has a straight path to the arena center that does not cross an asteroid, and at least 4 distinct spawn points per wave, re-roll with the next seed if any fails.

MOVEMENT AND COLLISION: This system reads the Player, Enemy, Projectile, Asteroid, and Powerup records and updates their positions each frame. It applies thrust and friction to the Player, chase, weave, hold-distance, and drift movement to enemies and asteroids, and straight-line or homing movement to projectiles. A hit is a circle-circle overlap between the stored radii; player projectiles collide with enemies and asteroids, enemy projectiles collide with the player, enemy bodies collide with the player, and the player body collides with asteroids and arena walls. The player is clamped to the arena, asteroids bounce off walls with a 1.0 restitution, enemies are allowed to enter from edges but are clamped to a 10 pixel margin after entry, and powerups drift with velocity 10 pixels per second and fade after ttl.

DAMAGE AND HULL: Damage events are records with fields id, targetId, amount, kind, x, y, and the system reads the Player, Enemy, and Asteroid records to apply them. When the player takes damage, shield absorbs first up to its current value, then hull absorbs the remainder; if hull is 0, lives decreases by 1, the ship returns to x 480 y 270 with hull 100 and shield 50, and invulnerable is 2.0 seconds. If lives is 0 after a death, the game ends. Enemy damage reduces hp, and when hp is 0 the enemy is removed, awards score, rolls dropChance, and Splitter spawns 2 Minis. Asteroid damage reduces hp, and when hp is 0 the asteroid is removed. Asteroid contact deals 15 damage to the player. Shield regenerates at 4.0 per second up to maxShield, but only after 3.0 seconds without damage.

POWER-UPS: Powerups are records with fields id, type, x, y, ttl, spin, vx, vy. The roster is Repair with effect +30 hull up to maxHull, Shield with effect +50 shield up to maxShield, Rapid with effect setting the player weapon to Rapid for the Rapid duration in WEAPONS, Spread with effect setting the player weapon to Spread for the Spread duration in WEAPONS, and Missile with effect setting the player weapon to Missile for the Missile duration in WEAPONS. A powerup spawns when an enemy's dropChance succeeds or from a wave's powerupCount at a seeded random safe point with no asteroid overlap and at least 60 pixels from the player, with ttl 8.0 seconds, spin 1.5 radians per second, and velocity 10 pixels per second. Pickup is a circle overlap with the player at radius 20, and pickup sets the effect immediately, replacing any active timer of the same weapon type.

SCORE AND PROGRESSION: The Score record has fields score, highScore, combo, comboTimer, kills, powerupsCollected, and is the source of truth for score and kill progression, read by UI AND HUD, SCREENS, and WAVE GENERATOR. Enemy score values come from the ENEMIES roster and boss score comes from BOSS WAVE. A kill within 3.0 seconds of the previous kill raises combo through the given stages 1, 2, 3, and 4 before resetting to 1, and the awarded score is the enemy score plus a combo bonus of 0, 25, 50, or 100 for those stages. Wave progression occurs when all enemies in the current Wave record are dead, then the next wave starts after 2.5 seconds. Victory occurs when wave 20 is cleared, and game over occurs when lives is 0.

BOSS WAVE: The boss is an Enemy record with type boss and the same fields as other enemies, plus phase, pattern, patternTimer, and bossWave. Boss hp is 900 at wave 5, 1250 at wave 10, 1600 at wave 15, and 1950 at wave 20; speed is 70, radius 46, contactDamage 35, score 1000, dropChance 1.0, and escort count is 4 Drones. The boss starts with Radial and uses three patterns: Radial fires 16 BossBullets every 1.8 seconds, Aimed fires 3 BossBullets at -0.25, 0, and +0.25 radians every 0.9 seconds, and Spiral fires 1 BossBullet every 0.12 seconds for 2.0 seconds. Phase 2 starts at hp 600 for wave 5, 825 for wave 10, 1060 for wave 15, and 1300 for wave 20, and it changes pattern to Aimed; phase 3 starts at hp 300 for wave 5, 410 for wave 10, 530 for wave 15, and 650 for wave 20, and it changes pattern to Spiral. The boss is removed when hp is 0, awards score, spawns 1 Missile powerup, and clears the wave.

UI AND HUD: The HUD record has fields score, highScore, wave, lives, hull, shield, weapon, bossHull, waveBanner, and pause, and it reads the Score, Player, Wave, and Boss records to display current state. It draws score and high score at the top, wave number at top center, hull as a 120 by 10 bar, shield as a 120 by 6 bar, lives as 3 ship icons, active weapon name, and a 320 by 12 boss bar when a boss is alive. WaveBanner shows the wave number for 2.0 seconds at the start of a wave, and pause dim is 0.6 alpha. The HUD does not change game state except to display it.

SCREENS: The Screen record has fields name, transition, input, and overlay, and the screens are a state machine: title (game name, high score, press Enter or tap) → game (arena and HUD) → paused (dim overlay with Resume, Restart, Mute) → game over (final score, high score, Enter or tap restarts, Escape title) or victory (final score, high score, Enter or tap plays again, Escape title). In title, Enter or tap starts; in game, Escape or P pauses, M mutes, and R restarts; in paused, Escape or Enter resumes, R restarts, and M toggles mute; in game over or victory, Enter or tap restarts and Escape returns to title.

AUDIO: The Audio record has fields muted, gain, and lastPlayed, with muted false and gain 0.5, and all audio is generated with the Web Audio API. The sounds are uiClick on start, pause, and menu choice; waveStart on a new wave; playerShot on every player projectile; enemyShot on every enemy projectile; playerHit when shield or hull takes damage; enemyExplosion when an enemy dies; asteroidCrack when an asteroid is hit; powerupPickup when a powerup is collected; bossAlarm when a boss spawns; bossPhase when the boss changes phase; gameOver on final death; and victory on clearing wave 20. Each sound uses short oscillator or noise bursts with the given durations: uiClick 0.05, waveStart 0.4, playerShot 0.04, enemyShot 0.06, playerHit 0.12, enemyExplosion 0.25, asteroidCrack 0.1, powerupPickup 0.15, bossAlarm 0.6, bossPhase 0.3, gameOver 0.8, victory 1.2.

ART: Ask for a deep space nebula background as a scene, a player ship as a sprite, a drone as a sprite, a stinger as a sprite, a splitter as a sprite, a mini as a sprite, a brute as a sprite, a boss as a sprite, a small asteroid as a sprite, a large asteroid as a sprite, a repair powerup as a sprite, a shield powerup as a sprite, a rapid powerup as a sprite, a spread powerup as a sprite, and a missile powerup as a sprite. Everything else — player bullets, enemy bullets, missiles, particle explosions, collision flashes, starfield twinkle, HUD bars, text, pause overlay, grid, and vignette — is drawn in code.
