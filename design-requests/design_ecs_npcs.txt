CONTROLS: When state is playing, WASD and the arrow keys are both bound to move the player, with W and Up moving -y, S and Down moving +y, A and Left moving -x, D and Right moving +x, and combined movement never exceeding Player.speed 160 px/s. When the player moves down, Player.facing becomes 0 degrees; when the player moves up, Player.facing becomes 180 degrees; when the player moves right, Player.facing becomes 90 degrees; when the player moves left, Player.facing becomes 270 degrees. When E is pressed and Player.talking is false, if the nearest VILLAGER is within 40 px, set Player.selectedVillagerId to that villager's id, set Player.talking to true, set state to dialogue, and open DIALOGUE. When E is pressed and no VILLAGER is within 40 px, if Player.x and Player.y are inside a LOCATION rectangle, trigger CLUE DISCOVERY. On touch, the left half of the screen is a virtual joystick mapped to WASD, the right half of the screen has a button mapped to E, and dialogue screens show on-screen buttons mapped to Up, Down, Enter, and Escape. When Escape is pressed in dialogue, set Player.talking to false and set state to playing. When Escape is pressed in game, set state to paused.

TIME AND SCHEDULE: When state is playing, Game.time increases by 1 ms for every 1 ms of real time. Whenever Game.time reaches 240000 ms, set Game.time to Game.time - 240000 ms, set Game.day to Game.day + 1, and set every LOCATION.searched to false. A time is inside an alibi when VILLAGER.alibiStart is less than or equal to the time and the time is less than or equal to VILLAGER.alibiEnd. For each VILLAGER, if Game.day is 1 and Game.time is less than alibiStart, the villager's target is the center of its homeLocationId; if Game.day is 1 and Game.time is inside the alibi, the villager's target is the center of its alibiLocationId; if Game.day is 1 and Game.time is greater than alibiEnd, the villager's target is the center of its homeLocationId; if Game.day is 2 or 3, the villager's target is the center of its homeLocationId. Move each VILLAGER toward its target at VILLAGER.speed 80 px/s. When Game.day becomes greater than Game.deadlineDay 3 and Game.solved is false, set state to lost.
