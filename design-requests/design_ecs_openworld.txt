Build this top-down squirrel foraging stealth game in a small neighborhood where you gather the freshest high-quality nuts, cache them at your home tree, and avoid a rival raccoon. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

RECORDS: SQUIRREL, NUT_KIND, NUT, TREE, BUSH, HOUSE, POND, RACCOON, HOME_TREE, WORLD.

SQUIRREL: The squirrel has x 480 px, y 560 px, input x 0, input y 0, speed 120 px/s, facing 0 radians, energy 100 with max 100, fullness 100 with max 100, an inventory list of up to 5 NUT references, best quality 0, and score 0.

NUT_KIND: The fixed nut roster is ACORN with quality 2, fullness 10, energy 5, score 10, decay 2 per s, HAZELNUT with quality 4, fullness 15, energy 8, score 25, decay 1.5 per s, WALNUT with quality 6, fullness 20, energy 12, score 40, decay 1 per s, BEECHNUT with quality 8, fullness 25, energy 18, score 80, decay 0.5 per s, and GOLDEN_ACORN with quality 10, fullness 35, energy 25, score 200, decay 0.25 per s.

NUT: Each nut has kind one of NUT_KIND, quality 1 to 10, freshness 100, and state carried, stored, or stolen.

TREE: Each tree has kind one of OAK, HAZEL, WALNUT, or BEECH, x, y, radius 36 px, richness 0 to 3, slots 0 to 4, regrow timer 0 to 60 s, and golden chance 0 to 5; the fixed tree roster is OAK with slots 4, regrow 20 s, golden chance 0, HAZEL with slots 4, regrow 25 s, golden chance 0, WALNUT with slots 3, regrow 40 s, golden chance 0, and BEECH with slots 2, regrow 60 s, golden chance 5.

BUSH: Each bush has x, y, radius 28 px, and concealment 1.

HOUSE: Each house has x, y, width 80 px, height 60 px, and blocks 1.

POND: Each pond has x, y, radius 40 px, and blocks 1.

RACCOON: The raccoon has x, y, speed 80 px/s, alertness 0 to 100, state patrol, chase, lost, or cooldown, timer 0, target point x 0 and y 0, and stolen count 0.

HOME_TREE: The home tree has x 480 px, y 560 px, radius 48 px, a cache list of up to 10 NUT references, goal count 5, and goal quality 8.

WORLD: The world has seed 1, width 960 px, height 640 px, day 1, time 0 s, day length 300 s, tree count 18, bush count 10, house count 5, pond count 2, raccoon count 1, and state playing, won, or lost.

SYSTEMS: SEEDED GENERATOR, PLAYER CONTROLS, SQUIRREL MOVEMENT, NUT GATHERING, NUT FRESHNESS, INVENTORY, HOME CACHING, RACCOON, DAY CYCLE, WIN/LOSE.

SEEDED GENERATOR: When a new game starts, use WORLD seed to place all world records. Place HOME_TREE at 480 px, 560 px. Place 5 HOUSES at random positions on a 20 px grid inside WORLD width and height with a 40 px margin, no two houses closer than 120 px center to center, no house within 100 px of HOME_TREE, and no house overlapping another by 20 px. Place 2 PONDS at random positions, each at least 120 px from any house center, at least 120 px from HOME_TREE, and at least 100 px from any other pond. Place 18 TREES at random positions, each at least 70 px from any other tree, at least 80 px from any house or pond center, at least 100 px from HOME_TREE, and within the 40 px margin. Assign each tree kind by rolling 1 to 100: 1 to 35 OAK, 36 to 65 HAZEL, 66 to 85 WALNUT, and 86 to 100 BEECH. Set slots to the kind's slots, regrow timer to 0, richness to a random integer 0 to 3, and golden chance to the kind's golden chance. Place 10 BUSHES at random positions, each at least 30 px from any tree center, at least 50 px from any house or pond center, and at least 80 px from HOME_TREE. Place 1 RACCOON at a random point at least 250 px from SQUIRREL start and not inside a HOUSE rectangle or POND circle. Set RACCOON target point to a random TREE center. Then run the verifier: rasterize the world into 20 px cells, mark HOUSE and POND cells blocked, flood fill from the HOME_TREE cell, and require every TREE cell to be reachable, require at least 18 trees, require at least 10 bushes, require no two trees closer than 70 px, require no HOUSE or POND overlap, and require the RACCOON start to be unblocked; re-roll with the next seed if any fails.

PLAYER CONTROLS: When A or left arrow is held, set SQUIRREL input x to -1; when D or right arrow is held, set SQUIRREL input x to 1; when W or up arrow is held, set SQUIRREL input y to -1; when S or down arrow is held, set SQUIRREL input y to 1; if no direction is held, set SQUIRREL input x and input y to 0,0; normalize SQUIRREL input x and input y to length 1. When Space or E is pressed, trigger interact. When Q is pressed, trigger eat. When C is pressed, trigger cache. On touch, the left half of the screen is a virtual joystick that sets the same SQUIRREL input x and input y values from its offset, the right lower corner has an interact button that triggers interact, the right middle has an eat button that triggers eat, and the right upper has a cache button that triggers cache.

SQUIRREL MOVEMENT: Each frame, if SQUIRREL input x and input y length is greater than 0, set SQUIRREL facing to the input angle and move SQUIRREL x and y by SQUIRREL speed times delta time times input magnitude; if SQUIRREL energy is greater than 50 use SQUIRREL speed, if energy is 50 or less use SQUIRREL speed minus 40, and if energy is 20 or less use SQUIRREL speed minus 80, with a minimum speed of 40. Clamp SQUIRREL x to 12 and WORLD width minus 12, clamp y to 12 and WORLD height minus 12. If a proposed move would put SQUIRREL inside a HOUSE rectangle or POND circle, cancel that axis of movement. While moving, decrease SQUIRREL energy by 1 every 6 s; while idle, decrease energy by 1 every 20 s; set energy to 0 if it would fall below 0. Each second, decrease SQUIRREL fullness by 1 every 8 s; set fullness to 0 if it would fall below 0.

NUT GATHERING: When the player triggers interact and SQUIRREL is within 48 px of a TREE center and SQUIRREL inventory count is less than 5 and TREE slots is greater than 0, roll a random integer from 1 to 100 and roll a second random integer from 0 to 2. If SQUIRREL is within 24 px of the same TREE center, add 1 to the second roll. If the first roll is less than or equal to TREE golden chance, create a NUT of kind GOLDEN_ACORN; otherwise create a NUT of kind ACORN for OAK, HAZELNUT for HAZEL, WALNUT for WALNUT, or BEECHNUT for BEECH. Set NUT quality to the NUT_KIND quality plus TREE richness plus the second roll, capped at 10; if the kind is GOLDEN_ACORN, set quality to 10. Set NUT freshness to 100, state to carried, and add the NUT to the front of SQUIRREL inventory. Set SQUIRREL best quality to the larger of best quality and NUT quality. Decrease TREE slots by 1 and set TREE regrow timer to the tree kind's regrow value. If the created NUT quality is at least 8, increase SQUIRREL score by 5. If the created NUT quality is 10, increase SQUIRREL score by 20. If the created NUT kind is GOLDEN_ACORN, increase SQUIRREL score by 50. Play gather. If SQUIRREL inventory count is 5, do nothing and play a full inventory blip. If TREE slots is 0, do nothing. Each second, for every TREE with slots less than its kind's slots, increase TREE regrow timer by 1 s; when TREE regrow timer is at least the kind's regrow value, set regrow timer to 0 and increase TREE slots by 1. When a TREE regrows a slot, if the tree kind is BEECH, set its golden chance to 5; if the tree kind is not BEECH and its richness is 3, set its golden chance to 2; if the tree kind is not BEECH and its richness is less than 3, set its golden chance to 0.

NUT FRESHNESS: Each second, for every NUT with state carried, decrease NUT freshness by its NUT_KIND decay; set freshness to 0 if it would fall below 0. Each time NUT freshness crosses below 75, 50, 25, 10, or 0, set NUT quality to quality minus 1; set quality to 1 if it would fall below 1.

INVENTORY: When the player triggers eat and SQUIRREL inventory is not empty, remove the front NUT, set SQUIRREL fullness to fullness plus the NUT_KIND fullness, capped at 100, set SQUIRREL energy to energy plus the NUT_KIND energy, capped at 100, set SQUIRREL score to score plus NUT quality times 2, and play eat. If SQUIRREL inventory is empty, do nothing.

HOME CACHING: When the player triggers cache and SQUIRREL is within 48 px of HOME_TREE and SQUIRREL inventory is not empty and HOME_TREE cache count is less than 10, remove the front NUT, add it to HOME_TREE cache, set NUT state to stored, set SQUIRREL score to score plus NUT_KIND score plus NUT quality times 10, and play store. If the stored NUT quality is at least 8, set SQUIRREL speed to speed plus 5, capped at 160. If HOME_TREE cache count is 10, do nothing and play a full cache blip. If SQUIRREL inventory is empty, do nothing.

RACCOON: When RACCOON state is patrol, if RACCOON target point is within 16 px of RACCOON position, choose a random TREE center as the new target; move RACCOON toward the target at RACCOON speed. When RACCOON state is chase, move RACCOON toward SQUIRREL at 100 px/s. RACCOON cannot enter a HOUSE rectangle or POND circle; if a proposed move is blocked, try the x axis alone, then the y axis alone, and if both are blocked, do not move. If RACCOON state is patrol and its position does not change for 1 s, choose a new random TREE target. If SQUIRREL is within 140 px of RACCOON and SQUIRREL is not inside any BUSH circle, set RACCOON state to chase and alertness to 100. If RACCOON state is chase, increase RACCOON timer by 1 s each second; if SQUIRREL is inside a BUSH circle, increase RACCOON timer by 2 s instead of 1 s; if RACCOON timer reaches 3 s while SQUIRREL is inside a BUSH circle, set state to lost, timer to 0, and alertness to 50; if RACCOON timer reaches 6 s, set state to lost, timer to 0, and alertness to 50. If RACCOON is within 24 px of SQUIRREL, remove the front NUT from SQUIRREL inventory if any, set that NUT state to stolen, increase RACCOON stolen count by 1, set RACCOON state to cooldown, timer to 5, and alertness to 0; if SQUIRREL inventory is empty, decrease SQUIRREL energy by 20, set energy to 0 if below 0, and still set RACCOON state to cooldown, timer to 5, and alertness to 0. When RACCOON state is lost, decrease alertness by 10 each second; when alertness is 0, set state to patrol. When RACCOON state is cooldown, decrease timer by 1 s each second; when timer is 0, set state to patrol. While RACCOON state is patrol or lost, decrease alertness by 5 each second, minimum 0.

DAY CYCLE: Each second, increase WORLD time by 1 s. When WORLD time is at least day length, set time to time minus day length, increase day by 1, and play day chime. When day becomes 4, if WORLD state is playing, set state lost.

WIN/LOSE: When HOME_TREE cache contains at least goal count NUTs with quality at least goal quality, set WORLD state won and play win. When SQUIRREL fullness is 0, set WORLD state lost and play lose. If both a win and a loss condition are true, set WORLD state won. When WORLD state is won or lost, stop SQUIRREL MOVEMENT, NUT GATHERING, NUT FRESHNESS, INVENTORY, HOME CACHING, RACCOON, and DAY CYCLE.

CORE LOOP: Minute to minute, the player uses SQUIRREL MOVEMENT to search the generated neighborhood, uses NUT GATHERING to pull fresh nuts from trees, watches NUT FRESHNESS while carrying them, avoids the RACCOON, and returns to HOME CACHING to store the best nuts. Each stored nut adds score, each nut with quality at least 8 makes SQUIRREL speed grow by 5, and the cache count of qualifying nuts moves the player toward the win, which is storing 5 nuts of quality 8 or higher before day 4 begins; losing comes from hunger, running out of time, or the raccoon stealing the nuts needed to finish.

SCREENS: Title shows the squirrel sprite, a neighborhood scene, and the prompt Enter or tap Start to begin; Enter or Start leads to game. Game shows the playfield and HUD; Escape leads to paused. Paused shows a dimmed playfield and the prompt Escape or tap Resume to continue; Escape or Resume leads back to game. Game over shows the result, SQUIRREL score, SQUIRREL best quality, and the prompt Enter or tap Restart to return to title; Enter or Restart leads to title. Escape does nothing on title or game over. The HUD shows SQUIRREL fullness, SQUIRREL energy, SQUIRREL inventory count, the quality of each NUT in SQUIRREL inventory, SQUIRREL score, SQUIRREL best quality, WORLD day, WORLD time, and the count of NUTs in HOME_TREE cache with quality at least HOME_TREE goal quality.

AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. Play gather when a NUT is created by NUT GATHERING. Play store when a NUT is added to HOME_TREE cache. Play eat when a NUT is removed by INVENTORY. Play full inventory blip when NUT GATHERING is blocked by a full SQUIRREL inventory. Play full cache blip when HOME CACHING is blocked by a full HOME_TREE cache. Play alert when RACCOON state changes to chase. Play steal when RACCOON removes a NUT or hits an empty SQUIRREL. Play day chime when DAY CYCLE increases WORLD day. Play win when WORLD state becomes won. Play lose when WORLD state becomes lost.
