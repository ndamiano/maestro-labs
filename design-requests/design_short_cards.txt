Build this turn-based card battle game against an AI. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

SYSTEMS: BATTLE RECORD, CARD CATALOG, DECK GENERATOR, PLAYER RECORD, BOARD STATE, TURN ENGINE, RESOURCE SYSTEM, AI OPPONENT, COMBAT RESOLUTION, CARD EFFECTS, GAME FLOW, UI STATE, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART.

BATTLE RECORD: The battle record is the source of truth for the overall battle state, and the deck generator, player records, turn engine, game flow, and UI state read from it. The battle record has fields battleId, seed, status, winner, finalTurn. seed starts at 12345 and increases by 1 each time a new game starts. status is playing, victory, or defeat. winner is 0 for player, 1 for AI, or -1 for none. finalTurn starts at 0 and is set to the turn engine's turnNumber when status changes from playing.

CARD CATALOG: The card catalog is the single source of truth for card definitions, and all systems read card fields from it instead of storing their own copies. Each card record has fields cardId, name, type, cost, attack, health, keywords, spellEffect, deathrattle, artId, and artId is the card name. The roster is: Spearman, cardId 1, minion, cost 1, attack 2, health 1, no keyword; Guard, cardId 2, minion, cost 2, attack 1, health 3, Taunt; Wolf, cardId 3, minion, cost 2, attack 3, health 2, no keyword; Golem, cardId 4, minion, cost 3, attack 2, health 5, Taunt; Knight, cardId 5, minion, cost 4, attack 4, health 4, no keyword; Dragon Whelp, cardId 6, minion, cost 5, attack 5, health 4, no keyword; Giant, cardId 7, minion, cost 6, attack 6, health 6, no keyword; Elemental, cardId 8, minion, cost 7, attack 7, health 7, no keyword; Boar, cardId 9, minion, cost 2, attack 3, health 2, Charge; Vampire, cardId 10, minion, cost 3, attack 3, health 3, Deathrattle draw 1; Phoenix, cardId 11, minion, cost 5, attack 4, health 4, Deathrattle deal 2 damage to all enemy minions; Warrior, cardId 12, minion, cost 6, attack 5, health 5, Windfury; Aegis, cardId 13, minion, cost 3, attack 2, health 4, Divine Shield; Fireball, cardId 14, spell, cost 4, deal 6 damage to any target; Arcane Missiles, cardId 15, spell, cost 1, deal 1 damage to a target 3 times; Healing Light, cardId 16, spell, cost 2, restore 6 health to your hero up to 30; Blessing, cardId 17, spell, cost 1, give +2 attack and +2 health to a friendly minion; Silence, cardId 18, spell, cost 2, remove keywords and temporary effects from a minion; Meteor, cardId 19, spell, cost 6, deal 3 damage to all enemy minions and 1 damage to enemy hero.

DECK GENERATOR: The deck generator is a seeded generator that takes the battle record seed and a side value 0 for player or 1 for AI and produces a deck record with fields deckId, ownerSide, seed, cardIds, drawIndex. It starts with the battle record seed and increases the seed by 1 on each re-roll. It fills cardIds with 30 entries in draw order by using the seed to pick a cardId from the card catalog with weights: cost 1 or 2 has weight 5, cost 3 or 4 has weight 4, cost 5 or 6 has weight 3, cost 7 has weight 2. It then shuffles the 30 entries with the same seed and sets drawIndex to 0. The verifier checks that cardIds has exactly 30 entries, no cardId appears more than 2 times, at least 10 cards have cost 1 or 2, at least 6 cards have cost 3 or 4, at least 3 spells are present, and at least 2 minions have Taunt; re-roll with the next seed if any fails.

PLAYER RECORD: The player record is the source of truth for each hero's persistent state, and the AI opponent, turn engine, resource system, combat resolution, and game flow read from it. Each player record has fields playerId, side, name, heroId, maxHealth, health, deckId, hand, fatigue, maxMana, mana. The player record starts with side 0, name Player, heroId 1, maxHealth 30, health 30, deckId from the deck generator for side 0, hand empty, fatigue 0, maxMana 0, mana 0. The AI player record starts with side 1, name AI, heroId 2, maxHealth 30, health 30, deckId from the deck generator for side 1, hand empty, fatigue 0, maxMana 0, mana 0. The player record's hand is a list of cardIds and is read by the UI state for display.

BOARD STATE: The board state is the source of truth for minion positions and current combat stats, and the turn engine, combat resolution, card effects, and UI state read from it. The board state record has fields boardId, playerMinions, aiMinions. playerMinions and aiMinions are lists of minion records, each with fields minionId, ownerSide, cardId, attack, health, maxHealth, keywords, canAttack, attacksLeft, summoningSick, divineShield, buffAttack, buffHealth, position. The board has 7 minion slots per side, positions 0 through 6, and a new minion is placed in the first empty position from the left. The hero targets are not stored as copies of hero health; the board state only knows that side 0 and side 1 have hero targets, and the combat resolution reads hero health from the player record.

TURN ENGINE: The turn engine is the source of truth for turn order and phase, and the resource system, AI opponent, combat resolution, and UI state read its active player and phase. The turn engine record has fields turnNumber, activeSide, phase, endTurnLock. turnNumber starts at 1, activeSide starts at 0, phase starts at main, and endTurnLock starts at false. At game start, the player draws 4 cards and the AI draws 3 cards. At the start of each turn, the active player's maxMana increases by 1 to a maximum of 10, mana is set to maxMana, the active player draws 1 card, and all friendly minions have summoningSick set to false, canAttack set to true, and attacksLeft set to 1, or 2 if the minion has Windfury. The phase changes from main to target when a card or minion needs a target, and back to main when the target is resolved. The active player may end turn by pressing Enter or Space, or by tapping End Turn, which sets activeSide to the other side and increases turnNumber by 1 when the AI turn ends.

RESOURCE SYSTEM: The resource system reads and writes mana and draw state on the player record and the deck record, and it does not keep a separate copy of deck cards. A card can be played only if the active player's mana is at least the card's cost; playing it subtracts the cost from mana and removes the card from hand. Drawing a card takes the card at drawIndex, increases drawIndex by 1, and then either adds it to the active player's hand if the hand has fewer than 10 cards or destroys it if the hand has 10 cards. If the deck is empty when a draw is required, the active player takes fatigue damage that starts at 1 and increases by 1 after each empty draw, and the player's fatigue field increases by 1. Healing Light restores 6 health to the casting hero, but hero health cannot exceed maxHealth 30.

AI OPPONENT: The AI opponent is a record with fields aiId, aggression 7, greed 5, and lastAction. It uses the seeded random generator with the battle record seed and turnNumber as parameters to choose actions during the AI turn, and the generator advances one step after each random choice. aggression 7 makes it prefer the higher-attack target in ties, and greed 5 makes it play the highest-cost affordable card first. On its turn, the AI plays affordable cards in descending cost order. Fireball targets the player hero if player health is 6 or less, otherwise the player minion with the highest attack, otherwise the player hero. Arcane Missiles targets the player hero 3 times if player health is 3 or less, otherwise a random player minion or hero. Healing Light is cast when AI health is 15 or less. Blessing is cast only if the AI has a minion, and it targets the AI minion with the highest attack. Silence is cast only if the player has a minion, and it targets the player minion with the highest attack. Meteor is cast when the player has 3 or more minions. For attacks, if the player has any Taunt minion, the AI attacks only Taunt minions; otherwise it attacks the player minion whose health is less than or equal to the attacker's attack and has the highest attack, otherwise the player hero. The AI ends its turn after it has no more affordable cards and all AI minions have attacked.

COMBAT RESOLUTION: Combat resolution reads board state and player record, and it writes damage, deaths, and attack counters. A friendly minion may attack only if canAttack is true, attacksLeft is greater than 0, and summoningSick is false. If the defending side has any minion with Taunt, the attack must target one of those minions; otherwise it may target any enemy minion or the enemy hero. When a minion attacks a minion, the attacker deals its attack damage to the defender's health and the defender deals its attack damage to the attacker's health. When a minion attacks a hero, the hero takes the attacker's attack damage. If a minion's health becomes 0 or less, it is removed from board state and its deathrattle effect triggers. A minion with Windfury has attacksLeft 2 on its first turn, and after an attack its attacksLeft decreases by 1. A minion with Charge may attack on the turn it is played. A minion with Divine Shield ignores the first time it would take damage, then loses Divine Shield.

CARD EFFECTS: Card effects are triggered by the turn engine, resource system, and combat resolution, and they read card definitions from the card catalog and current state from board state and player record. When a minion is played, it is added to board state with attack equal to the card's attack plus buffAttack 0, health equal to the card's health plus buffHealth 0, maxHealth set to the new health value, keywords copied from the card, divineShield true if the card has Divine Shield, summoningSick true, attacksLeft 0, and canAttack false unless the card has Charge, in which case summoningSick false and attacksLeft 1, or 2 if it has both Charge and Windfury. Blessing adds 2 to the target minion's attack and 2 to its health and maxHealth. Silence sets the target minion's keywords to empty, sets divineShield to false, sets buffAttack and buffHealth to 0, but does not change current attack or health. Arcane Missiles deals 1 damage to the chosen target 3 times, and if the target dies, each remaining missile chooses a new random target using the seeded random generator with the battle record seed and turnNumber as parameters. Meteor deals 3 damage to every enemy minion and 1 damage to the enemy hero. Deathrattle draw 1 draws 1 card for the dead minion's owner. Deathrattle deal 2 damage to all enemy minions deals 2 damage to each enemy minion of the dead minion's owner. Deathrattle effects trigger in board position order from left to right.

GAME FLOW: The game flow reads the battle record, player records, and board state to determine the result, and it writes status, winner, and finalTurn to the battle record. The game starts when the title screen Start button is pressed, creates both player records, generates both decks, draws starting hands, and enters the game screen. The player wins when the AI hero health becomes 0 or less. The AI wins when the player hero health becomes 0 or less. If both heroes reach 0 or less in the same turn, the active player wins. If turnNumber reaches 120, the side with higher hero health wins; if both hero health values are equal, the player wins. The game flow then shows the victory screen for a player win or the defeat screen for an AI win.

UI STATE: The UI state is the source of truth for selection, cursor, and target mode, and it reads from the player record, board state, turn engine, and card catalog to render the game. The UI state record has fields screen, selectedCardId, selectedMinionId, cursorRow, cursorColumn, targetMode, message, animationId, animationTimer. cursorRow 0 is the player hand, cursorRow 1 is the player board, cursorRow 2 is the enemy board, and cursorRow 3 is the enemy hero. A and Left move cursorColumn left by 1, D and Right move cursorColumn right by 1, W and Up move cursorRow up by 1, S and Down move cursorRow down by 1, and the row clamps between 0 and 3. The cursor wraps within the current row. Space or Enter confirms the selected card or minion, and Escape cancels the current selection or target mode. Touch controls let the player tap a hand card to select it, tap a valid target to play it or attack, and tap the End Turn button to end the turn. targetMode is none, spell, or attack; valid targets are highlighted in the UI state.

SCREENS: The game is a state machine with title, game, victory, and defeat screens. Title shows the game name, a Start button, and the text WASD or arrows to aim, Space to confirm, tap to play; Start leads to game. Game shows the enemy hand count, enemy hero, enemy board, player board, player hero, player hand, turn number, mana, and End Turn button; Escape opens a pause overlay with Resume and Quit, Resume returns to game, and Quit returns to title. Victory shows the text Player Wins, final turn number, and Play Again and Title buttons; Play Again leads to game with a new battle seed, and Title leads to title. Defeat shows the text AI Wins, final turn number, and Play Again and Title buttons; Play Again leads to game with a new battle seed, and Title leads to title. Escape on victory or defeat leads to title.

AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. Each sound is a record with fields soundId, trigger, and playback state. uiClick plays when a button or card is selected. cardDraw plays when a card is drawn from a deck. cardPlay plays when a card leaves the hand and enters play. attackSwing plays when a minion begins an attack. heroHit plays when a hero takes damage. minionDeath plays when a minion is removed from the board. victoryFanfare plays when the victory screen appears. defeatDrone plays when the defeat screen appears. turnBell plays when the active side changes.

ART: AI art is used for one subject per image. Each art asset is a record with fields assetId, subject, and kind. Ask for player hero portrait as a sprite, ask for AI hero portrait as a sprite, ask for card back as a sprite, ask for battle background as a scene, ask for victory emblem as a sprite, ask for defeat emblem as a sprite, ask for Spearman art as a sprite, ask for Guard art as a sprite, ask for Wolf art as a sprite, ask for Golem art as a sprite, ask for Knight art as a sprite, ask for Dragon Whelp art as a sprite, ask for Giant art as a sprite, ask for Elemental art as a sprite, ask for Boar art as a sprite, ask for Vampire art as a sprite, ask for Phoenix art as a sprite, ask for Warrior art as a sprite, ask for Aegis art as a sprite, ask for Fireball art as a sprite, ask for Arcane Missiles art as a sprite, ask for Healing Light art as a sprite, ask for Blessing art as a sprite, ask for Silence art as a sprite, and ask for Meteor art as a sprite. Everything else — board slots, card frames, buttons, text, numbers, health bars, mana crystals, cursor, damage numbers, particles, status icons, and target highlights — is drawn in code.
