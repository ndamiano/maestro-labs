Build this four-lane browser rhythm game where the player hits falling notes to its own generated music. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.
SYSTEMS: PLAYING A SONG, NOTE TIMING AND JUDGMENT, SONG SELECT AND UNLOCKS, GENERATED MUSIC, NOTE CHART GENERATOR, RESULTS AND PROGRESSION, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART.
PLAYING A SONG: The core loop is to choose a generated song, watch four lanes, and press the matching lane key as each note reaches the hit line, then survive the 120000 ms song and chase a higher rank. The playfield is 960 px wide and 540 px high. Four lanes are 120 px wide, with centers at x=300, x=420, x=540, and x=660. The hit line is a horizontal bar at y=460 px. Notes are 64 px tap squares or 40 px wide hold bodies, spawn at y=-40 px, and fall at 250 px/s, so a note is visible for 2000 ms before its hit time. Lane 1 is bound to W and Arrow Up, lane 2 to A and Arrow Left, lane 3 to S and Arrow Down, and lane 4 to D and Arrow Right. On touch, four pads are 120 px wide and 80 px tall, placed at the lane centers from y=460 px to y=540 px, and a hold note requires the player to keep the pad or key pressed. At the start of a song, a 2000 ms countdown runs, then the song plays for 120000 ms. The player's health starts at 100 and cannot exceed 100; judgments change health as defined in NOTE TIMING AND JUDGMENT, and if health reaches 0 the song fails immediately. The player earns score and combo by hitting notes, loses combo on Miss, and finishes with a results screen that records best rank and unlocks. The loop is repeated by retrying the same song, choosing a harder difficulty, or moving to the next unlocked song, because each song has a different BPM, musical seed, and chart seed, and each difficulty changes note count and hold probability. During gameplay, the screen shows the song title at top left, score at top center, combo at center, health bar at bottom left, and judgment text for 300 ms above the hit line. A lane flashes for 100 ms when pressed, a Perfect makes the hit line pulse for 200 ms, and the combo counter pulses for 150 ms on every 10 combo. The health bar is 200 px wide and 16 px tall. The player can see the next 2000 ms of notes, plan holds, and choose when to release. The game does not auto-advance; the player must manage four lanes simultaneously. The repeated minute-to-minute action is a cycle of anticipation, press, judgment, health update, and combo update, repeated for 120000 ms. The player can pause with Escape or P, resume with Enter, return to song select with Escape from pause, or retry with R from results or game over. The stated goal is to clear all five songs on any difficulty and then maximize rank on Hard, because the five songs increase BPM from 100 to 140 and make the same timing windows harder.
NOTE TIMING AND JUDGMENT: Each note record has lane, startMs, type, endMs, and judged. A note is active from startMs - 90 ms to startMs + 90 ms. A key down for a lane searches for the nearest unjudged active note in that lane, choosing the smallest absolute difference, not the earliest note. If found, the judgment is Perfect if the difference is 30 ms or less, Great if it is 60 ms or less, and Good if it is 90 ms or less. If no active note is found, the press has no effect and does not consume the input. Key repeat events are ignored. The window is symmetric around startMs, so a note 91 ms early is not active and a note 91 ms late is already Miss. The search does not judge a note in another lane. An unjudged note becomes Miss when song time passes startMs + 90 ms. A hold note has a head and a tail. The head is judged by key down exactly as a tap. The hold is only successful if the same lane key or pad is still pressed at endMs and the key up occurs within 90 ms of endMs; if key up is 30 ms or less from endMs the tail is Perfect, 60 ms or less Great, 90 ms or less Good. If the key is released before endMs - 90 ms, or is still pressed after endMs + 90 ms, the tail is Miss. If the head is Miss, the hold ends immediately, the tail is marked Miss, and only the head health penalty applies. A key up with no active hold has no effect. Judgment values are Perfect 1000, Great 700, Good 400, Miss 0; hold tail values are Perfect 500, Great 350, Good 200, Miss 0. The combo multiplier starts at 1.00, increases by 0.10 every 10 combo, cannot exceed 3.00, and resets to 1.00 when combo resets. Combo increments on every Perfect, Great, or Good head or tail, and resets to 0 on any Miss. Health changes are Perfect +2, Great +1, Good +0, head Miss -12, hold tail Miss -6. If health reaches 0, the song fails. Accuracy is (perfect * 1 + great * 0.70 + good * 0.40) divided by total judged events, where total judged events counts tap notes, hold heads, and hold tails. The result record has perfect, great, good, miss, maxCombo, score, and rank. The system stores maxCombo as the largest combo reached during the song. The timing system updates score, combo, health, and maxCombo immediately after each judgment, shows the judgment text for 300 ms, and checks for fail after every judgment. The song end check happens when song time reaches 120000 ms and all notes are judged. The system makes the core challenge timing precision, because the player does not move a character and the only failure is missed timing.
SONG SELECT AND UNLOCKS: Five songs are listed. Each song record has name, seed, BPM, rootMidi, scale, intensity, and unlockIndex. Song 1 is Static Bloom, seed 101, BPM 100, rootMidi 57, scale minor, intensity 2, unlockIndex 0. Song 2 is Copper Rain, seed 202, BPM 112, rootMidi 58, scale minor, intensity 3, unlockIndex 1. Song 3 is Glass Antenna, seed 303, BPM 124, rootMidi 55, scale minor, intensity 3, unlockIndex 2. Song 4 is Red Circuit, seed 404, BPM 132, rootMidi 53, scale minor, intensity 4, unlockIndex 3. Song 5 is Night Voltage, seed 505, BPM 140, rootMidi 57, scale minor, intensity 5, unlockIndex 4. Scale minor uses intervals 0, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, and 10. Song 1 is unlocked. A song with unlockIndex N greater than 0 is unlocked when the song with unlockIndex N - 1 has a stored rank of B or higher on any difficulty. Difficulty is Easy, Normal, or Hard. Each difficulty record has noteCount 96, 168, or 240; holdProbability 0, 0.15, or 0.25; scoreMultiplier 0.80, 1.00, or 1.25; and chartOffset 0, 1000, or 2000. In song select, W or Up and S or Down move the selected song, A or Left and D or Right move the selected difficulty, Enter starts the selected unlocked song, and Escape returns to title. Locked songs show a lock icon and cannot be started. The screen shows each song's BPM, best rank per difficulty, and unlock state. The player can select any unlocked song and any difficulty.
GENERATED MUSIC: A seeded music generator uses each song's seed, BPM, rootMidi, scale, and intensity to place a 120000 ms song. The generator uses a 16-step bar, with 16 sixteenth notes per bar. For chord progression, every 4 bars it chooses a chord degree from 0, 5, 2, or 6 in the minor scale; it never chooses the same degree twice in a row, and it changes degree at least once every 2 bars. The chord root is the song rootMidi plus the scale interval for that degree. The generator places a kick on steps 0 and 8 of every bar, an 80 ms sine from 55 Hz to 30 Hz at gain 0.9. It places a snare on steps 4 and 12, a 100 ms noise burst through a 1800 Hz bandpass at gain 0.5. Closed hi-hats are placed every 4 steps for intensity 2, every 2 steps for intensity 3 and 4, and every step for intensity 5; each closed hi-hat is 20 ms, highpass 7000 Hz, gain 0.18. Open hi-hats are placed on step 14 for intensity 4 and 5, 60 ms, highpass 6000 Hz, gain 0.12. Bass notes are placed on steps 0, 6, 8, and 14 for intensity 2 and 3, and on steps 0, 3, 6, 8, 11, and 14 for intensity 4 and 5; each bass note uses the current chord root, is 120 ms, square, lowpass 500 Hz, gain 0.35. The lead motif is generated once per song with 4 notes for intensity 2, 6 notes for intensity 3, 8 notes for intensity 4, and 10 notes for intensity 5; each lead note is a scale degree 0 through 6 plus an optional 12 semitones, 80 ms, square, gain 0.22, with a 12 ms delay. The motif uses distinct steps, repeats every 4 bars, and every 8 bars the generator shifts the motif up 12 semitones. At step 0 of each bar, a pad plays three chord tones, root, third, and fifth, as triangle notes for 1500 ms at gain 0.12. Master music gain is 0.8. The music verifier checks that no two lead notes start closer than 80 ms, no bar contains more than 12 lead notes, and every bar contains at least one bass note; if any fails, re-roll with the next seed.
NOTE CHART GENERATOR: A seeded chart generator places notes for a selected song and difficulty. It uses chartSeed equal to the song seed plus the difficulty chartOffset, where chartOffset is 0 for Easy, 1000 for Normal, and 2000 for Hard. It creates a 16th-note grid from 2000 ms to 115000 ms using the song BPM. It selects exactly the difficulty noteCount starts: 96 for Easy, 168 for Normal, and 240 for Hard. Step selection is weighted: step 0 of a bar has weight 4, step 4 has weight 2, and all other steps have weight 1. Each start receives a lane 0 through 3 from the seeded random. With the difficulty holdProbability, a start becomes a hold note; its endMs is startMs plus 1000 ms, 2000 ms, or 4000 ms chosen by seed, and a hold is dropped if endMs would exceed 115000 ms. The chart verifier checks that no two starts in the same lane are closer than 120 ms, no more than 2 starts occur in any 50 ms window, no hold body overlaps another note in the same lane, the total start count equals noteCount, no gap between consecutive starts exceeds 1500 ms, the first start is at least 2000 ms, and the last start is at most 115000 ms. If any check fails, re-roll with the next chartSeed, meaning chartSeed plus 1. The generated chart is the only source of note positions, and no note is hand-placed.
RESULTS AND PROGRESSION: Each result record has perfect, great, good, miss, maxCombo, score, rank, song, and difficulty. Raw score is the sum of judgment values times the combo multiplier, then multiplied by the difficulty scoreMultiplier. Score is stored as a whole number after rounding down. Accuracy is (perfect * 1 + great * 0.70 + good * 0.40) divided by total judged events. Rank is S if accuracy is 0.95 or higher, A if it is 0.85 or higher, B if it is 0.70 or higher, C if it is 0.50 or higher, and D below 0.50. A song is cleared if the player reaches 120000 ms with health above 0. A fail occurs if health reaches 0 before 120000 ms. Clearing a song with rank B or higher unlocks the next song in the roster. The best rank and best score for each song and difficulty are stored. The best overall rank is the highest rank stored across all songs and difficulties. The results screen shows the judgment counts, max combo, score, accuracy, rank, and an unlock message if one happened. The player can retry the same song with R, go to the next unlocked song with Enter, or return to song select with Escape. The progression goal is to unlock all five songs and then earn S rank on every Hard song.
SCREENS: TITLE shows the game title, best overall rank, and a start prompt; Enter, Space, or a tap on start leads to SONG SELECT, and Escape does nothing. SONG SELECT shows the five songs, difficulty, best ranks, and lock state; W or Up and S or Down move song selection, A or Left and D or Right move difficulty, Enter starts an unlocked song, Escape returns to TITLE, and touch buttons mirror these actions. COUNTDOWN is a 2000 ms overlay inside gameplay and automatically becomes GAMEPLAY. GAMEPLAY shows the lanes, notes, score, combo, health, and song timer; lane keys or pads play notes, Escape or P opens PAUSE, and M toggles mute. PAUSE shows resume, retry, and quit options; Enter resumes, R restarts the song, Escape returns to SONG SELECT, and M toggles mute. RESULTS appears after a cleared song and offers next, retry, and quit; Enter goes to the next unlocked song if one is available, R retries, and Escape returns to SONG SELECT. GAME OVER appears after a fail and offers retry and quit; Enter or R retries, and Escape returns to SONG SELECT. The state machine is TITLE → SONG SELECT → COUNTDOWN → GAMEPLAY → PAUSE → RESULTS or GAME OVER, with SONG SELECT reachable from TITLE, PAUSE, RESULTS, and GAME OVER.
AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. The generated song music uses the events named in GENERATED MUSIC. The countdown plays four ticks at 0 ms, 500 ms, 1000 ms, and 1500 ms, each an 880 Hz sine for 80 ms, and a go tone at 2000 ms, a 1320 Hz sine for 120 ms. A Perfect judgment plays a 1200 Hz sine for 60 ms, Great plays a 900 Hz sine for 60 ms, Good plays a 600 Hz sine for 60 ms, and Miss plays a 120 Hz square for 120 ms. A hold tail Perfect plays a 1500 Hz sine for 60 ms, hold tail Great plays an 1100 Hz sine for 60 ms, hold tail Good plays an 800 Hz sine for 60 ms, and hold tail Miss plays a 100 Hz square for 120 ms. Every 50 combo milestone plays a 1760 Hz sine for 80 ms. Unlocking a song plays a four-note fanfare at 660 Hz, 880 Hz, 1100 Hz, and 1320 Hz, each 100 ms. A fail plays a 200 Hz sawtooth for 400 ms and then stops the song music with a 500 ms fade. UI select moves play a 500 Hz sine for 40 ms, UI confirm plays a 700 Hz sine for 60 ms, and UI back plays a 400 Hz sine for 60 ms. Master gain is 0.8, and mute sets master gain to 0.
ART: Ask for a neon arcade title background as a scene, ask for a starfield song-select background as a scene, ask for an empty neon highway gameplay background as a scene, ask for a scoreboard terminal results background as a scene, and ask for a red alarm fail background as a scene. Everything else — lane guides, tap notes, hold bodies, hold tails, hit line, key labels, touch pads, score text, combo text, health bar, judgment text, particles, lock icons, difficulty tags, pause overlay, and unlock banner — is drawn in code.
