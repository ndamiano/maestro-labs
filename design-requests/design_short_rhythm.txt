Build this four-lane rhythm game with generated original music. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.
SYSTEMS: SONG GENERATOR, CHART GENERATOR, NOTE RECORD, INPUT SYSTEM, HIT WINDOW, COMBO SYSTEM, SCORE SYSTEM, HEALTH SYSTEM, RIVAL SYSTEM, DIFFICULTY PROGRESSION, RANK RECORD, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART.
SONG GENERATOR: The song generator is the source of truth for all music timing and uses a seeded generator to place five original songs in order. It takes a campaign seed and creates a Song record with fields level, seed, bpm, bars, steps, durationMs, density, rootMidi, scale, melody, bass, kick, snare, and hat. It places Song 1 with bpm 100, bars 16, steps 256, durationMs 38400, density 0.20, rootMidi 69, and scale A minor pentatonic; Song 2 with bpm 108, bars 16, steps 256, durationMs 35556, density 0.25, rootMidi 64, and scale E minor pentatonic; Song 3 with bpm 116, bars 20, steps 320, durationMs 41379, density 0.30, rootMidi 60, and scale C minor pentatonic; Song 4 with bpm 124, bars 20, steps 320, durationMs 38710, density 0.35, rootMidi 67, and scale G minor pentatonic; Song 5 with bpm 132, bars 24, steps 384, durationMs 43636, density 0.40, rootMidi 62, and scale D minor pentatonic. The minor pentatonic scale is the semitone offsets 0, 3, 5, 7, and 10 above rootMidi. Each event in melody, bass, kick, snare, and hat is an event record with fields step, midi, durationMs, and velocity. The generator places kick on steps 0 and 8 of every bar, snare on steps 4 and 12 of every bar, hat on every step, bass on steps 0 and 8 using rootMidi minus 12, and melody on steps chosen by density from the song scale, with melody midi equal to rootMidi plus one of the scale offsets plus either 0 or 12. Kick events have durationMs 80, snare events have durationMs 60, hat events have durationMs 30, bass events have durationMs 160, melody events have durationMs 120, and every event has velocity 1. The verifier requires five songs, the listed steps for each song, every melody event to use an allowed scale midi, no two melody events in the same step, at least 24 melody events per song, and no song longer than 120000 ms; re-roll with the next seed if any fails.
CHART GENERATOR: The chart generator reads the current Song record and uses a seeded generator to place the gameplay notes for that song. It takes the song seed and level and creates a Chart record with fields songId, level, notes, and totalNotes. It places Note records in ascending timeMs order, with lane 1 to 4 left to right, and uses the song bpm and bars to set note times on 16th-step boundaries. For level 1 it places 48 notes with no holds; level 2 places 64 notes with 4 holds; level 3 places 80 notes with 8 holds; level 4 places 96 notes with 12 holds; level 5 places 120 notes with 16 holds. A hold has durationMs 400, 600, 800, 1000, or 1200. The verifier requires the exact note count for the level, no two notes in the same lane closer than 160 ms, no two notes in the same lane overlapping, no more than 2 simultaneous notes on levels 1 and 2, no more than 3 simultaneous notes on levels 3 to 5, every hold to end before the next note in its lane, and at least one note in each lane; re-roll with the next seed if any fails.
NOTE RECORD: The Note record is the source of truth for each gameplay note, and the input, hit, combo, score, health, and rank systems read through it. Each Note record has fields id, songId, lane, timeMs, durationMs, type, hit, judgment, inputTimeMs, and releaseTimeMs. type is tap or hold. hit is false at start and becomes true when a tap is judged or when a hold head is judged. judgment is none, perfect, great, good, miss, or ghost. inputTimeMs is the timestamp of the lane press that judged the note head, or 0 when absent. releaseTimeMs is the timestamp of the lane release that judged the hold tail, or 0 when absent. A hold note has a tail at timeMs plus durationMs, but the tail is not a separate Note record and is judged only by the same Note record.
INPUT SYSTEM: The input system binds four lanes to keys and touch. Lane 1 is W, Left Arrow, and the leftmost touch zone; lane 2 is A, Down Arrow, and the second touch zone; lane 3 is S, Right Arrow, and the third touch zone; lane 4 is D, Up Arrow, and the rightmost touch zone. Each press or release is an InputEvent record with fields lane, timestamp, and kind, where kind is press or release. It passes presses to the hit window system and releases to the Note record for hold tails. It ignores a press if the same lane is already pressed, and it allows one press per lane per frame.
HIT WINDOW: The hit window system compares an input timestamp to the Note record timeMs. A tap note is judged perfect if the input is no more than 40 ms from timeMs, great if no more than 80 ms, good if no more than 120 ms, and miss if it is more than 120 ms but no more than 160 ms, or if no input occurs by timeMs plus 120 ms. A hold note head is judged by the same tap windows, and its tail is judged perfect if release is no more than 40 ms from timeMs plus durationMs, great if no more than 80 ms, good if no more than 120 ms, and miss if release is more than 120 ms early or late, or if no release occurs by timeMs plus durationMs plus 120 ms. If the hold head is judged miss, the tail is ignored and the note remains miss. A lane press with no unmissed note within 160 ms is a ghost press. The system sets the Note record judgment and hit fields, and it never changes the Note record timeMs or lane.
COMBO SYSTEM: The combo system reads Note record judgments and maintains a Combo record with fields combo, maxCombo, and active. It increases combo by 1 on perfect, great, or good, resets combo to 0 on miss or ghost, and sets maxCombo to the larger of maxCombo and combo. A hold note that becomes miss after a successful head resets combo to 0 and sets maxCombo if the current combo before reset is larger. The combo value is read by the score and health systems, but the combo system is the only system that changes it.
SCORE SYSTEM: The score system reads Note record judgments and combo milestones and maintains a Score record with fields score, perfect, great, good, miss, ghost, and maxCombo. It adds 300 for perfect, 200 for great, 100 for good, and 0 for miss or ghost. When combo reaches 25, it adds 500; when combo reaches 50, it adds 1000; when combo reaches 100, it adds 2000; when combo reaches 200, it adds 5000; and when combo reaches 300, it adds 10000. If a hold note changes from a successful head judgment to miss, the score system removes the head points already added for that note. It counts judgment totals and copies maxCombo from the combo record at song end.
HEALTH SYSTEM: The health system reads Note record judgments and maintains a Health record with fields health, maxHealth, and alive. It starts health at 50, sets maxHealth to 100, and sets alive to true. It adds 2 on perfect, adds 1 on great, adds 0 on good, subtracts 10 on miss, and subtracts 2 on ghost. It clamps health to 0 and maxHealth. When health reaches 0, alive becomes false and the song is failed. A hold note that changes to miss after a successful head subtracts an additional 10, but only once per note. At song end, success requires alive true.
RIVAL SYSTEM: The rival system is the source of truth for opponent targets and uses a seeded generator to place five Rival records in order. It takes the campaign seed and creates a Rival record with fields songId, name, targetScore, and skill. It places Rival 1 with songId 1, name Echo, targetScore 12000, and skill 0.6; Rival 2 with songId 2, name Pulse, targetScore 18000, and skill 0.7; Rival 3 with songId 3, name Volt, targetScore 26000, and skill 0.8; Rival 4 with songId 4, name Nova, targetScore 30000, and skill 0.9; Rival 5 with songId 5, name Axiom, targetScore 42000, and skill 1.0. The rank, difficulty, and screens systems read through it. The verifier requires five rivals, targetScore ascending, unique names, skill values exactly 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, and 1.0, and targetScore at least 8000; re-roll with the next seed if any fails.
DIFFICULTY PROGRESSION: The difficulty progression system reads the Rank record and the current Song record and maintains a Campaign record with fields currentLevel, unlockedLevel, wins, and failed. It starts currentLevel at 1, unlockedLevel at 1, wins at 0, and failed at 0. After a song ends, if the Rank record clear is true, it sets unlockedLevel to the higher of unlockedLevel and currentLevel plus 1, and if currentLevel is 5 it sets wins to 1 and the campaign is won. If clear is false, it adds 1 to failed and leaves currentLevel unchanged for retry. The player may retry any unlocked level, and the next level is chosen from the song generator by level number.
RANK RECORD: The rank record is the source of truth for song results and is read by difficulty progression and screens. Each Rank record has fields songId, accuracy, rank, clear, score, maxCombo, perfect, great, good, miss, ghost, and beatRival. Accuracy is a whole-number percentage from 0 to 100, and it is stored after applying the weights perfect 100, great 80, good 50, miss 0, and ghost 0 over totalNotes. Rank is S if accuracy is 95 or higher, A if accuracy is 90 or higher but below 95, B if accuracy is 80 or higher but below 90, C if accuracy is 70 or higher but below 80, and D if accuracy is below 70. beatRival is true if score is at least the Rival record targetScore for that song. clear is true if health is alive at song end, accuracy is 70 or higher, and beatRival is true, and false otherwise. The rank record copies score, maxCombo, and judgment totals from the score and health systems at song end.
SCREENS: The screen state is a Screen record with fields name, selectedLevel, and paused, and the game is a state machine with title, song select, countdown, play, results, fail, and win screens. Title shows the game name and a start button; pressing Enter, Space, or tapping start goes to song select, and Escape does nothing. Song select shows the five generated songs, the unlocked level, and a play button; pressing 1 to 5 selects an unlocked song, Enter starts the countdown for the selected song, Escape returns to title, tapping a song selects it, and tapping the play button starts the countdown. Countdown shows a 3, 2, 1, go sequence at 1000 ms per count; Escape cancels to song select. Play shows four lanes, notes, the hit line, score, combo, health, and song time; Escape pauses and opens a pause overlay with resume, retry, and quit buttons, where Enter resumes, R retries, Escape quits to song select, and touch buttons do the same. Results shows rank, accuracy, score, maxCombo, judgment totals, the rival target, and buttons for next, retry, and quit; if clear is true and currentLevel is below 5, Enter goes to next, if clear is true and currentLevel is 5, Enter goes to win, if clear is false, Enter retries, R always retries, Escape quits to song select, and touch buttons do the same. Fail shows the reason, health, and buttons for retry and quit; R retries and Escape quits to song select. Win shows the campaign win, final song score, and buttons for replay and quit; Enter goes to title and Escape goes to song select.
AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. Each sound is a Sound record with fields name, waveform, frequency, durationMs, and gain, and noise sounds use frequency 0. The music sounds are kick as sine 60 Hz 80 ms gain 0.5 on kick events, snare as noise 60 ms gain 0.4 on snare events, hat as noise 30 ms gain 0.2 on hat events, bass as square at the event midi 160 ms gain 0.35 on bass events, and lead as square at the event midi 120 ms gain 0.3 on melody events. UI sounds are: key click as square 1000 Hz 20 ms gain 0.2 on every lane press, perfect ding as sine 1200 Hz 120 ms gain 0.3 on perfect, great chime as sine 900 Hz 100 ms gain 0.25 on great, good blip as triangle 700 Hz 80 ms gain 0.2 on good, miss buzz as sawtooth 120 Hz 200 ms gain 0.3 on miss, ghost thud as sine 80 Hz 80 ms gain 0.25 on ghost, combo fanfare as a three-note square sequence at 1200, 1500, and 1800 Hz with 100 ms each and gain 0.3 when combo reaches 25, 50, 100, 200, or 300, countdown beep as sine 600 Hz 100 ms gain 0.3 for 3, 2, and 1 and sine 800 Hz 200 ms gain 0.3 for go, results jingle as a three-note sine sequence at 1200, 1500, and 1800 Hz with 150 ms each and gain 0.3 on S, A, or B rank, results sting as a two-note sawtooth sequence at 300 and 250 Hz with 200 ms each and gain 0.25 on C or D rank, fail drone as sawtooth 100 Hz 800 ms gain 0.3 on fail, and win fanfare as a four-note square sequence at 1200, 1500, 1800, and 2400 Hz with 150 ms each and gain 0.3 on win. The audio system reads the Song record for music and the Note record and Rank record for judgment sounds, and it never stores note times.
ART: Each AI art asset is an Asset record with fields name, image, and scale, and scale is 1 for every asset. The AI art is: ask for a red tap note as a sprite, a yellow tap note as a sprite, a blue tap note as a sprite, a green tap note as a sprite, a red hold cap as a sprite, a yellow hold cap as a sprite, a blue hold cap as a sprite, a green hold cap as a sprite, a player avatar as a sprite, a miss burst as a sprite, a perfect ring as a sprite, and a neon arcade background as a scene. Everything else — lane lines, hold note bodies, hit line, note trails, key prompts, score text, combo text, health bar, judgment text, screen flashes, particles, and rank letters — is drawn in code.
