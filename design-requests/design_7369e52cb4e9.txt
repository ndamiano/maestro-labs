Build this F1 racing game where you drive against 7 AI rivals across a 5-race season on procedurally generated circuits. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

SYSTEMS: CAR, TRACK, RACER, AI_DRIVER, PHYSICS, LAP_SYSTEM, POSITION, HUD, MINIMAP, SEASON, INPUT, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART.

CAR: The car is a record with fields x, y, angle, speed, maxSpeed, acceleration, braking, turnRate, width, length, offTrackPenalty, driftFactor. Every car on screen reads from its RACER record to know which CAR it drives. The player's car has maxSpeed 320, acceleration 4.2, braking 6.0, turnRate 0.045, width 22, length 48, offTrackPenalty 0.85, driftFactor 0.3. AI cars have maxSpeed 310, acceleration 4.0, braking 5.8, turnRate 0.044, width 22, length 48, offTrackPenalty 0.85, driftFactor 0.3. The CAR record is the single source of truth for geometry and physics parameters; PHYSICS, HUD, and MINIMAP all read through it and never store a second copy of speed or angle.

TRACK: The track is a record with fields points (an array of {x, y} vertices forming a closed loop), width, sectorCount, startLine, length. A seeded generator builds the track from a seed integer: it picks 14 to 22 control points placed on a ring of radius 900 to 1400 around the origin with angular jitter of 0.15 radians, then smooths them with a Catmull-Rom spline sampled at 400 points, assigns width 56, and divides the loop into 3 equal sectors by index. The start line sits at point index 0. The generator also computes total track length as the sum of segment distances. VERIFIER: the closed loop must have no self-intersection (no two non-adjacent segments cross), minimum track length of 6000, minimum corner radius of 40, and all 400 points must be unique. Re-roll with the next seed if any fails. TRACK is the single source of truth for geometry; PHYSICS, MINIMAP, LAP_SYSTEM, and HUD all read through it.

RACER: A racer is a record with fields id, name, isPlayer, car (a CAR record), x, y, angle, speed, lap, totalLaps, finished, finishTime, sectorTimes (array of 3), currentSector, racePosition, championshipPoints. There are 8 racers per race: the player (id 0) and 7 AI. The 5 season races use seeds 1001, 1002, 1003, 1004, 1005 for their tracks. Every racer has totalLaps 3. The RACER record is the single source of truth for each racer's live state; LAP_SYSTEM, POSITION, HUD, MINIMAP, and SEASON all read through it.

AI_DRIVER: Each AI racer has a skill value and a rubber-band factor. The 7 AI rivals have skills 0.92, 0.90, 0.88, 0.86, 0.84, 0.82, 0.80. Rubber-band factor is 0.02: if an AI is more than 2 seconds behind the leader its effective maxSpeed increases by 6; if more than 2 seconds ahead its effective maxSpeed decreases by 6. AI steering targets the track centerline point 80 units ahead of its current position, corrected by skill. AI braking begins at a distance of 200 times (1 minus skill) from the next corner apex. AI_DRIVER reads the RACER and TRACK records and writes back x, y, angle, speed into the RACER.

PHYSICS: Each frame, for every racer, speed changes by acceleration times input minus braking times input, clamped to 0 and maxSpeed. Angle changes by turnRate times steerInput times (speed / maxSpeed). Position x and y advance by speed times cos(angle) and speed times sin(angle) respectively. Off-track detection: if the racer's center is more than (track width / 2 + car width / 2) from the nearest track point, multiply speed by offTrackPenalty each frame. Car-to-car collision: if two racer circles of radius 14 overlap, push both apart along the collision normal by 2 units per frame and reduce both speeds by 10. PHYSICS reads CAR and TRACK and writes x, y, angle, speed back into each RACER.

LAP_SYSTEM: A lap counts when a racer crosses the start-line segment (from track point 0 to point 1) in the forward direction and has previously crossed both sector-1 and sector-2 checkpoints (at indices 133 and 266). On a valid lap crossing, increment lap, push the sector times, and if lap exceeds totalLaps set finished to true and record finishTime as elapsed race seconds. LAP_SYSTEM reads RACER and TRACK and writes lap, sectorTimes, finished, finishTime back into RACER.

POSITION: Race position is recomputed every 500 ms. For each non-finished racer, compute a progress value as (lap × track length) + distance along track from start line to current nearest point. Sort all 8 racers by finished-first (earlier finishTime first), then by progress descending. Assign racePosition 1 through 8. POSITION reads RACER and TRACK and writes racePosition back into RACER.

HUD: The HUD displays, top-left: current position (e.g. "P4/8"), lap "LAP 2/3". Top-right: sector times for the current lap and the best lap. Bottom-center: a speed readout in km/h (speed × 3.6 rounded) and a gear indicator (1 through 6, gear = floor(speed / maxSpeed × 6) + 1). Bottom-left: a 3-bar off-track warning that fills as the car drifts toward the grass edge. The HUD reads RACER, TRACK, and CAR.

MINIMAP: A 160 × 160 pixel panel in the top-right corner renders the track as a thin grey path (scaled to fit) with 8 colored dots (player white, AI red) positioned by projecting each RACER's x, y into minimap coordinates. The start line is marked with a small white rectangle. MINIMAP reads RACER and TRACK.

SEASON: The season is a record with fields raceIndex (0 to 4), totalRaces (5), championship (array of 8 entries each with racerId and points). After each race, award points to the top 8 finishers: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4. The season ends after race 5; the winner is the racer with the highest total championship points. SEASON reads RACER (finish order) and writes championship points.

INPUT: Keyboard: W and Up Arrow accelerate, S and Down Arrow brake, A and Left Arrow steer left, D and Right Arrow steer right. Touch: a virtual steering wheel (left half of screen) for turning and a brake/accelerator pedal cluster (right half) for throttle and braking. Escape opens a pause overlay. INPUT writes steerInput, throttleInput, and brakeInput values that PHYSICS reads each frame.

SCREENS: title (shows "F1 CIRCUIT CHAMPIONSHIP", a "START SEASON" button, and a "SKIP TO RACE 3" button) → race (the live driving view with HUD and minimap) → results (shows a 8-row finish table with times and points earned, a "NEXT RACE" button or "CHAMPIONSHIP COMPLETE" text after race 5) → championship (shows the final 8-racer points table, a "NEW SEASON" button that resets to title). Escape during race pauses the game and shows a "RESUME" and "QUIT TO TITLE" button.

AUDIO: Generated with the Web Audio API. Engine loop: a sawtooth oscillator whose frequency maps linearly from 80 Hz at speed 0 to 440 Hz at maxSpeed, running continuously during the race. Skid: a filtered noise burst (bandpass 2000 Hz, 0.3 s) triggered when driftFactor exceeds 0.5. Collision: a short square-wave thud at 60 Hz for 0.15 s. Lap complete: a two-tone chime (880 Hz then 1320 Hz, 0.2 s each). Countdown: three beeps at 440 Hz (1 s apart) then one at 880 Hz (0.5 s). Finish: a rising arpeggio of 440, 550, 660, 880 Hz over 1 s.

ART: Ask for 1 AI-generated sprite sheet containing 8 top-down F1 car sprites (one per racer, distinct livery colors: red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, teal, white) at 48 × 22 pixels each. Ask for 1 AI-generated title-screen background scene of a grandstand and track under sunset. Everything else — track surface, kerbs, grass, HUD panels, minimap, speedometer, sector markers, collision sparks, off-track dust particles, the countdown numbers, the results table — is drawn in code.
