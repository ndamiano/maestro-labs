Build this monkey swarm survival game where you guide a banana-hungry monkey through jungle waves, dodge rival monkeys, collect bananas, and survive ten escalating waves. It is made of these systems. Every number below is a given value to write into the code as-is; none of them needs checking or deriving.

SYSTEMS: PLAYER, MONKEY, RIVAL, BANANA, JUNGLE, WAVE, SCORE, SHOP, UPGRADE, SCREENS, AUDIO, ART

PLAYER: The player is one record named player with fields id, x, y, vx, vy, facing, health, maxHealth, speed, bananaCount, dashSpeed, dashDuration, dashCooldown, dashTimer, and invulnerableTimer. It is the source of truth for the leader monkey's position, movement, health, bananas, and dash state, and MONKEY, RIVAL, BANANA, SCORE, SHOP, UPGRADE, SCREENS, AUDIO, and ART read it. The player starts at the center of JUNGLE with facing right, health 100, maxHealth 100, speed 160, bananaCount 0, dashSpeed 420, dashDuration 0.18, dashCooldown 1.2, dashTimer 0, and invulnerableTimer 0. The player moves with W, A, S, D and ArrowUp, ArrowDown, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight; Space starts a dash in the current facing direction if dashTimer is 0 and dashCooldown is 0; touch uses a virtual joystick on the left half of the screen for movement and a tap on the right half for dash. A dash moves the player at dashSpeed for dashDuration in the facing direction and sets dashCooldown to 1.2. The player takes damage from a RIVAL attack equal to that RIVAL's damage field unless invulnerableTimer is greater than 0; after any hit, invulnerableTimer is set to 1.0. If health is 0, SCREENS goes to game over.

MONKEY: A follower monkey is a record with fields id, x, y, vx, vy, health, maxHealth, attackPower, attackInterval, attackTimer, targetId, followIndex, state, and deathTimer. The followers list is the source of truth for all allied monkeys, and PLAYER, RIVAL, BANANA, SCORE, WAVE, SHOP, UPGRADE, SCREENS, AUDIO, and ART read it. Follower monkeys are created only through UPGRADE when the player spends bananas; each new monkey has health 30, maxHealth 30, attackPower 8, attackInterval 1.0, followIndex equal to the current follower count, and state follow. The maximum follower count is 12. Follower monkeys follow the player in a chain: each monkey's target position is the player position for followIndex 0, or the previous monkey's position for other indices; each monkey moves toward that position at speed 140 while state is follow. A follower monkey enters state attack when a RIVAL is within 96 pixels and returns to state follow when no RIVAL is within 96 pixels. When state is attack, a follower monkey moves to within 32 pixels of its target RIVAL and then attacks every attackInterval seconds for attackPower damage; attackPower is updated by UPGRADE. A follower monkey with health 0 has state dead and deathTimer 0.6; after deathTimer expires it is removed. Follower monkeys collect bananas for the player and are damaged by RIVAL attacks.

RIVAL: A rival monkey is a record with fields id, type, x, y, vx, vy, health, maxHealth, speed, damage, attackInterval, attackTimer, targetId, state, deathTimer, and scoreValue. The rivals list is the source of truth for all enemy monkeys, and PLAYER, MONKEY, BANANA, WAVE, SCORE, SHOP, UPGRADE, SCREENS, AUDIO, and ART read it. Rival monkeys are created only by WAVE events. The roster is: Chimp with health 20, maxHealth 20, speed 90, damage 10, attackInterval 1.0, and scoreValue 5; Marmoset with health 10, maxHealth 10, speed 140, damage 5, attackInterval 0.67, and scoreValue 3; Macaque with health 60, maxHealth 60, speed 70, damage 15, attackInterval 1.25, and scoreValue 12; Baboon with health 120, maxHealth 120, speed 60, damage 25, attackInterval 1.67, and scoreValue 30. A rival with state chase moves toward the nearest target, which is the player if no follower monkey is within 80 pixels, otherwise the nearest follower monkey, at its speed, and enters state attack when within 28 pixels of its target. A rival with state attack stops when within 28 pixels of its target, attacks every attackInterval seconds for damage, and returns to state chase when its target is more than 40 pixels away or dead. A rival with health 0 has state dead and deathTimer 0.5; after deathTimer it is removed and SCORE gains scoreValue. A rival attack that hits the player reduces player health by damage and sets player invulnerableTimer to 1.0; a rival attack that hits a follower monkey reduces that monkey's health by damage.

BANANA: A banana is a record with fields id, x, y, value, bobTimer, and collected. The bananas list is the source of truth for all pickups, and PLAYER, MONKEY, RIVAL, SCORE, SHOP, WAVE, SCREENS, AUDIO, and ART read it. Bananas are created by JUNGLE bush spawns and by defeated RIVAL drops. A normal banana's value is read from UPGRADE at spawn time; a golden banana's value is read from UPGRADE at spawn time. One banana spawns from a random JUNGLE bush every 4.0 seconds, up to 25 bananas on screen. A normal banana has bobTimer amplitude 0. A golden banana has bobTimer amplitude 3 pixels. A banana is collected when the player's circle of radius 16 overlaps the banana's circle of radius 8, or when any follower monkey's circle of radius 16 overlaps the banana's circle of radius 8; collection adds value to the player's bananaCount, adds value to SCORE, and sets collected true. A banana with collected true is removed after 0.2 seconds. A defeated RIVAL drops one normal banana at its death position with probability 0.3.

JUNGLE: The jungle is one record named jungle with fields seed, width, height, obstacles, bushes, and spawnEdges. Each obstacle is a record with fields id, x, y, w, h, and type; each bush is a record with fields id, x, y, and radius; each spawn edge is a record with fields id, edge, openingX, and openingY. The obstacles array is the source of truth for all static geometry, and PLAYER, MONKEY, RIVAL, BANANA, WAVE, SCREENS, and ART read it. A seeded generator places the jungle using seed 7, width 960, and height 600. It first places 12 rectangular tree obstacles of size 48 by 48 or 64 by 64 at random non-overlapping positions, then places 8 bush spawn points of radius 20 at random positions, then places 4 vine obstacles of size 8 by 64 or 64 by 8, and finally chooses 4 spawn edges from the rectangle border. The generator rolls positions with a seeded random number generator and rejects any placement that overlaps another obstacle or any bush by less than 32 pixels. The VERIFIER requires that at least 240 pixels of clear path exist from the player start to every bush, that no obstacle overlaps another, that no bush lies inside an obstacle, that there are exactly 12 tree obstacles, 8 bushes, and 4 vine obstacles, and that each spawn edge has a 40-pixel-wide clear opening; re-roll with the next seed if any fails.

WAVE: A wave is one record named wave with fields waveNumber, seed, events, time, active, completed, and shopOpen. Each event is a record with fields time, kind, type, count, edge, spread, bushId, and golden. The events array is the source of truth for all RIVAL and BANANA spawns for the current wave, and RIVAL, BANANA, SCORE, SHOP, SCREENS, AUDIO, and ART read it. A seeded generator builds the wave using seed 101 for wave 1, 102 for wave 2, 103 for wave 3, 104 for wave 4, 105 for wave 5, 106 for wave 6, 107 for wave 7, 108 for wave 8, 109 for wave 9, and 110 for wave 10. It places events in increasing time order. The wave parameters are: wave 1 has 7 rival events and 4 normal banana events of count 1, and 0 golden banana events; wave 2 has 9 rival events and 5 normal banana events of count 1, and 0 golden banana events; wave 3 has 11 rival events and 6 normal banana events of count 1, and 1 golden banana event of count 1; wave 4 has 13 rival events and 7 normal banana events of count 1, and 0 golden banana events; wave 5 has 15 rival events and 8 normal banana events of count 1, and 0 golden banana events; wave 6 has 17 rival events and 9 normal banana events of count 1, and 1 golden banana event of count 1; wave 7 has 19 rival events and 10 normal banana events of count 1, and 0 golden banana events; wave 8 has 21 rival events and 11 normal banana events of count 1, and 0 golden banana events; wave 9 has 23 rival events and 12 normal banana events of count 1, and 0 golden banana events; wave 10 has 25 rival events and 13 normal banana events of count 1, and 1 golden banana event of count 1. Rival types are chosen by wave: waves 1 and 2 use only Chimp, waves 3 and 4 use Chimp and Marmoset, waves 5 through 7 add Macaque, and waves 8 through 10 add Baboon. A wave is completed when all events have occurred and the rivals list is empty; after completion, waves 1 through 9 open SHOP and wave 10 sends SCREENS to victory. The VERIFIER requires that no two events occur at the same time, that total rival count equals the wave table, that no event time is before 0 or after 45, that each edge used has at least one spawn opening, and that every banana event references an existing bush; re-roll with the next seed if any fails.

SCORE: The score is one record named score with fields points, best, wave, and totalKills. It is the source of truth for score, best score, current wave number, and kill count, and PLAYER, MONKEY, RIVAL, BANANA, SHOP, UPGRADE, SCREENS, AUDIO, and ART read it. Score points increase by the value of a collected banana and by the scoreValue of a killed rival. totalKills increases by 1 for each RIVAL removed. wave is set by WAVE when a wave begins. best is the highest points reached in the session. The score record is not copied into other systems.

SHOP: The shop is one record named shop with fields open, timer, selected, and items. Each item is a record with fields id, name, price, effect, and timesBought. It is the source of truth for between-wave purchases, and PLAYER, SCORE, UPGRADE, SCREENS, AUDIO, and ART read it. The shop opens for 6.0 seconds after every completed wave except after wave 10, and closes immediately when timer reaches 0 or when the player presses B. The items are: Swift Vines with price 8 and effect speedLevel increase; Tough Hide with price 10 and effect healthLevel increase plus 20 player health; Sharp Fingers with price 12 and effect attackLevel increase; and Banana Charm with price 15 and effect charmLevel increase. Purchases require enough bananas in player.bananaCount, subtract the price from player.bananaCount, apply the effect once, and mark the item purchased; each item can be bought up to 3 times. The shop does not change wave geometry.

UPGRADE: The upgrade system is one record named upgrades with fields speedLevel, healthLevel, attackLevel, charmLevel, summonCost, and maxMonkeys. It is the source of truth for all permanent stat changes, and PLAYER, MONKEY, BANANA, SHOP, SCORE, SCREENS, and ART read it. Starting values are speedLevel 0, healthLevel 0, attackLevel 0, charmLevel 0, summonCost 3, and maxMonkeys 12. When the player presses E, the game spends summonCost bananas from player.bananaCount to create one MONKEY if the follower count is below maxMonkeys; the summonCost is 3 for the first summon, 4 for the second, 5 for the third, 6 for the fourth, and 6 for every later summon. The speedLevel values are 0 speed 160, 1 speed 180, 2 speed 200, and 3 speed 220. The healthLevel values are 0 maxHealth 100, 1 maxHealth 120, 2 maxHealth 140, and 3 maxHealth 160. The attackLevel values are 0 attackPower 8, 1 attackPower 10, 2 attackPower 12, and 3 attackPower 14. The charmLevel values are 0 normal banana value 1 and golden banana value 5, 1 normal banana value 2 and golden banana value 6, 2 normal banana value 3 and golden banana value 7, and 3 normal banana value 4 and golden banana value 8. The upgrade record is the only place these stat levels are stored; other systems read the final numbers from the player, monkey, and banana records.

SCREENS: The screens are a state machine: title (shows the game name, a monkey, and the controls) → playing (shows the jungle, HUD, and wave number) → pause (shows Resume and Quit) → playing, playing → shop (shows the shop panel over the jungle) → playing, playing → game over (offers Retry and Title), and playing → victory (offers Play Again and Title). On title, Enter starts a new game and Escape does nothing. During playing, Escape opens pause, Resume returns to playing, and Quit goes to title. During pause, Escape resumes playing. During shop, B closes the shop and returns to playing, and Escape closes the shop and returns to playing. During game over, Enter or R restarts the game and T returns to title. During victory, Enter or R starts a new game and T returns to title. Touch buttons mirror the keyboard buttons on each screen. The HUD shows score.points, player.bananaCount, score.wave, and the follower count.

AUDIO: All audio is generated with the Web Audio API. The sounds are: title click, a short square-wave blip at 440 hertz for 0.08 seconds; collect, a sine-wave blip at 660 hertz rising to 880 hertz for 0.10 seconds; summon, a triangle-wave chord of 220, 330, and 440 hertz for 0.15 seconds; dash, a noise burst filtered at 1200 hertz for 0.08 seconds; hit, a sawtooth thud at 110 hertz for 0.12 seconds; kill, a descending square-wave tone from 520 to 260 hertz for 0.15 seconds; shop open, a two-note arpeggio of 523 and 784 hertz for 0.20 seconds; purchase, a sine-wave blip at 988 hertz for 0.08 seconds; wave start, a drum-like noise burst at 80 hertz for 0.25 seconds; game over, a descending minor arpeggio of 440, 415, and 349 hertz for 0.60 seconds; victory, an ascending major arpeggio of 523, 659, and 784 hertz for 0.60 seconds. Each sound plays at the moment its event occurs and no sound loops.

ART: Ask for the player monkey as a sprite, the follower monkey as a sprite, the Chimp rival as a sprite, the Marmoset rival as a sprite, the Macaque rival as a sprite, the Baboon rival as a sprite, the normal banana as a sprite, the golden banana as a sprite, the tree obstacle as a tile, the bush as a tile, the vine obstacle as a tile, the jungle background as a scene, the title screen background as a scene, and the shop panel as a scene. Everything else — movement, collision shapes, hit flashes, health bars, score text, wave timer, buttons, particles, and HUD — is drawn in code.
